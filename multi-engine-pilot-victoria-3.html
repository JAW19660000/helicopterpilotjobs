Multi-Engine Pilot-Victoria

Victoria · Posted 2026-03-03 · 👁 … views

About the role: We are inviting expressions of interest from qualified Multi-Engine Helicopter Pilots to join our Aviation & Critical Services team. The available opportunity is to perform the role as an AW139 Line Pilot based at Essendon, Victoria. Babcock MCS operates at several bases throughout Victoria in support of Ambulance Victoria. As this is a Fixed Base position, successful applicants will be required to reside within 60 minutes of the nominated base. It is also a requirement for the successful applicant to be able to support operational needs across all AV bases. About you: The successful candidate must satisfy the following contract minimum qualifications and experience: CASA ATPL; Class 1 Medical; Instrument Rating with MEH endorsement and minimum 3 renewals; 2500+ hrs total helicopter; 1500+ hrs PIC helicopter; 500+ hrs PIC MEH, not including ICUS; 250+ hrs instrument flying time; 100+ hrs night PIC; AW139 type rating and 100+ hrs experience on type; NVIS rating; Low-level rating with winch endorsement; Prior EMS/SAR operational experience preferred. Applicants are requested to show their compliance against the above criteria. Type rating or qualification training may be offered to the successful candidate. Your future at Babcock - what we offer you: An agile work environment where you're able to work onsite and remotely, with a laptop and working from home kit provided, plus a compressed week option; Work life integration policy bringing together leave entitlements including annual leave, wellbeing leave, paid parental leave, defence reservist leave plus more; A supportive and engaging environment to grow your career, supported by the capability framework plus ongoing access to self-led learning modules through LinkedIn Learning and access to study leave; Access to beRewarded for discounts across retail, health, entertainment, travel and much more, plus novated leasing options and corporate shares plan; Wellbeing support through the online wellbeing program Munch, Mind, Move, Money, access to the employee assistance program plus two days annually from personal leave as wellbeing days; Participation in the weRecognise program recognising outstanding work in line with company Principles. Who we are: Babcock International Group, the aerospace and defence company, has been trusted to deliver bespoke, highly skilled engineering services for over a century. Across Australia and New Zealand, Babcock has grown to a team of over 1,300 talented people. We operate across sectors such as defence, commercial marine, aviation, airports, communications, emergency services and engineering consultancy, including providing emergency medical and search & rescue aviation services through our Aviation & Critical Services business.
