Full-Time Helicopter Line Pilot-V2 Helicopters Archerfield Brisbane QLD

Archerfield Airport, Brisbane QLD · Posted 2026-11-23 · 👁 … views

V2 Helicopters has an opportunity for a motivated and professional full-time helicopter line pilot to join its growing team at Archerfield Airport, Brisbane QLD. V2 is looking for someone who is passionate about aviation, takes pride in their flying and is keen to be part of a dynamic and supportive team.

Minimum requirements
- CPL(H) – Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter)
- Minimum 300 hours total flight experience
- Low Level Rating
- Dangerous Goods (DG) qualification
- R22/R44 experience
- Current Class 1 Aviation Medical Certificate

Highly regarded
- Flight Instructor Rating
- Willingness and ability to deliver flight theory instruction
- A positive attitude, strong work ethic and commitment to safety and professionalism

What’s on offer
- Competitive salary package
- Full-time employment
- A diverse and engaging flying environment
- Opportunities for professional development and career progression
- The opportunity to join an established and growing helicopter operation with a great team culture

How to apply
Email info@v2heli.com or get in touch with V2 Helicopters to express your interest.
