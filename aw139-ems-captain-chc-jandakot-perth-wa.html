AW139 EMS Captain-Jandakot, Perth WA

Jandakot, Perth WA · Posted 2026-10-26 · 👁 … views

CHC Helicopter Australia delivers helicopter services across the offshore energy sector, emergency medical services and search and rescue. It is seeking an AW139 EMS Captain for a permanent, full-time position supporting its DFES contract based at Jandakot, Perth WA. The role operates on an eight-day roster cycle: two day shifts, two night shifts and four consecutive days off.

Essential
- Valid ATPL(H)
- Valid IR-C-ME(H) with a minimum of 3 renewals
- Minimum 3,000 flying hours (H)
- Minimum 1,200 multi-engine hours (H)
- Minimum 2,000 hours PIC (H)
- Current AW139 type rating with 100 PIC hours on type
- NVIS rating
- 200 hours night flying
- Low Level rating with Winch Endorsement
- Ability to obtain and maintain a CASA Class 1 Medical and ASIC

Desirable
- Previous SAR/EMS experience

Applicants must have the legal right to work in Australia. CHC manages this vacancy directly and may close applications early, so apply as early as possible.
