R44 Tour/Charter Pilot-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2025-12-17 · 👁 … views

Base Pilot at Heli Adventures Flights. Location: Taupo, New Zealand. Minimum Requirements: NZ CAA CPL(H) licence; R44 Rating; Right to live and work in New Zealand. Desirable: Commercial Experience; R44 Experience; Customer service Experience. As a Base Pilot, you'll be responsible for conducting scenic and charter flights over the stunning landscapes of the National Park, Kaimanawa Ranges, and around Taupo. This role requires a high level of professionalism and a commitment to safety while delivering exceptional customer experiences. You will be required to work closely with the chief pilot and other members of the team. Please note that this is not a purely flying role. In addition to piloting duties, you will be involved in day-to-day operations, including answering calls, managing emails and bookings, and ensuring top-tier customer service. For the right candidates, further career growth and advancement may be offered. Start date: Early January 2026. Only Successful Candidates will be contacted. Located at 415 Huka Falls Road, Taupo, New Zealand.

Apply via email: info@heliadventures.co.nz
