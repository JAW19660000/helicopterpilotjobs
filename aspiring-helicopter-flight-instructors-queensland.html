Aspiring - Helicopter Flight Instructors-Queensland

Queensland (Redcliffe) · Posted 2026-07-23 · 👁 … views

Aeropower is a leader in airborne electrical infrastructure, Maintenance and Construction throughout Australia and internationally. We also operate two busy flight schools in Redcliffe, QLD and Bankstown, NSW. We are seeking to employ a pilot to teach CPL theory whilst receiving training for their flight instructor rating. Join our busy flying school based at Redcliffe, QLD and be part of a talented and passionate team delivering high quality, safety focused training to Australia's next generation of helicopter pilots. Training for your instructor rating will be conducted in house at no cost to you and a reasonable return of service will be expected. Requirements: CPL(H); 250 hours rotary flight time; 100 hours rotary PIC; Current Class 1 aviation medical; Current ASIC; A strong understanding of the CPL(H) theory knowledge. Preferred: Cabri G2 experience; Previous adult education or other teaching experience; Cert IV in training and checking. The ideal candidate is motivated and enthusiastic about training and passing on their knowledge and skills to the next generation of pilots. A firm grasp of the rules, regulations and CPL(H) theory knowledge is essential.

Applications via SEEK (https://au.seek.com/job/93500537) or email hoo@aeropower.com.au
