B412/H145/AS332 Touring Captains-PNG-(Re-Post)

PNG · Posted 2025-08-19 · 👁 … views

HEVILIFT is a leading aviation charter service operating in Papua New Guinea, currently seeking expressions of interest for experienced B412/H145/AS332 Captains. This will involve mostly onshore utility flying in mountainous jungle terrain, some offshore possible in near future. Travel, internet, Life/disability and full lodging will be provided whilst on shift. Salaried position. Minimum 3000 hours TT; 200 hours ME; 50 hours on type; Longline experience an asset; Have the ability to operate in austere tropical mountain environments; Be able to safely operate in mountainous terrain VFR; Good command in English written and verbal communication; Multi crew /IFR experience an advantage; Previous utility experience an asset; IFR experience for H145/332 preferred. Candidates must hold existing endorsement on one of the above types or more. Candidates must be accident free in the last three years, operate to a high safety culture. Visa sponsorship will be supported for successful applicants outside Papua New Guinea. Interested applicants please send your PDF format application documents with salary expectation and B412 Captain (or one of the above types you have experience in) in the title via the application link below. If you have applied before and meet these requirements for one of the above aircraft types, please feel free to submit your CV again. We appreciate your applications and regret that we can only notify short-listed candidates.

Apply via email: hr@hevilift.com
