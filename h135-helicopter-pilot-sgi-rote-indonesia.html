H135 Helicopter Pilot-SGI Rote Indonesia

Rote, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia · Posted 2026-11-17 · 👁 … views

SGI is seeking a highly skilled and safety-conscious H135 Helicopter Pilot to join its flight operations team on Rote, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia. Employment type: fulltime / touring. The ideal candidate is a professional pilot with extensive multi-engine turbine experience and a commitment to the highest standards of safety and operational excellence. Applicants must demonstrate a professional, courteous, service-oriented demeanour appropriate for VIP passenger operations, have a good safety culture and be able to operate independently. This is a 6/6 touring position.

Key responsibilities
- Perform flight operations in accordance with company SOPs, DGCA regulations and client requirements
- Ensure the safe and efficient operation of the H135 helicopter for luxury corporate transport
- Maintain accurate flight documentation and mission reporting
- Collaborate with ground crews and maintenance teams to ensure the aircraft is mission ready

Requirements
- Valid CPL-H or ATPL-H
- Current H135 type rating with minimum 100 hrs on type; must have flown the type in the last 12 months
- Minimum 2,000 hours total helicopter time
- Minimum 500+ hours on multi-engine helicopters
- Proven experience in remote area operations
- Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate
- English proficiency (ICAO Level 4 minimum)
- Strong situational awareness, decision-making skills and ability to operate in challenging environments
- Professional, disciplined and safety oriented attitude

What’s on offer
- Competitive salary and benefits package
- Opportunity to operate advanced aircraft in a professional and challenging environment
- A solid commitment to flight safety and continuous professional development

How to apply
Send your updated CV, licence copies and logbook summary to talent.hub@sgi-aero.com with the subject: Application for H135 Pilot - [Full Name].
