Helicopter Pilot-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2026-02-20 · 👁 … views

Take your aviation career to new heights in one of the most iconic flying environments in the world. Heliworks Queenstown and Heli Glenorchy are locally owned and operated, specialising in premium tourism and commercial helicopter operations across the Southern Lakes and Fiordland regions. From alpine landings and heli-skiing to precision long line and fire operations, we operate at the highest professional standard while delivering unforgettable experiences to clients from around the globe. We are currently inviting applications from experienced helicopter pilots to join our team at either our Queenstown. This is a full-time, year-round position rostered 5 day work weeks. We are looking for more than just flying hours. We are seeking a pilot with a positive attitude, strong work ethic, and a genuine love for meeting people from around the world. Our pilots are ambassadors for our brand, and the ability to deliver a premium guest experience alongside technical excellence is essential. The successful applicant will have: NZ citizenship, residency, or legal right to work in New Zealand; Availability to work weekends, public holidays, and peak season (Dec-Mar); Excellent communication and prioritisation skills; Strong teamwork and adaptability; A positive, personable, and professional character. Minimum Requirements: 3000+ hours helicopter; Precision long line experience; Firefighting experience; Rated on AS350; Proven mountain flying experience including snow operations. Desirable Attributes: Multi-engine experience (BK117 / H145); Heli-skiing experience; Queenstown / Milford local knowledge; Instructor rating. Required (or ability to obtain): Current First Aid Certificate; CAA Dangerous Goods certification; NZ Class 1 Driver's Licence; NZTA Dangerous Goods endorsement. What We Offer: Competitive remuneration; Professional development opportunities; Access to exclusive landing locations and high-end tourism experiences; A supportive, collaborative team culture; A strong operational focus on safety and excellence; The opportunity to live and work in one of the most spectacular aviation environments in the world. Key Selling Points: Fly in a World-Class Aviation Environment; Premium Operations with a Supportive Team Culture; A full-time, stable position based in Queenstown or Glenorchy.

Apply via SEEK: https://www.seek.com.au/job/90449522
