R44 Aerial Work/Charter Pilot – Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-07-12 · 👁 … views

Helicopter Pilot (R44) – Line & Base Operations. Bekaa Air | Archerfield Airport & Tangalooma Island Resort. Bekaa Air is seeking a professional, customer-focused Helicopter Pilot to join our tours, charter, and aerial operations team. Operating a well-maintained fleet of H125/AS350, R44RII, and Bell 206L series helicopters, we are a close-knit team dedicated to delivering an outstanding customer experience.

Position Structure & Locations: This role operates across our mainland base at Archerfield Airport (Brisbane) and our island base at Tangalooma Island Resort. Please note that a mainland-only position is not available. Depending on candidate suitability and operational requirements, this role can be structured in one of three ways: Full-Time Dual-Base (roster split between Archerfield and Tangalooma); Full-Time Tangalooma (based exclusively on the island); Part-Time Tangalooma (3 days per week, based exclusively on the island).

About the Role: This is a hands-on, versatile position. Bekaa Air operates as a team, and all pilots share on ground and base operation responsibilities. Duties include: Manage day-to-day operations, including answering phone and email inquiries; Process bookings and manage flight schedules; Ensure aircraft are immaculately presented at all times; Deliver premium customer service from check-in to disembarkation; Collaborate with the Chief Pilot and Base Manager to assist with future company projects.

Minimum Qualifications: CASA CPL(H), Class 1 Medical, ASIC, Dangerous Goods; 200 hours total rotary time; 50 hours Robinson R44; Demonstrated competence operating in Class C & D airspace; Right to work in Australia (Mandatory).

The Ideal Candidate: Strong attention to detail with impeccable personal presentation; Highly self-motivated with the ability to work independently; Excellent communication skills for customer-facing and administrative duties; Desirable: Base Management experience; Desirable: Background in Marketing, Business, or Tourism; Desirable: AS350/H125 rating (100+ hours preferred) and/or 100+ hours on Bell 206L.

Additional Information: Remuneration commensurate with experience and above the relevant award. Start Date: Immediate start available. To apply, please submit your CV and a cover letter indicating your preferred operational structure to careers@bekaaair.com.au

Apply via email: careers@bekaaair.com.au
