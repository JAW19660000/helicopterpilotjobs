Line Pilot
Ongoing Full-time
Bankstown

About us
The NSW Police Force (NSWPF) is one of the largest police forces in the western world, with more than 20,000 employees, including more than 4,000 administrative employees who support the sworn officers that provide a range of law and order services 24 hours a day, seven days a week to the socially, geographically and culturally diverse community of NSW.

About the role
We have an opportunity for a Line Pilot - Rotary Wing to be part of our Aviation Command, Police Transport and Public Safety Command. The position of rotary line pilot undertakes the responsibility of flying single pilot multi-engine helicopters under the instrument flight rules in a safe and professional manner, supporting all operations of the aviation command and NSWPF objectives.

The successful candidates must hold the following requirements:
Valid Class 1 Medical
Commercial Pilot Licence (H) or ATPL (H)
Helicopter Instrument Rating (IR H) (ME)
Night Vision Imaging Rating (Grade 1)
Low Level Rating – winch, sling
3000 hours PIC helicopter
1000 hours experience turbine-powered helicopter aircraft
200 hours PIC night experience
Low level experience in helicopters

The successful position occupant may also be eligible to receive additional qualification based allowances, subject to maintaining eligibility: Instrument Flight Rules (IFR) $15,000 per annum; Night Vision Imagery System (NVIS) $15,000 per annum; bonus payment on attainment of all NVIS proficiencies (NVIS SAR – Winch, NVIS Tactical – Fast Rope, Aerial Fire Support) $5,000 per annum, with a further $5,000 per annum following 12 months of having completed IFR certification and all NVIS proficiencies.

How to apply
Applications can only be submitted electronically via the I Work for NSW website. Attach a cover letter (2 pages maximum) and an up-to-date resume, and address the two Target Questions (300 words each). Do not attach copies of qualifications. Enquiries: hiring manager A/Inspector Christine Woods.

Eligibility
Applicants must be an Australian citizen, a permanent resident of Australia or a New Zealand citizen. The successful candidate must undergo a National Police (criminal history) Check and obtain and maintain a Security Clearance. This recruitment may be used to create a Talent Pool for similar future roles over the next 18 months.

Apply via SEEK (https://au.seek.com/job/95192831) or the I Work for NSW website (applications are submitted electronically there)
