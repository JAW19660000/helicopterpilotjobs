EC135 HEMS Pilot (Touring)-South Australia

South Australia · Posted 2025-11-13 · 👁 … views

COMPANY BACKGROUND
Established in 1968, The Aerotech Group is South Australia's largest privately owned aviation company, with specialised aircraft, innovative thinking and a team of talented people. With 160+ staff and a fleet of 40 aircraft, Aerotech Group have a well-deserved reputation as an Australian leader in providing aerial solutions, working across several sectors including firefighting, agricultural spraying, mining support and medical evacuations.

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are excited to recruit a HEMS Pilot to work in the iconic Cooper Basin. You will operate in a challenging environment based at Moomba in a EC135 P2+ aircraft. The operation is 24/7 and requires MCC, IFR and NVIS skillsets to successfully conduct your role.

With access to the camp facilities, you will find yourself with your own room and ensuite, with access to extensive amenities including recreation and cinema rooms, sauna, pool, tennis/basketball and squash courts, a daily fitness class as well as three gyms (cardio, weights and general purpose/stretching/boxing). All meals are supplied.

At this time we are looking to fulfil the role of co-pilot however if you have experience more closely aligned to the captain minimums we still encourage you to apply. This role begins as a casual however you are able to transition into an Individual Flexibility Agreement (IFA) after a 3-month period, offering long-term stability and structure.

- Base of operations is the Cooper Basin, Moomba Airport. Company arranged connecting flights are available from any airport with an RPT service.
- Operate EC135P2+ helicopter under the VFR, IFR and NVIS conditions
- Typical roster periods of 15 days on 13 days off
- Self-manage daily duties and rest periods to maintain a 24 hour standby roster in accordance within Aerotech Helicopters FRMS
- Implement, review, update and contribute to company SOP's to facilitate continuous improvement of the operation
- Actively participate in the Safety Management System
- Conduct secondary duties as allocated by the Base Manager or Contract Manager from time to time
- Strive to ensure customer satisfaction and high standards of service are maintained

EXPERIENCE
- EC135 Type Rating (or similar complexity aircraft)
- Total hours helicopter experience: 2000 hours (captain), 1000 (co-pilot)
- Total hours in command helicopters: 1000 (captain), 100 (co-pilot)
- Multi engine experience: 500 PIC (captain), 250 (co-pilot)
- Instrument Rating with MEH endorsement
- NVIS rating
- Class 1 Medical
- Hours on EC135: 100 (captain), 50 (co-pilot) (desirable)
- Holding, or working towards Helicopter ATPL (desirable)

CULTURE AND BENEFITS
Boasting an enviable, relaxed and down to earth culture, you will get to work within a well-established and successful family business, where staff pride themselves on providing exceptional service to their varied clients. Additionally, you will be part of a South Australian success story, working within a team who are passionate about aviation and committed to all that they do.
