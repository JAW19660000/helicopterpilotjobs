Helicopter Pilot-TSV Heli Townsville QLD

Townsville, QLD · Posted 2026-11-24 · 👁 … views

TSV Heli is a CASA authorised pilot training facility with its main operating base in Townsville, North Queensland. The flight training school operates from an established, private facility adjacent to the city’s main airport, offering rotary and fixed wing flight training from ab-initio to advanced, as well as charter and aerial work, supported by an in-house engineering department.

What TSV Heli is looking for
A motivated pilot looking to join a dynamic team of aviation professionals. Full time.

What’s on offer
- Supported internal training
- Relaxed, professional environment
- Well maintained, modern fleet encompassing R22, R44, AS350 and EC135
- Diverse training environments
- Aerial work and charter opportunities
- Ongoing professional pilot development
- The ability to be home most nights and weekends, occasional travel may be required

Minimum requirements
- CPL(H)
- 250 hours flight time as a pilot and 100 hours as pilot in command; or CPL(H) completed through an integrated course of training and passed the flight test for the grant of a helicopter category rating
- Class 1 medical
- Current ASIC
- Working with Children Blue Card (or ability to obtain)
- HUET (or ability to obtain)
- DAMP
- Dangerous Goods
- Australian citizen or permanent resident

TSV Heli is an equal opportunity employer and encourages applicants from all genders and ethnic backgrounds to apply.
