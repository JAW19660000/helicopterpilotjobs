EOI Future Line Pilot Positions-NSW

NSW · Posted 2026-06-17 · 👁 … views

Job summary: Toll Group is seeking expressions of interest for future Line Pilot positions with our Helicopter Emergency Medical Services (HEMS) in NSW/ACT. This role offers an opportunity to provide critical aeromedical services while working on an equal time roster. Candidates must hold a CASA Air Transport Pilots License (Helicopter), have extensive flying experience, and meet specific medical and training requirements. Enjoy a competitive salary with opportunities for growth within a global organization.

Qualifications: 2500 hours rotary wing flight experience; 1500 PIC turbine rotary wing; 500 hours PIC multi engine turbine rotary wing; Minimum 12 months and 100hrs PIC in EMS/SAR operation.

Responsibilities: Provide safe, reliable, mission-critical aeromedical services; Work with Base Managers on an equal time roster; Operate within the Toll Helicopters Enterprise Bargaining Agreement 2022.

Skills: Crew resource management; Communication skills; NVG Endorsement; Multi-Engine Command Instrument Rating; Night, winch and IFR experience.

Education: CASA Air Transport Pilots License (Helicopter); CASA Class 1 Medical.

About Toll Group: At Toll, we do more than just logistics – we move the businesses that move the world. Our 16,000 team members help solve any logistics, transport, or supply chain challenge – big or small. We have been supporting our customers for more than 130 years, with 500 sites in 27 markets and a forwarding network spanning 150 countries. We are proudly part of Japan Post.

About Toll Aviation: Toll Aviation, part of the Toll Group, operates multiple contracts across Australia and is looking for experienced pilots interested in joining our HEMS team in the future. Our highly qualified team of pilots, aircrew, engineers and safety managers have wide experience in a diverse range of operations, under challenging conditions, including remote, maritime and mountainous locations, in all weather conditions – day or night.

Job Description: Toll Aviation is seeking expressions of interest for future vacancies on our HEMS contract in NSW and the ACT. These opportunities allow you to play an integral role in our specialist helicopter business, providing safe, reliable, mission-critical aeromedical services to NSW and ACT Health. Reporting to Base Managers, these positions work on an equal time roster and employment is pursuant to the 'Toll Helicopters' Enterprise Bargaining Agreement 2022.

Qualifications And Experience: Excellent crew resource management and communication skills; CASA Air Transport Pilots License (Helicopter) with the appropriate endorsements and ratings to act as pilot in command of all NSW/ACT EMS operations authorized by the Operator's certificate; 2500 hours rotary wing flight experience; 1500 PIC turbine rotary wing; 500 hours PIC multi engine turbine rotary wing; Minimum 12 months and 100hrs PIC in EMS/SAR operation; NVG Endorsement with minimum 50hrs PIC NVG; Multi-Engine Command Instrument Rating with minimum 3 renewals; CASA Class 1 Medical; Comprehensive night, winch and IFR experience.

Highly desirable: AW139 Type Endorsement with minimum 100hrs PIC relevant experience on type; EMS/SAR experience on AW139.

You will be rewarded with an attractive salary and the added security of joining a global organization willing to explore opportunities both within Australia and internationally. You must be entitled to work in Australia and be prepared to undertake pre-employment checks including a criminal history check and medical.
