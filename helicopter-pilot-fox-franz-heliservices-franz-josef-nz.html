Helicopter Pilot-Franz Josef Glacier, West Coast, NZ

Franz Josef Glacier, West Coast, New Zealand · Posted 2026-09-18 · 👁 … views

We're seeking an experienced helicopter pilot to deliver unforgettable scenic flights across South Westland's most iconic destinations - including Franz Josef Glacier, Fox Glacier, and Haast. You'll provide premium alpine experiences, blending tourism and aerial support in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

The position is for a full-time line pilot for a six-days on, two-days off roster based primarily in Franz Josef.

Essential Requirements:
- Legal Work Eligibility: Must hold New Zealand citizenship or the legal right to work in New Zealand
- New Zealand CPL(H) with a current Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Minimum 1500 hours rotary
- Hughes 500 rating and experience
- AS350 rating and experience
- Mountain flying experience
- Flexibility to work weekends and public holidays
- Reliable and safety conscious with a strong work ethic

Preferred Requirements:
- Proficiency in external load operations
- Mountain flying experience
- Dangerous Goods Certificate - CAA and NZTA

Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct scenic passenger operations, including alpine landings
- Conduct aerial utility tasks - long-line operations, film/charter flights, and hunters
- Safely operate in diverse mountain environments and variable weather

What We Offer:
- Stunning landscapes: Glacier valleys and pristine wilderness
- Supportive work environment: Close-knit, experienced and safety-driven team culture
- Competitive salary: Based on your experience, qualifications, and flight hours

Please forward your CV to ceo@heliservices.nz
