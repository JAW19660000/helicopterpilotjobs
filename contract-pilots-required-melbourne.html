Contract Pilots Required-Melbourne

Melbourne · Posted 2026-06-30 · 👁 … views

Helicopter Pilot Single Engine – On Shore. Looking for a change? Based at Essendon Fields, we're looking for Part-Time, Casual, or Contract Helicopter Pilots to join our team.

Do You Fit the Picture? You will succeed in this role because you: are proactive, focused and adaptable, and want to be part of a team; are well presented and have excellent communication and interpersonal skills; are a people person, warm and confident; are safety conscious.

Job Requirements: Have a current Victorian Drivers License; Have a current Working with Children's Check 'Employee' (or willing to get); Hold a current CPL(H); Hold a current Class 1 medical; Hold a current 'Red' ASIC; Hold a current DAMP check; Have at least 500 hrs Pilot in Command (PIC) of a single-engine helicopter; Have at least 150 hrs Pilot in Command Turbine (H); Hold endorsements in the following helicopters: Airbus H125, Bell 206 (preferred but not mandatory), Robinson R44.

Successful applicants will need to successfully finish a criminal record check and undergo a relevant proficiency check and training flight.

Apply by email: careers@leisuretours.com.au (c/o Head of Flying Operations)
