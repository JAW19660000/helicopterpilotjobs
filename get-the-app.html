Get the Helicopter Pilot Jobs App

Helicopter Pilot Jobs can be installed on your phone, tablet or computer like an app — no app store needed. Once installed, it opens in its own window with its own icon, and loads faster on return visits.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome, for this to work on iPhone/iPad).
  2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.

The app icon will now appear on your Home Screen alongside your other apps.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app, if shown).
  4. Confirm by tapping Add or Install.

The app icon will now appear on your Home Screen.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open this website in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look for an install icon in the address bar (usually on the right-hand side, a small screen with a down arrow), or open the three-dot menu and choose Install Helicopter Pilot Jobs.
  3. Click Install when prompted.

The app will open in its own window and can be launched from your desktop, taskbar, or Start menu/Applications folder, just like any other app.
