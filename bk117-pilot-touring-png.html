BK117 Pilot (Touring)

Papua New Guinea · Posted 2026-08-14 · 👁 … views

We're expanding!

Manolos Aviation is seeking experienced BK117 Pilots for a 28 days on / 28 days off touring position in Papua New Guinea.

For suitable candidates, there will also be an opportunity to progress to the Bell 412.

Roster: 28 days on / 28 days off touring position

Minimum Requirements:
- 2000 hours total helicopter flight time
- 1500 PIC turbine time
- Current & Valid medical
- 100 hours on type
- Ability to operate in remote and demanding environments
- Excellent interpersonal skills

Advantages:
- Mountain flying experience
- Current CASA PNG CPL (H) / ATPL (H)
- IFR Rating

Note: Successful candidates will have the opportunity to progress onto the Bell 412.

Send your CV and Cover Letter to: hr@manolosaviation.org
