NT · Posted 2025-09-09 · 👁 … views

R22 Mustering Pilot
Location: Katherine, NT
Full-Time Position – other arrangements by negotiation

We are currently seeking an experienced R22 mustering pilot to join our team at NT Heli-Ops. This is a hands-on role suited to someone with a strong work ethic, excellent stock sense, and a genuine passion for the agriculture industry.

Requirements:
- CPL-H
- Minimum 1000 hours mustering experience in Robinson R22 helicopters
- Proven ground mustering experience using motorbikes and horses
- Strong understanding of cattle behaviour and low-stress stock handling
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team
- Current CASA license and 1st class medical
- Must be safety-conscious, and reliable

Desirable Attributes:
- Station work experience beyond flying (e.g., fencing, yard work, maintenance)
- Mechanically minded with basic helicopter maintenance awareness
- Adaptable and calm under pressure
- R44 endorsement an advantage
- Current car license

What We Offer:
- Competitive salary package commensurate with experience
- Well-maintained aircraft and equipment
- Supportive team environment with opportunities for long-term employment
- Accommodation available

If you're a skilled mustering pilot who's keen to fly over some of Northern Australia's most scenic landscapes, we'd love to hear from you. Resumes and cover letters can be emailed.

Apply via email to operations@ntheli-ops.com.au
