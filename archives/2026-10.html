Head Of Flying Operations - Rotary Wing-Brisbane QLD

Brisbane QLD · Posted 2026-10-02 · 👁 … views

Lead an operation that makes a real difference. As Head of Flying Operations - Rotary Wing, you will lead a high-performing team, uphold the highest standards of safety and compliance, and drive operational excellence across all rotary wing activities.

As a key member of the Queensland Government Air (QGAir) aviation leadership team, you will help shape strategic direction while supporting life-saving aeromedical, search and rescue, and emergency response missions across Queensland.

Queensland Government Air (QGAir) forms part of the Queensland Police Service (QPS), Aviation Capability Group (ACG) and provides government aviation services specialising in both rotary and fixed wing operations in support of the QPS and partner agencies in regular and emergency response operations across Queensland.

This is a full-time 3-year contract position. Whilst the position is currently based in Brisbane, applications are also accepted from individuals interested in being based in Cairns or Townsville. The final location will be determined through negotiation with the successful candidate.

What's offered: Competitive salary package, 12.75% superannuation, salary sacrifice up to $17,000 reportable fringe benefit amount, relocation assistance available, career growth and development opportunities.

Mandatory requirements:
- Current Australian Air Transport Pilots Licence (ATPL)
- Low Level Rating
- Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS) Rating
- Multi Engine Command Instrument Rating - 5 Renewals
- Minimum 2,500 hours total time
- Minimum 1,500 hours Pilot in Command (PIC) Helicopter
- Minimum 500 hours Night PIC Helicopter
- Minimum 1,500 hours Multi-Engine PIC Helicopter
- Minimum 1,200 hours Turbine Helicopter
- Minimum 100 hours NVIS Operations
- Hold or be eligible to hold an Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC)
- Valid Class 1 Aviation Medical
- Comprehensive understanding of CASR 1998 Part 119, 133 and Part 138, with a strong track record of operating compliantly and leading a workforce in that environment

Closing Date: Monday, 19th October 2026. Job Ad Reference: QLD/703256/26
