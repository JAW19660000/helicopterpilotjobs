- Previous military flying instruction experience
Highly desirable: AUS / UK / US AH-64E V6 qualification; military standards or instructor assessor role (or civilian equivalent)

International Helicopter Pilot (HEMS Pool)-USA

Tuscaloosa, Alabama, USA · Posted 2026-11-03 · 👁 … views

Air Methods is seeking international pilots who are looking for the right to work in the United States and are eager to join its Helicopter Emergency Medical Services (HEMS) team. Pilots help transport medical crews and patients, on a 7/7 schedule, with strict compliance to FARs, the AIM, the General Operations Manual and Air Methods policies. Listed location: Tuscaloosa, Alabama (US-AL-Tuscaloosa). Regular full-time. Travel approximately 10% of the time.

Immigration support
For qualifying pilots selected for a conditional offer, Air Methods provides outside immigration counsel and covers the associated legal and filing fees to support the EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) process.

Responsibilities
- Operate Air Methods aircraft safely and legally
- Pre-flight inspections and walk-arounds; sign off the Daily Flight Log
- Final authority for operation of the aircraft and safety of passengers, cargo and equipment
- Know all aircraft and medical systems, including backup aircraft
- Detailed flight planning at short notice, accurate documentation
- Take part in meetings, projects, committees and community outreach

Experience and conditions
- Associate degree (or equivalent) and 2-5 years related experience; bachelor degree preferred
- Flown in category for a commercial operator or the military within the previous 24 months (otherwise reviewed for a flight assessment program)
- Flight crew weight not to exceed 250 lb; under 70 years of age

VFR program
- 2,000 total hours, minimum 1,500 in category
- 1,000 hours PIC in category
- 500 hours rotor wing turbine
- 200 hours cross-country, at least 50 at night
- 100 hours unaided night as PIC (50 unaided can be substituted by 100 hours NVG time)
- 50 hours actual or hood instrument time in flight and in category (simulator does not count)

IFR program
- 2,500 total hours, minimum 2,000 in category
- 1,000 hours PIC in category
- 500 hours cross-country, 100 hours night
- 75 hours actual or simulated instrument time, at least 50 in an aircraft including 20 in actual instrument conditions in category

Certificates
- FAA licensure; proof of hours via logbook
- Commercial and Instrument rating for category and class (ATP in category meets this)
- First or second class FAA medical certificate

AS350 B3+ Fire Pilot (Contract)-Orange NSW

AS350 B3+ Fire Pilot – 2026/27 Australian Fire Season
Orange Helicopters | Orange, NSW | Seasonal contract

Orange Helicopters is seeking an experienced AS350 B3+ Fire Pilot to join its team for the upcoming 2026/27 Australian fire season. This is a seasonal independent contractor opportunity for an experienced pilot who is confident operating in demanding fire and utility environments and who places safety, professionalism and sound decision-making at the forefront of every operation.

The role
- Aerial firefighting and water-bombing operations
- Longline and sling operations
- Fire mapping and aerial reconnaissance
- Flight planning considering weather, terrain, fuel and operational requirements
- Coordination with fire agencies, incident controllers and ground crews
- Pre-flight and post-flight aircraft inspections, accurate flight and operational record keeping
- Line pilot and other authorised commercial helicopter operations as required
During quieter periods, other aviation, operational, logistical or practical duties may also be available.

Mandatory requirements
- Current CASA CPL(H) or higher
- Current Class 1 medical
- Current ASIC
- Experienced AS350 pilot
- Experienced longline / Vertical Reference Longline (VRLL) pilot
- Appropriate Low Level and Sling Load qualifications/endorsements
- Demonstrated aerial firefighting or relevant utility helicopter experience
- Meet all applicable Australian aerial firefighting standards, agency, competency and recency requirements
- Eligible and current to undertake Australian fire operations
- Strong judgement, situational awareness and aircraft handling in high-workload environments
- Able to work professionally with emergency services, ground crews and other aviation operators

Current HUET, CRM, Dangerous Goods, Wildfire Awareness, Wire Awareness and relevant fire/utility training will be highly regarded. Applicants should provide a detailed flying-hours breakdown: Total PIC | Turbine | AS350 | Longline/VRLL | Sling | Firefighting | Bucketing.

Contract and remuneration
Independent contractor engagement, compensated and evaluated on the experience of the pilot. Additional line-pilot and approved aerial-work duties may be available. Contract rates reviewed after the first month, subject to satisfactory performance. Further incentives based on individual performance and ongoing operational contribution.

What is provided
Reasonable pre-approved travel and accommodation for active operational assignments, per the contractor agreement. Invoices are submitted fortnightly and payable within 14 days of receipt. Contract pilots maintain their own ABN, tax and superannuation, licences, medical and ASIC, endorsements, and public liability and professional indemnity insurance.

Email Dean Brus, Chief Executive Officer, Orange Helicopters Pty Ltd: dean@orangehelicopters.com.au or phone +61 438 270 370. Send your CV, flying-hours breakdown, licence and endorsements, fire/longline experience and fire-season availability. Source listing: helijobs.net.
