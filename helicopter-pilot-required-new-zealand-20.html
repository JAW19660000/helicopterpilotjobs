Helicopter Pilot Required-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2025-11-21 · 👁 … views

We're looking for a skilled and personable tourism helicopter pilot to join our team. This is a rare opportunity to fly in one of the most breathtaking regions in the world, delivering premium scenic experiences across Queenstown, Fiordland, Milford Sound, Earnslaw Burn and beyond.

We operate in a fast-paced, guest-focused environment with a strong commitment to safety, professionalism, and unforgettable experiences. You'll need to be confident flying in mountainous terrain, experienced in tourism operations, and ready to be an integral part of a tight-knit team.

The successful applicant will have:
- New Zealand citizenship/residency or the legal right to work in New Zealand
- Availability to work every weekend, public holidays and during peak season (Dec-Mar)
- Excellent communication and prioritisation skills
- Strong teamwork and adaptability
- A positive, personable, and professional character

Minimum requirements:
- NZ CPL(H) and current Class 1 medical
- Rated on AS350
- Proven mountain flying experience
- Ability to work every weekend on a 5 on and 2 off roster

Required or able to obtain the following certifications/licenses:
- Current First Aid certificate
- CAA Dangerous Goods certification
- NZ Class 1 driver's licence
- NZTA Dangerous Goods endorsement

What we offer:
- Full-time, Queenstown or Glenorchy based role with variety and flexibility
- Competitive pay and professional development opportunities
- Access to exclusive landing locations and high-end client experiences
- A collaborative, passionate team culture with a strong focus on safety and guest service

If you're ready to fly in one of the world's most iconic aviation environments and deliver premium tourism experiences, we'd love to hear from you.
