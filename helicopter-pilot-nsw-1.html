Helicopter Pilot-NSW

NSW (Bankstown Airport) · Posted 2026-06-30 · 👁 … views

Helicopter pilot – Bell 212 Eagle Single. EPS Helicopter Service's was established in 2003 and is a joint venture partner of NRMA Insurance operating their Bell 212 Eagle Single. The Role: EPS is seeking expressions of interest for an experienced Bell medium pilot for the 2026/27 fire season based at Bankstown Airport NSW. Our offer: Contract period 1 November 2026 – 28 February 2027; Even time roster, 7 days on/7 days off; $100,000.00 package; Contract period up to 5 years for the right person post the initial first term. Minimum requirements: Australian CPL (H); 2500 TT helicopters; 100+ hours Bell 212/205 or UH1; 100+ hours fire experience; Low level; APC; ASIC; HUET; DG/CRM.

Apply via email: info@epsheli.com.au
