EOI Aviation Opportunities (Pilots, Aircrew, Engineers)-Babcock Adelaide SA

Adelaide, SA · Posted 2026-11-23 · 👁 … views

Join Australia’s leading rotary-wing emergency services provider. Babcock is Australia’s leading provider of rotary-wing emergency and critical services, delivering life-saving Helicopter Emergency Medical Services (HEMS), Search and Rescue (SAR), aerial surveillance and airborne law enforcement operations across the country, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, for government, emergency services and community organisations.

As Babcock continues to grow and support operations across Australia, it is seeking expressions of interest from experienced aviation professionals who are passionate about making a difference and contributing to missions that save lives and support communities.

The roles
- Pilots
- Aircrew Officers
- Rescue Crew Officers
- Aircraft Maintenance Engineers
- Avionics Engineers
- Aviation Support and Operations Personnel
- Project Management and delivery

Why join Babcock
- Meaningful career pathways and opportunities for advancement
- Investment in training, development and ongoing capability growth
- Recognition and reward for high performance
- A culture of safety, teamwork and operational excellence
- The opportunity to contribute to genuinely meaningful, purpose-driven work
