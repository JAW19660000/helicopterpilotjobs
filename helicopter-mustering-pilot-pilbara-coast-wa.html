Helicopter Mustering Pilot-Pilbara Coast, WA

Pilbara Coast, WA · Posted 2026-02-02 · 👁 … views

Clock real mustering hours, fly well-maintained aircraft, and work across some of WA's most iconic cattle country.

- Hands-on mustering work flying R22 helicopters across large-scale Pilbara stations. - A varied role combining aerial mustering, inspections, and station support. - Attractive remote package including accommodation, meals, and lifestyle benefits.

Our client is a family-owned aggregation of cattle stations located along the Pilbara coastal region surrounding Port Hedland, WA. Operating across multiple northern WA coastal properties, the business runs a well-established Brahman and Brahman-cross pastoral enterprise. They are known for their strong safety culture, professionalism, and supportive working environment, offering employees the chance to work in one of Australia's most unique remote landscapes.

This role reports directly to Station Management and works closely with ground crews and operational staff. It is ideally suited to experienced helicopter pilots who are confident mustering in remote pastoral environments and who value safety, teamwork, and sound decision-making. Positions are available for the 2026 mustering season, with flexibility for seasonal or year-round engagement.

Key Responsibilities & Duties: - Conduct aerial mustering of cattle using R22 helicopters. - Perform water runs, infrastructure inspections, and station support flights. - Complete all pre-flight, post-flight, and operational checks in line with regulations. - Maintain accurate flight records while coordinating closely with station management and ground crews.

Selection Criteria: - Proven experience as a helicopter pilot in pastoral, agricultural, or remote operations. - Current PPL (Helicopter) with valid medical and helicopter mustering endorsement. - Demonstrated ability to work independently and make sound decisions under pressure. - Strong communication skills and a commitment to safety and regulatory compliance.

Favourable Experience: - Mustering operations beyond standard requirements. - Aerial surveying, mapping, or related services. - Familiarity with Brahman cattle. - Mechanical aptitude and basic helicopter maintenance knowledge.

Additional Particulars: - Accommodation provided - private room with ensuite. - All meals supplied. - Competitive remuneration rates. - Well-maintained aircraft and hangar infrastructure. - Access to Pilbara coastline for fishing and camping. - Opportunities to assist in other station operations. - Work opportunities may be available for a partner. - Flexible engagement - seasonal or year-round options. - No dogs permitted. - Applications will be considered as they are received. - This is a permanent position and not available to Working Holiday Visa Holders (Backpackers). Nor does our client offer sponsorship for overseas jobseekers.
