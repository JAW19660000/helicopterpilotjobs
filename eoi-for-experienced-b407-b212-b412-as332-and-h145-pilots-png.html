EOI For Experienced B407/B212/B412, AS332 and H145 Pilots - PNG

PNG · Posted 2026-07-27 · 👁 … views

HEVILIFT is a leading aviation charter service operating in Papua New Guinea, currently seeking expressions of interest for experienced B407/B212/B412, AS332 and H145 pilots. Minimum 3000 hours TT: 3000 hours total flight time in helicopters, 300 hours on type, 100 hours VRLL, ICAO English Level 6, Accident free in the last 3 years, Below 65 years of age. Hevilift offers a competitive salary package, Visa sponsorship, flights from home base to PNG and return, and accommodation with meals to the selected candidate. Interested applicants please send your updated CV or resume and copy of certificates before close of business (COB) on the 15th of September, 2026. Only short-listed candidates will be contacted for interview.
