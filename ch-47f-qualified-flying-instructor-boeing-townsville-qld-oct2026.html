CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor-Townsville, QLD

Townsville, Northern QLD · Posted 2026-10-12 · 👁 … views

About the role
Boeing Defence Australia is shaping the future of aerospace and delivering some of the nation's most important programs for the Australian Defence Force. We are seeking a highly skilled CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor (QFI) to join our Chinook Integrated-Services Support (CISS) contract in Townsville.

This position is embedded within the School of Army Aviation (SAA) Chinook Wing and plays a critical role in the training and readiness of aviation personnel. It requires a dedicated professional capable of delivering high-level instruction across airborne, synthetic and ground-based environments while maintaining the highest standards of flight safety.

Responsibilities
Instructional delivery: execute high-quality CH-47F airborne, synthetic and ground-based instruction to support pilot development.
Flight operations: oversee and conduct CH-47F general flying operations in accordance with established protocols.
Compliance and currency: maintain all flying currency and professional qualifications as mandated by Army Aviation Orders, Instructions and Publications.
Safety leadership: champion a robust safety culture.
Operational support and continuous improvement of training methodologies.

Essential criteria
CH-47F QFI (Category C)
CH-47F NVIS Command
Group 4 Command Instrument Rating
CASA CPL-H (or higher)
Current Class 1 Medical
CASA ASIC
Ability to obtain and maintain an NV1 Defence security clearance

Desirable criteria
CH-47F QFI (Category B/A)
Group 4 Instrument Rating Assessor
CH-47F TFPS Instructor Operator Simulator
CH-47F Maintenance Pilot qualification
Simulator Reference Pilot

Eligibility
Applicants must be Australian citizens to meet Defence security requirements and must be willing to undergo the standard government vetting process. BDA will sponsor and support the required clearance process. This site-specific position is subject to physical requirements.

Benefits
Competitive salary and yearly bonuses; paid leave entitlements; discounted health insurance, travel and retail shopping with a nationwide Fitness Passport; novated leasing and salary sacrifice for superannuation; mentorship and career development; recognition award program.
