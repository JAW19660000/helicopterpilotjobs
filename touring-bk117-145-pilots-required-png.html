WE'RE HIRING - H145 LINE PILOT. LOCATION: PAPUA NEW GUINEA. COMPANY: HELI NIUGINI LIMITED. Key Responsibilities: Ensure safe and compliant flight operations in accordance with company and civil aviation regulations; Conduct pre-flight and post-flight inspections and maintain accurate flight documentation; Supervise refueling, loading, and passenger embarkation/disembarkation procedures; Maintain professional conduct, discipline, and communication at all times; Report and record any maintenance discrepancies in accordance with company procedures. Qualifications - Captains: ATPL/CPL; 3,000+ total flight hours; 1,500+ PIC hours (including 1,500 PIC Multi-Engine); 500+ hours on H145; Comfortable operating at high density altitude (DA) exceeding 10,000 ft. Experience & Skills: Previous experience in remote base operations; Strong communication and teamwork skills; Ability to operate effectively in a multicultural environment; High attention to safety, detail, and operational discipline; Proven longline (VRLL) experience with up to 200' Longline. BK117 LINE PILOT - WE'RE HIRING - BK117 LINE PILOT. LOCATION: PAPUA NEW GUINEA. COMPANY: HELI NIUGINI LIMITED. Key Responsibilities: Ensure safety of passengers, crew, and cargo at all times; Conduct pre-flight and post-flight inspections and documentation; Supervise refueling operations and ensure compliance with regulations; Maintain high discipline and professionalism during all operations; Conduct flights in accordance with Company Exposition and Operations Manual; Maintain accurate flight logs and records; Ensure all operational, meteorological, and technical requirements are met before flight. Experience & Skills: Proven experience in remote base operations; Strong communication and leadership skills; Ability to work effectively in a multi-cultural environment; High attention to safety, detail, and operational discipline; Proven longline (VRLL) experience with up to 200' Longline.
