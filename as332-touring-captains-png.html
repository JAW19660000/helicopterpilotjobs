AS332 Touring Captains

HEVILIFT is a leading aviation charter service operating in Papua New Guinea, currently seeking expressions of interest for a short term experienced IFR Offshore AS332 contract Captain. This will involve attending sim in France first week of December. Contract start January/February for 6 to 8 weeks—competitive USD day rate.

Minimum 3000 hours TT - 500 hours ME - 100 hours on type - Below 65 years of age - Good command in English written and verbal communication. - Multi crew / IFR offshore experience required - IFR experience for any AS332 with 100 hours on type

Candidates must be accident free in the last three years, operate to a high safety culture. Visa sponsorship will be supported for successful applicants outside Papua New Guinea.

Apply via website (hevilift.com/pngjobs)
