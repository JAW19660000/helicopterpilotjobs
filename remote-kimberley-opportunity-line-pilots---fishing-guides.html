Remote Kimberley Opportunity - Line Pilots / Fishing Guides

Not specified · Posted 2026-05-18 · 👁 … views

About Us: Mount Hart Wilderness Lodge is a unique and highly remote outback destination in the Kimberley region. We're looking for an experienced pilots to join our airwing operation for the 2026 dry season. The Role: As a Line Pilot, you will take Mount Hart guests on incredible adventures across the breathtaking West Kimberley region. Scenic helicopter tours include off-airport landings in spectacular Kimberley destinations. All Line Pilots will fly R44 aircrafts, ensuring the highest standard of safety, customer service, and professionalism. This is a full-time, seasonal role. Season concludes 30 September. Are you a fishing enthusiast with your pilot's licence? Turn your passion for fishing and flying into a once-in-a-lifetime role in the Kimberley. We're on the lookout for a Fishing Guide Pilot to join our team, delivering unforgettable remote fishing adventures by air. This is a hands-on, adventure-driven role combining piloting, guiding, and guest experience in one of Australia's most spectacular regions. Key Responsibilities: Safely operate aircraft on helicopter tours across the West Kimberley region, including popular destinations such as Horizontal Falls, Bell Gorge, Isdell Gorge, Charnley Gorge, and Twin Flame Falls, including some off-airport landings in the remote Kimberley; Conduct pre-flight inspections and daily reporting to ensure all aircraft are maintained to the highest standards; Provide exceptional customer service to passengers, ensuring a memorable, knowledgeable, and enjoyable experience; Collaborate with ground crew and other team members to ensure smooth operations; Adhere to all aviation regulations and company policies. Essential Qualifications: Commercial Pilots Licence (CPL)(H); CASA Class 1 Medical Certificate; Remote flying experience; Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC); Current Single Engine Helicopter (SEH) Flight Review; Current Dangerous Goods Awareness Certificate; Gas Turbine Engine Rating; Desirable: Low Level Rating; Dangerous Goods Awareness Course; Proficient knowledge of Australian aviation legislation; High level of written and verbal communication skills; Driver's Licence (manual); Excellent communication and interpersonal skills; Ability to work well under pressure and adapt to changing circumstances; Australian citizenship or appropriate work visa. Minimum Experience: Line Pilot: Minimum 500 flying hours (1,000+ desirable). Desirable Experience: Instructor rating Grade 3 or higher; Helicopter Underwater Escape Training (HUET); Low Level with Sling. Benefits: Competitive wages commensurate with experience; Seasonal work from May to October, offering flexibility during the off-season; Opportunity to fly over some of the most stunning landscapes in Australia; Collaborative and supportive work environment; Potential for career advancement within the company. Application Process: To apply for the position, please submit your resume, cover letter, and copies of relevant licences and certifications.

Apply via email: jobs@mounthart.com.au
