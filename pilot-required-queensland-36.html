Pilot Required-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-07-26 · 👁 … views

Country Mile Helicopters is a family-owned, independent aerial application business based in Innisfail FNQ. We pride ourselves on providing reliable, high-quality aerial application to our local farming community. We are currently looking for a reliable, safety-focused pilot to join our team. This position is open to experienced ag pilots or a motivated pilot looking to transition into the ag industry.

Qualifications: Commercial Pilot's Licence (CPL-H); Class 1 Medical; Low-Level Rating; Minimum 500 hours PIC; R44 Endorsement; Current AAAA Spraysafe Accreditation, Chemical Licence and Aerial Application Rating or willing to obtain; HR Licence desirable. Salary to be based on experience.
