Commercial Helicopter Pilot-New Zealand

New Zealand (New Plymouth) · Posted 2026-03-19 · 👁 … views

Company Background: Skywork Helicopters is a family owned and operated aviation company with bases in Whangarei, Thames, Taranaki, and our head office in Warkworth. As an industry-leader, we pride ourselves on consistently delivering helicopter support services to the highest standard. Skywork provide a diverse range of professional aviation services including precision lifting, human external cargo, search and rescue, infrastructure surveying, firefighting, charter and agricultural services operating throughout New Zealand. The job comes with variety and team comradery. The minimum experience requirements for this position provides an opportunity for a low hour pilot, with pathways to develop and progress your general aviation skills. Position: We are looking for a motivated and passionate team player for a line pilot role at our New Plymouth facility. The successful applicant will have: NZ citizenship, residency, or legal right to work in New Zealand; Ability to listen and learn; Motivated to work around weather; Good communication; Adaptability and prioritisation skills; Team player; A positive, personable and professional character. Minimum Requirements: NZ Commercial Helicopter Licence; Gas Turbine Exam; Class 1 Medical; Helicopter Ag Rating Grade 2 or 1. Required (or ability to obtain): Current First Aid Certificate; CAA Dangerous Goods Certification; NZ Class 1 and 2 Driver's Licence; NZTA Dangerous Goods endorsement. What we Offer: Competitive remuneration; Working with modern aircraft; Type ratings; Industry leading role and support equipment; A strong operational focus on safety, precision and excellence; Opportunity to develop utility pilot skills and experience. Your responsibilities and remuneration will reflect your skill and experience. If you don't meet the applicant criteria or minimum requirement, then please do not respond.

Apply via SEEK NZ: https://www.seek.co.nz/job/91012348
