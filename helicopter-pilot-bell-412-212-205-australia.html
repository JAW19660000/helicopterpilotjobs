Helicopter Pilot (Bell 412/212/205)-Australia

Australia · Posted 2026-02-11 · 👁 … views

Our client is a leading global helicopter operator, providing aerial firefighting, lifting, charter, emergency assistance, and disaster-response services across multiple countries. They are currently looking for Helicopter Pilots (Bell 412/212/205) who will take on the responsibility of conducting helicopter firefighting operations during both the firefighting and non-firefighting season. This is a position on an equal touring rotation (6 weeks on/ 6 weeks off) with company accommodation, travel and meal allowance provided. Job Responsibilities: Ensuring safe and compliant operations under CASA, FAA, and company regulations. Conducting thorough pre- and post-flight inspections. Monitoring weather, flight conditions, and operational risks. Collaborating effectively with crew, engineers, and support staff to maintain mission readiness. This role requires flexibility, adaptability, and strong decision-making in fast-paced, high-risk environments, including night and instrument flight operations. Job Requirements: Valid CPL; Bell 412/212/205 Type Rating; Total Time: 1500 Hours; IFR/NVG rating; Preferred experience in working with Firefighting/SAR/Longlining operations.
