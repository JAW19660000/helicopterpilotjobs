ATO Helicopter Pilot (Single Engine)-Port Hedland, WA

Port Hedland, Port Hedland, Karratha & Pilbara WA · Posted 2026-08-17 · 👁 … views

Auriga Group is a safe, focused, customer centric, dedicated team of Marine and Aviation professionals located across Australia. Auriga Group comprises three operational divisions which provide services across 20 locations nationally. These operating divisions are across five business entities, all with a plan to grow.

AURIGA AVIATION
As one of the key operating divisions, Auriga Aviation has earned a strong reputation as an industry leader in the delivery of specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services throughout Australia - operating a modern well-maintained fleet of Airbus helicopters, supporting the maritime industry with safe, efficient and highly specialised aviation solutions. Our services support a diverse customer base and are delivered across multiple coastal and offshore locations nationwide. Safety, operational integrity, and customer satisfaction underpin every aspect of our aviation operation.

CURRENT OPPORTUNITY
Auriga Aviation is currently seeking an experienced Air Transport Operations (ATO) Single-Engine Helicopter Pilot based in Port Hedland WA to join our growing team.

If you're a motivated, self-driven aviation professional with a strong commitment to safety and operational excellence, this opportunity could be ideal for you. You'll enjoy working as part of a collaborative team while also having the confidence and capability to operate independently when required. If this sounds like you, we encourage you to apply.

ATO SEH Helicopter Pilot - Mandatory Qualifications and experience
- Australian Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter)
- Minimum 1000 hours helicopter
- Minimum 500 hours PIC helicopter
- SEH Instrument Rating
- Low Level Helicopter Rating
- Current Class 1 Australian Aviation Medical Certificate

ATO SEH Helicopter Pilot - Desirable Qualifications and experience
- Instrument Rating - SEH/MEH
- Previous maritime experience
