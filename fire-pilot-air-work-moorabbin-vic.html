Fire Pilot (Air Work)-Moorabbin Airport, Melbourne VIC

Moorabbin Airport, Melbourne VIC · Posted 2026-08-21 · 👁 … views

Fire Pilot (Air Work) – Fly. Respond. Make a Difference.

Location: Moorabbin Airport, in Melbourne's South-East
Employment: Seasonal Contract – Summer Fire Season
Permanent Full-Time opportunities available post-season for suitable candidates

The Role
Microflite is seeking experienced Line Pilots to join our operational team for the upcoming summer fire season. This is an opportunity to fly the AS350 in aerial firefighting operations, supporting bushfire suppression and response activities across Victoria and interstate, as well as undertaking other aerial work operations such as dispensing operations, external load, surveillance & reconnaissance (ISR) and aerial shooting.

We are looking for pilots with strong AS350 experience, excellent aircraft handling skills, and sound operational decision making in high workload or time critical environments. Experience in aerial firefighting or other specialist aerial work is essential.

Successful seasonal contractors who demonstrate the required operational capability, professionalism and cultural fit may be considered for permanent full-time employment with Microflite following the fire season. With new contracts secured for 5+ years, Microflite offers long-term stability and genuine career growth for the right candidates.

Why Microflite?
At Microflite, we live by our values: Safety. Integrity. Excellence. Innovation. Engagement.

Join Microflite, an industry leader in helicopter and fixed-wing operations. Our modern, meticulously maintained fleet enables us to deliver critical missions such as firefighting, search and rescue, aerial filming, and surveillance for government clients. Due to continued growth and expansion, this is your chance to be part of a dynamic team that's shaping the skies.

What You'll Do:
- Contribute to a positive, professional and safety-focused operational culture.
- Conduct AS350 aerial firefighting operations.
- Conduct other aerial work and operational tasks as required.
- Operate safely and effectively in challenging terrain, weather and dynamic operational environments.
- Work closely with aircrew, fire agencies and other aviation operators as part of coordinated fire response operations.
- Maintain a strong focus on situational awareness, risk management and operational decision making.

What You'll Bring:
- Professional approach to aviation, with a strong commitment to safety and operational standards.
- CASA CPL(H) or ATPL(H).
- Current CASA Class 1 Medical.
- Australian Citizen or Permanent Resident.
- Minimum 1,500 hrs. PIC
- Significant AS350 PIC experience
- Demonstrated experience in aerial firefighting, firebombing or other demanding aerial work operations.
- Strong AS350 aircraft handling and operational decision-making skills.
- Experience operating in challenging terrain and dynamic operational environments.
- Ability to work effectively as part of a highly experienced operational team.
- Low Level Rating with Sling Load endorsement
- HUET

Desirable Experience
- Experience operating in alpine or mountainous environments.
- Aerial shooting experience, particularly in alpine or mountainous environments.
- Vertical reference experience
- Experience working with emergency services or within coordinated incident response operations.

Career Progression and Development — Grow your career with access to internal training and development initiatives, plus opportunities to advance into new roles as the company continues to expand.

Work with a Leading Name in Australian Aviation — Join a fast growing, highly respected aviation company where your contributions directly support our operational excellence.

Supportive and Inclusive Team Culture — Be part of a friendly and professional workplace where collaboration, respect, and a positive attitude are genuinely valued.

Attractive Remuneration — Competitive pay based on your skills and experience.

If you have the experience, professionalism and operational capability to contribute to a high-performing aerial firefighting team, we'd love to hear from you. Apply now to join Microflite and help shape the future of aviation operations.
