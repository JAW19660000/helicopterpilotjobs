AW169 IFR NVG HEMS/SAR Captain-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2026-01-16 · 👁 … views

Description. The opportunity | Nga whakaritenga o te mahi: We are seeking an experienced emergency service Pilot to join our dedicated team operating in a twin-pilot, IFR, HEMS environment engaged in day and night operations. This is a full-time, permanent position based in Auckland. About you | He korero mou: You will be someone with: Minimum qualifications for our Pilot: ATPL or CPL (Helicopter); Instrument Rating including PBN. Desirable qualifications: AW169 experience; HEMS operational experience; NVG experience; Hoist experience; Multi-engine turbine experience. Required Skills: Excellent interpersonal and communication skills; Highly developed time management skills; IT literate; Comprehensive knowledge of current aviation legislation and Part 119 regulations; Ability to collaborate and deal with change constructively. Main Responsibilities: Carry out duties as a Pilot in accordance with CAA requirements and NRHL's Operations Manual. Ensure all flights are conducted safely and in compliance with regulations and authorisations. Maintain operational readiness and competency checks. Liaise regularly with colleagues, HEMS crew, engineering, and relevant stakeholders. Assist with operational and training requirements. Represent NRHL at occasional promotional or fundraising events to support and promote our activities. About us | He korero tenei mo tatou: Northern Rescue Helicopter Ltd provides a dedicated emergency rescue helicopter and air ambulance service for the Northern region of Aotearoa New Zealand, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. By joining us, you are making a big difference for the people we help and our communities. If you are a highly motivated person with a positive attitude and want to become part of our operations team, apply now! Applications close: 30th January 2026. Applicants must have the right to work and live in New Zealand.
