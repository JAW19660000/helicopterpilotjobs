AS350/H125/UH-60 Fire/Utility Pilots – Australia & International

Australia & International · Posted 2026-07-03 · 👁 … views

Company: ROTORDYNE HELICOPTERS. Location: AUSTRALIA. Type: Permanent/Seasonal. Relocation: Negotiable. Travel: Domestic and International.

Description: Rotordyne Helicopters is accepting applications for utility pilot positions in UH-60 and H125/AS350 Helicopters. We are looking for seasonal and year-round expressions of interest. Pay and benefit package commensurate with experience.

Job Requirements: 5000 PIC; 1000 hours VRLL experience Demonstrated (100ft+); USFS Carded LL and Tank (UH-60); Current 61.58 (UH-60); 100 hours helicopter PIC of operations in mountainous areas (10 hours in make and model); 1000 hours helicopter PIC in weight class (+/-12,500 lbs UH-60); 500 hours PIC in make/model; 100 hours PIC in make/model in last 12 months; 10 seasons experience wildfire/bushfire operations (AUS/USA/Canada); Ability and willingness to pass pre-employment and random drug screenings; Ability to legally work in Australia; Nil accident History.

Company Details: Rotordyne Helicopters, Bankstown Airport.

Apply by email: ops@rotordyne.com.au
