Pilot Required-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2025-10-13 · 👁 … views

Zoom Helicopters is a growing, family owned and operated tourism business based in Cairns, Queensland, providing the top-rated scenic flights over the World Heritage listed Great Barrier Reef and surrounding area. We pride ourselves on being a tight-knit team where safety, customer service, and a positive attitude always come first.

We're looking for our next friendly, professional helicopter pilot to join our team. The ideal candidate will not only have a strong focus on safety, but also a warm, approachable personality – someone who enjoys meeting new people, creating memorable experiences for our customers, and working together as part of a supportive team.

What we're looking for: - Australian citizen or permanent resident - CPL-H - Minimum 700 hours total time - Turbine experience (essential) - Bell 206B/L experience (preferred) - Pontoon/ship landing experience (preferred) - Strong focus on safety and professionalism - A genuine people person with great customer service skills - Positive, can-do attitude and team player - ASIC - HUET certificate - DG Awareness certificate

What we offer: - Full-time position - Above award wage - Fixed base (great for family life) - Be a part of a close-knit, supportive team where your contribution really matters - Opportunity to fly in one of the world's most stunning places

If you're ready for an exciting new adventure with us at Zoom Helicopters, we welcome suitably qualified applicants to send CVs. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted.

Apply via email to brenton.natalier@zoomhelicopters.com.au
