UH60 Black Hawk Pilot (Touring)

Adelaide, SA · Posted 2026-08-14 · 👁 … views

ABOUT THE ROLE
Due to continued growth, Black Hawk pilots are required for aerial firefighting operations beginning in October 2026. These operations involve touring rosters of two to three weeks across various locations. Company arranged connecting flights will be available from any airport with an RPT service and accommodation is provided when away from home.

Key Responsibilities:
- Act as PIC and/or Co-pilot of UH60 Black Hawk helicopter in firefighting operations
- Operate in accordance with Aerotech Helicopters operations manuals, policies and standard operating procedures
- Operate in accordance with National Aerial Firefighting Centre (NAFC) and Fire Agency requirements
- Strive to ensure customer satisfaction and high standards of service are maintained

ABOUT YOU
To be considered for the role you will hold:
- CASA licence with SK70 Type Rating
- 100 Hours on Type
- CASA Class 1 Medical
- Current HUET
- Current CRM
- Sling Endorsement (desirable)
- Aerial Application Fire Rating (desirable)
- Multi Crew Coordination (MCC) certificate (desirable)

ABOUT US
Established in 1968, The Aerotech Group is South Australia's largest privately owned aviation company, with specialised aircraft, innovative thinking and a team of talented people. With 170+ staff and a fleet of 40+ aircraft, Aerotech Group have a well-deserved reputation as an Australian leader in providing aerial solutions, working across several sectors including firefighting, agricultural spraying, mining support and medical evacuations.

Boasting an enviable, relaxed and down to earth culture, you will get to work within a well-established and successful family business, where staff pride themselves on providing exceptional service to their varied clients. Additionally, you will be part of a South Australian success story, working within a team who are passionate about aviation and committed to all that they do.

All candidates must have full working rights within Australia, only candidates with a right to work in Australia will be considered.

Applications close Thursday 27 August 2026 at 4pm.
