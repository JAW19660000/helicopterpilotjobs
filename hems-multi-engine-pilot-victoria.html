HEMS Multi-Engine Pilot-Victoria

Victoria · Posted 2026-06-04 · 👁 … views

Multi-Engine Helicopter Pilot. Full-time fixed-base opportunity. Serve a purpose in our community. Join an experienced team.

About the role: We are inviting expressions of interest from qualified Multi-Engine Helicopter Pilots to join our Aviation & Critical Services team. The available opportunity is to perform the role as an AW139 Line Pilot based at Bendigo, Victoria. Babcock MCS operates at several bases throughout Victoria in support of Ambulance Victoria. As this is a Fixed Base position, successful applicants will be required to reside within 60 minutes of the nominated base. It is also a requirement for the successful applicant to be able to support operational needs across all AV bases.

About you: The successful candidate must satisfy the following contract minimum qualifications and experience: CASA ATPL; Class 1 Medical; Instrument Rating with MEH endorsement and minimum 3 renewals; 2500+ hrs total helicopter; 1500+ hrs PIC helicopter; 500+ hrs PIC MEH, not including ICUS; 250+ hrs instrument flying time; 100+ hrs night PIC; AW139 type rating and 100+ hrs experience on type; NVIS rating; Low-level rating with winch endorsement; Prior EMS/SAR operational experience. Applicants are requested to show their compliance against the above criteria. Type rating or qualification training may be offered to the successful candidate.

Your future at Babcock - what we offer you: We provide an agile work environment where you're able to work onsite and remotely, with a laptop and working-from-home kit if required, plus a compressed week for more flexibility; Our work life integration policy brings together leave entitlements including annual leave, wellbeing leave, paid parental leave, defence reservist leave plus more; A supportive and engaging environment where you can grow your career, supported by our capability framework plus ongoing access to self-led learning modules through LinkedIn Learning and access to study leave; Access to beRewarded, providing endless discounts across retail, health, entertainment, travel and more, plus novated leasing options and corporate shares plan; Wellbeing support through our online program Munch, Mind, Move, Money, access to an employee assistance program, plus two wellbeing days annually from personal leave; Participation in weRecognise, an opportunity to recognise outstanding work in living our principles (Be kind, Collaborate, Be Curious, Think Outcomes, Be Courageous and Own & Deliver).

Who we are: Babcock International Group, the aerospace and defence company, has been trusted to deliver bespoke, highly skilled engineering services for over a century. Across Australia and New Zealand, Babcock has grown to a team of over 1,300 talented people, operating across sectors such as defence, commercial marine, aviation, airports, communications, emergency services and engineering consultancy, including providing emergency medical and search & rescue aviation services through our Aviation & Critical Services business; designing and supporting key systems for submarines and surface ships through our Marine business; providing fleet wide through life support and asset management services for Navy customers; and managing critical assets for a range of sectors through our Land Business.

Please note, should your application progress you may be required to complete pre-employment checks including reference checks, police check, security check, qualifications and right to work checks. Relevant vaccinations may be required to comply with public health orders or customer requirements. Babcock Australasia is a Circle Back Initiative Employer - we commit to respond to every applicant.
