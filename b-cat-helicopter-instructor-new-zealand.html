B Cat Helicopter Instructor-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2025-11-18 · 👁 … views

About the role: Aroha Helicopters Ltd. is seeking an experienced B Cat Helicopter Instructor to join our team in Bay View, Hawkes Bay. This is a full-time position that will play a crucial role in training the next generation of helicopter pilots. As our B Cat Helicopter Instructor, you will be responsible for providing high-quality flight instruction and ensuring our students meet all necessary regulatory and safety standards. What you'll be doing: Conducting theoretical and practical flight training for student pilots, including pre-flight briefings, in-flight instruction, and post-flight debriefings; Monitoring student progress and providing comprehensive feedback to help them develop their skills; Ensuring all flight operations adhere to Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) regulations and Aroha Helicopters' safety protocols; Maintaining accurate records of student progress and training activities; Assisting with the development and implementation of training programs and curricula; Collaborating with the broader Aroha Helicopters team to support the overall success of the business. What we're looking for: A current New Zealand CAA B Category Helicopter Instructor Rating; Extensive experience as a helicopter pilot with Commercial Experience; Excellent instructional skills and the ability to effectively communicate complex aviation concepts; Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities, with a focus on safety; Adaptability and the capacity to work in a dynamic, fast-paced environment; A passion for aviation and a commitment to delivering exceptional training to our students and an amazing experience to our commercial clients. What we offer: At Aroha Helicopters, we are dedicated to fostering a supportive and inclusive work environment. We offer competitive remuneration, comprehensive training and development opportunities, and a range of employee benefits to support your overall wellbeing.
