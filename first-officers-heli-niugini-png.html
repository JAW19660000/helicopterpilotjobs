First Officers (Where Applicable) – Heli Niugini, Papua New Guinea

Papua New Guinea (Port Moresby, Lae, Madang, Kiunga) · Posted 2026-08-05 · 👁 … views

Heli Niugini is Papua New Guinea's leading aviation operator. We are expanding our rotary-wing capability (H145, BK117, Bell 407, Bell 206 operations) and seeking experienced, safety-driven professionals to join our team and support a wide range of operations including mining, oil & gas, utility, seismic, medevac, charter and government services across PNG.

Role: First Officers (Where Applicable). Requirements: CPL with Instrument Rating. Type Rating preferred. Multi-engine & VFR experience. Strong learning attitude. Willing to operate in remote conditions.

All operations are conducted in accordance with CASA PNG regulations including Part 100 (SMS), Part 119 (Air Operator Certification) and Part 136 (Rotorcraft Operations). We are committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance and professionalism.

Base locations: Port Moresby, Lae, Madang, Kiunga, with project locations throughout Papua New Guinea.

To apply, please submit: cover letter, CV, licence copies, references, medical, and the last 3 pages of your logbook. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. All appointments will be made in accordance with CASA PNG Part 136 requirements; successful applicants must hold or be working towards a CASA PNG licence and medical certification.

Apply via email: hr.manager@heliniugini.com or visit www.heliniugini.com/careers
