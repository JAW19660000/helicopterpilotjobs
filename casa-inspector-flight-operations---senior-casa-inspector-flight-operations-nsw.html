CASA Inspector (Flight Operations)/ Senior CASA Inspector (Flight Operations)-NSW

Sydney, NSW · Posted 2026-03-20 · 👁 … views

Ongoing (Permanent). $150,381 to $187,972 plus 15.4% superannuation. Sydney.

The Role: As a member of CASA's Aviation Technical Workforce (ATW), a Flying Operations Inspector (FO1) works under direction and guidance from a Manager and National Manager. The role sits within a team of inspectors and other related staff, who work collaboratively to deliver on CASA's strategic operational priorities and work plans, including the National Oversight Plan (NOP). An FO1 is expected to work reasonable additional hours beyond ordinary hours, which may include weekends, compensated per CASA's enterprise agreement.

The FO1 is responsible for: Exercising decision making, including as a delegate, on routine regulatory service and surveillance activities including flight assessments and oversight of authorisation holders; Working collaboratively with colleagues, sharing knowledge, and managing personal performance in accordance with CASA's values and policies; Actively participating in work programs delivering CASA's strategies including issuing initial approvals, compliance assurance and reporting, regulatory improvement, stakeholder engagement and resolution of safety issues; Working professionally with regulated entities, government departments, the aviation sector and the public; Conducting oversight and education of industry examiners and providing technical input to professional development programs; Identifying non-compliance or offences under civil aviation legislation and presenting findings through high quality reports; Assessing industry information and monitoring external influences to develop compliance strategies; Contributing to a positive health and safety culture at CASA; Positively engaging in and responding to change. In addition, the FO2 is responsible for exercising decision making on complex regulatory service and surveillance activities.

Ideal Candidate - Mandatory: Ability to work independently under guidance from managers; Ability to exercise initiative, analysis, problem solving, judgement and decision-making; Developed negotiation, facilitation, influencing and communication skills; Demonstrated ability to engage in effective stakeholder management including conflict resolution; Ability to identify and assess emerging issues and trends; Ability to seek out diverse perspectives for improvement; Understanding of aviation industry, civil aviation legislation, and relevant standards; Demonstrated knowledge and skill in regulatory assessment. FO2 additionally requires: Ability to work under direction from CASA; Ability to exercise initiative on complex/unusual matters; Well-developed negotiation and communication skills at senior stakeholder level; Experience coaching colleagues in complex environments; In-depth understanding of the aviation industry and legislation.

Desirable: Flight Instructor Rating with a current Pilot Instructor Rating proficiency check or equivalent; Recent experience with a Part 141 or 142 operator including training and checking experience; Auditing experience; An Australian Class C driver's licence.

Eligibility requirements: Pre-employment screening and police record check; Must be an Australian citizen with ability to obtain and maintain required security clearance; Must perform or be available to perform safety sensitive aviation activities; Must pass pre-employment alcohol and drug testing; 8+ years of appropriate experience including holding an Air Transport Pilot's Licence (Fixed Wing) or Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter Category Rating); Current Class 1 Medical certificate; Civil or military equivalent qualifications relevant to the role (flight examiner, instructor rating, training and checking pilot); Supervisory experience in aviation in a key person role (HOFO, HOO, HoTC, or NAA equivalent); Understanding of Australia's Civil Aviation Act 1988 and relevant CASRs/CARs; Hold or be eligible to hold an ASIC.

About Our Division: The Regulatory Oversight Division (ROD) delivers CASA's aviation safety obligations for surveillance and regulatory services, and provides regulatory guidance to support industry understanding of the regulatory framework. For further information, contact Paul Musumeci, +61 2 8651 3034, paul.musumeci@casa.gov.au.
