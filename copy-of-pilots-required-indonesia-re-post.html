Pilots Required-Indonesia (Re-Post)

Indonesia · Posted 2025-09-12 · 👁 … views

B412/212 and B407 Aerial Work Pilots. SGI, an aviation company operating in Indonesia, is currently seeking experienced Rotary Wing Pilots for precision long-line (VRLL) operations on the B412/212 and B407 helicopter type. The successful candidate will be based in Indonesia and will need to meet the following minimum qualifications. Requirement for Rotary Wing Pilots for B407 for precision long-line (VRLL) operations: Current CPL or ATPL License including a Class 1 Medical Certificate; Minimum Flight Time in Command on B407 Helicopter Type - 300; Minimum Total Flight Time - 3,000 hours; Total Minimum Flight Time in Command in Helicopters - 1,500 hours; Minimum Flight Time in Command in Role - min 300 hours (External load operations 200 hours, > 200ft, VRLL 100 hours); High Altitude and Seismic Operations experience preferred; The applicant is subject to passing the Heli SGI's Medical Examination standards; Must have nil violation or accidents in the last five (5) years. Requirement for Rotary Wing Pilots for B412/212 for precision long-line (VRLL) operations: Hold a current CPL or ATPL License, as well as a Class 1 Medical Certificate; Possess a minimum of 2,500 total flight hours, which includes 1,500 hours total as pilot in command, 500 hours Multi-engine and 150 hours total on type; Have a minimum 200 hours of external load operations and 100 hours of VRLL operations; Previous experience in high altitude and seismic operations is preferred; The applicant must pass Heli SGI's Medical Examination standards; No violations or accidents in the last five (5) years are allowed. About Us - Sayap Garuda Indah (SGi): At Sayap Garuda Indah (SGi), we're more than just a helicopter service provider - we're a team of professionals committed to connecting Indonesia's most remote regions through safe, efficient, and reliable aviation solutions. For over a decade, SGi has supported major clients in the mining & exploration industry, tourism sector, and specialized logistics across the archipelago. With over 130+ employees across various functions, SGi continues to grow as one of Indonesia's leading aviation companies. Head Office: Benoa Heliport Complex, Jl. Raya Pelabuhan Benoa, 80222 Pesanggaran, Bali - Indonesia.
