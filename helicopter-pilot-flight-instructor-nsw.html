Helicopter Pilot - Flight Instructor-NSW

NSW · Posted 2026-05-03 · 👁 … views

Company: HV Helicopters. Location: Cessnock Airport, NSW. Type: Fixed Term Contract. Start: Immediate ASAP. Description: Hunter Valley Helicopters is an established company that conducts Flight Training, Passenger Transport and Aerial Work. We believe in high standards, have a premier training facility as well as a strong focus on safety and customer service. Due to a short term vacancy and projected growth we are looking for someone to join our team. The position will be for a minimum of 3 months, with the possibility of continued employment for the right candidate. Requirements: Full Australian living and working rights; CASA CPL(H); 500+ hours (Helicopter); R22 and R44 endorsements; FIR (Helicopter) Grade 2; ASIC; Dangerous Goods by Air Awareness; Australian Drivers License (C Class Manual preferred); Nil accident history preferred; CASA DAMP; Good interpersonal relationship skills; Current CASA Class 1 Medical; A strong work ethic and commitment to working safely. Desirable: A genuine interest in instruction; FIR (H) Grade 1; Low Level Training Endorsement; Design Feature Training Endorsement (GTE); Previous flight experience in Sydney/Newcastle Area; 100+ hours B206.

Enquiries: info@huntervalleyhelicopters.com.au, phone 0488 711 778
