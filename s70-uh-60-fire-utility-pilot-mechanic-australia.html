S70/UH-60 Fire/Utility Pilot/Mechanic – Australia

Australia · Posted 2026-04-30 · 👁 … views

Hawk Helicopters Pty Ltd is a small and highly experienced Helicopter company based in Victoria utilizing the UH-60/S70 Blackhawk for heavy lift precision utility and firefighting operations in Australia. Hawk Helicopters is actively looking to recruit both a qualified and highly motivated Pilot/Mechanic with the following essential requirements for the role.

Pilot/Mechanic requirements: FAA CPL(H) or ATP with UH-60/S70 Type Rating; Current FAA Medical; Current 61.58 Check ride; Current USFS Card; HUET; FAA A&P; FAA IA; No less than 10 years experience with Type 1 Helicopters; Minimum 5 fire seasons in the U.S and Australia; Meet all NAFC requirements; Willing to travel and work in adverse conditions. The position requires travel and the ability to work in a close-knit team environment.

Please only respond if you meet the above requirement, referencing the position you are applying for. Closing date 28th May 26.

Apply by email: admin@hawkhelicopters.com.au
