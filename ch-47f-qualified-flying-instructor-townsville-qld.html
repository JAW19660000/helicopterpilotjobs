CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor-Townsville, Northern QLD

Townsville, Northern QLD · Posted 2026-08-04 · 👁 … views

Boeing Defence Australia is shaping the future of aerospace and delivering some of the nation's most important programs for the Australian Defence Force. Joining us is a chance to make your mark, working with a diverse team that is united in pushing the boundaries of imagination and excellence. We currently employ more than 3,000 people across 14 sites in Australia and multiple international locations, offering a range of opportunities as we plan for future growth.

We are seeking a highly skilled CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor (QFI) to join our Chinook Integrated-Services Support (CISS) contract in Townsville. This position is embedded within the School of Army Aviation (SAA) Chinook Wing, you will play a critical role in the training and readiness of aviation personnel. This position requires a dedicated professional capable of delivering high-level instruction across airborne, synthetic, and ground-based environments while maintaining the highest standards of flight safety and operational excellence.

Responsibilities: As a CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor (QFI), you will: Execute high-quality CH-47F airborne, synthetic, and ground-based instruction to support pilot development; Oversee and conduct CH-47F general flying operations in accordance with established protocols; Proactively maintain all flying currency and professional qualifications as mandated by Army Aviation Orders, Instructions and Publications; Champion a robust safety culture, promoting a secure and effective work environment for all team members; Manage associated instructional and administrative tasks to ensure seamless training workflows; Actively contribute to the evolution of training methodologies and team performance.

Essential Criteria: CH-47F QFI (Category C); CH-47F NVIS Command; Group 4 Command Instrument Rating; CASA CPL-H (or higher); Current Class 1 Medical; CASA ASIC; Ability to obtain and maintain a NV1 Defence security clearance.

Desirable Criteria: CH-47F QFI (Category B/A); Group 4 Instrument Rating Assessor; CH-47F TFPS Instructor Operator Simulator; CH-47F Maintenance Pilot qualification; Simulator Reference Pilot.

Note: This site-specific position is subject to physical requirements. A formal assessment may be required in consultation with the Environment, Health & Safety Department.

Eligibility: Applicants must be Australian citizens to meet Defence security requirements and must be willing to undergo the standard government vetting process, including a full life background check. BDA will sponsor and support the required clearance process.

Benefits: Competitive salary and yearly bonuses; access to various paid leave entitlements to support personal and family needs; discounted health insurance premiums, travel, and retail shopping, along with a nationwide Fitness Passport; options for novated leasing and salary sacrifice for superannuation; tailored mentorship to accelerate career growth and designed promotional pathways; a recognition award program that acknowledges professional achievements through a points and payment scheme.

About the company: With more than 4,500 team members across Boeing Australia, BDA plays a critical role in supporting Australia's security and strengthening its partnership with the ADF through advanced systems integration and defence capability. Our people are central to this success, with their commitment to local partnerships and community engagement reinforcing BDA's reputation as a trusted and valued contributor. We empower all voices and embrace change, so help us be part of that change, and better the future of the nation. We are committed to building a unique and inclusive workplace. Female applicants, people of Aboriginal or Torres Strait Island descent and ex-defence personnel are encouraged to apply.
