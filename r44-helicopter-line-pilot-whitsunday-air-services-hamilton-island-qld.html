R44 Helicopter Line Pilot-Hamilton Island, QLD

Hamilton Island, Northern QLD · Posted 2026-10-13 · 👁 … views

Job Title: R44 Helicopter Line Pilot
Full time · Hamilton Island, Northern QLD

About the company
Whitsunday Air Services is a helicopter, seaplane and fixed wing aircraft tour and transfer operator servicing the Whitsundays, Great Barrier Reef and Hamilton Island area, providing luxury tours such as scenic flights, helicopter transfers, reef cruises and snorkelling tours including “Journey to The Heart”.

About the role
A full-time R44 Helicopter Line Pilot position requiring the candidate to reside on Hamilton Island. Reporting to the Deputy HOFO, the role supports the day to day operations of the business, providing safe, professional and compliant line pilot operations as per the company operations manual, SOPs, aircraft flight manual, AIP, ERSA and CASA rules and regulations.

Minimum requirements
350 hours PIC helicopter
10 hours PIC R44
Current HUET course
Current DG
Current ASIC
Current DAMP test
Australian citizen or resident
Satisfactory target score in a Pre-Employment Compass Pilot Aptitude Test
Nil incidents/accidents in the last 5 years

Desirable
Over water and float experience
Turbine endorsement
Preference to applicants with a hospitality/tourism and/or customer service background.

What’s in it for you
Salary sacrifice options, supportive team culture, generous staff discounts, and the chance to work and live in the beautiful Whitsundays. Only applicants who meet the minimum requirements can be considered; in the event of high volume only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
