R44 Helicopter Line Pilot-Whitsunday Air Services Hamilton Island QLD

Hamilton Island, QLD · Posted 2026-11-16 · 👁 … views

Whitsunday Air Services is a helicopter, seaplane and fixed wing aircraft tour and transfer operator servicing the Whitsundays, Great Barrier Reef and Hamilton Island area. The company provides luxury tours such as scenic flights, helicopter transfers, reef cruises and snorkelling tours, including the world famous “Journey to The Heart”.

The role
A full time R44 Helicopter Line Pilot position requiring the candidate to reside on Hamilton Island. Reporting to the Deputy HOFO, the role supports the day to day operations of the business. Your primary responsibility is to provide safe, professional and compliant line pilot operations as per the company operations manual, company SOPs, Aircraft Flight Manual, AIP, ERSA and the CASA Rules & Regulations.

Minimum requirements
- 350 hours PIC helicopter
- 10 hours PIC R44
- Current HUET course
- Current DG
- Current ASIC
- Current DAMP test
- Australian citizen or resident
- A satisfactory target score in a Pre-Employment Compass Pilot Aptitude Test
- Nil incidents/accidents in the last 5 years

Desirable
- Over water and float experience
- Turbine endorsement
- Preference given to applicants with a hospitality/tourism and/or customer service background

What’s on offer
- Access to salary sacrifice options
- Supportive team culture
- Generous staff discounts
- The chance to work and live in the beautiful Whitsundays

How to apply
Apply via the SEEK listing and send a copy of your cover letter and resume. Only applicants who meet the minimum requirements can be considered, and in the event of high volume only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
