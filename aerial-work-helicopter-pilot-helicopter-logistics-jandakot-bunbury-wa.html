Aerial Work Helicopter Pilot-Jandakot/Bunbury WA

Jandakot / Bunbury, WA · Posted 2026-11-04 · 👁 … views

Helicopter Logistics is seeking expressions of interest from suitably qualified helicopter pilots to fill a casual/seasonal position based at either Jandakot or Bunbury, Western Australia. Immediate start for a period of 5 months.

Helicopter Logistics is an experienced provider of Aerial Work services operating both fixed wing and rotary aircraft. Primary contracts support land management projects and Aerial Bushfire Operations for the Dept of Biodiversity, Conservation and Attractions.

Duties
Air Attack Pilot providing aerial support and communications to assist with fire management.

Essential requirements
- Australian CPL(H)
- Australian Class 1 medical
- Australian citizen or permanent resident
- Minimum 1,500 hours aeronautical experience
- Minimum 1,000 hours as Pilot in Command
- Excellent communication skills
- Low Level rating with operational experience

Desirable
- Experience with Part 138 Aerial Work operations
- Experience with AS350 series helicopters
- Previous aerial fire operations experience
- Sling rating and experience

How to apply
Email a current CV with a cover letter addressing the essential and desirable requirements to caroline.clarke@resourcegroup.com.au. Source listing: AFAP.

Apply: caroline.clarke@resourcegroup.com.au (CV and cover letter addressing the requirements)
