Mi 171 Pilot Required-Indonesia

Indonesia · Posted 2026-04-11 · 👁 … views

Job Title: Captain Mi 171. Department: OPERATIONS. Reporting to: CHIEF PILOT ROTARY WING. Location: Within Indonesia. Principal Accountabilities: Provide piloting services on the Mi-171 helicopter to ensure the safe and efficient execution of charter assignments. Assess, monitor, and provide feedback on the performance of the Mi-171 to enable engineering personnel to troubleshoot and rectify engine faults, thereby achieving optimal aircraft performance. Demonstrate the capability and readiness to serve as Pilot-In-Command across the full spectrum of utility helicopter operations, including but not limited to passenger and cargo transport, medical evacuation (medevac), precision lifting using longline, and other specialized missions in diverse and challenging environments. Professional Requirements: Graduate of an accredited aviation school; Hold a valid ATPL Instrument rating; Hold a current type rating for Mi-171; Minimum total flight hours: 3,000 hours; Minimum total hours as Pilot-In-Command (PIC): 2,500 hours; Minimum PIC multi-engine time: 1,000 hours; Minimum time on type (Mi-171): 1,000 hours; Minimum external load hours: 200 hours; Minimum horizontal reference external load hours: 200 hours; Minimum vertical reference long line hours: 100 hours; Possession of TRI/TRE qualification and experience is preferred; Hold a current Class 1 Medical Certificate; Hold a current ICAO English Proficiency Level 4 or higher; Possess thorough knowledge of DGCA CASR regulations; Maintain a valid license, passport, and medical certification; Willing to be assigned to remote areas with minimal facilities. Non Technical Requirements: Non-smoker; Ability to manage professional relationships and work effectively in diverse environments; Capability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team; Willingness to work in remote locations; Successful completion of a medical health examination.
