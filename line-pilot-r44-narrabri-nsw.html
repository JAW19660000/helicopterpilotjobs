R44 Helicopter Pilot
Location: Narrabri NSW
Company: Northwest Helicopters
Position Type: Full-Time/Contract
Start Date: October 2026

About Us
Northwest Helicopters is a growing operator providing aerial services including feral animal control, charter and low-level survey work. We value precision, safety, and professionalism. As we expand, we are looking for a dedicated, safety-conscious and vibrant R44 Helicopter Pilot to join our team.

The Role
This position involves a variety of operational flying, including:
- Low-level aerial survey
- Feral animal control (shooting and flying)
- Charter including scenic flights
- Maintain aircraft and hanger duties
- General R44 operations in regional and remote areas

This is a full-time role with most flying conducted locally, so you'll be home most nights

Minimum Requirements
- Valid Commercial Pilot Licence – Helicopter (CPL-H)
- R44 Type Rating
- 500 TT + hrs minimum for this position
- Demonstrated low-level flight experience
- Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Completed Dangerous Goods (DG) awareness course
- Valid First Aid certificate
- Valid ASIC Card
- Current DAMP Test
- Nil accident history
- Valid manual driver's licence
- Well developed communication skills and situational awareness
- Ability to work independently and in remote field conditions

Desirable Skills/Experience
- Low level flying eg; aerial culling, mustering, surveys.
- Firearms licence/Firearms Experience
- Basic mechanical knowledge/helicopter servicing Eg; 50 hr Maintenance Authority
- Positive attitude and a strong commitment to safety and teamwork
- Willingness to assist with ground operations when required
- Need to be able to work independently and be always mindful of the companies best interests.
- Female applicants encouraged

We Offer
- Industry-competitive salary with room for growth
- A safety-first culture and supportive team
- Exposure to varied operational flying and real-world experience
- Based in Narrabri, great community with modern facilities.

How to Apply
To apply, please submit your resume, cover letter, and flight hours breakdown
