Flight Instructor / Line Pilot-Moorabbin Airport, Melbourne VIC

Moorabbin Airport, Melbourne VIC · Posted 2026-08-21 · 👁 … views

Flight Instructor/ Line Pilot – Develop Pilots. Drive Excellence.

Location: Moorabbin Airport, in Melbourne's South-East
Full Time, Permanent

Microflite is seeking an experienced, motivated and personable CASA Flight Instructor / Line Pilot to join our team.

This is a diverse flying role combining flight instruction with operational line flying, including firefighting and a range of specialist aerial work operations. The successful candidate will have a strong operational background, excellent flying skills and a genuine interest in developing the capability of other pilots.

As an approved Airbus Training Centre, Microflite delivers advanced internal and external flight training across a diverse range of aviation operations. Our line operations include firefighting, aerial work, surveillance and other specialist helicopter operations.

We are particularly interested in pilots with aerial shooting experience in alpine or mountainous environments, as well as experience in firefighting or other demanding aerial work operations.

Why Microflite?
At Microflite, we live by our values: Safety. Integrity. Excellence. Innovation. Engagement.

Join Microflite, an industry leader in helicopter and fixed-wing operations. Our modern, meticulously maintained fleet enables us to deliver critical missions such as firefighting, search and rescue, aerial filming, and surveillance for government clients. Due to continued growth and expansion, this is your chance to be part of a dynamic team that's shaping the skies.

What You'll Do:
- Conduct flight training and checking across a range of rotary-wing operations for internal and external candidates.
- Conduct operational line flying, including firefighting and other specialised aerial work tasks.
- Apply your operational experience to support the development, mentoring and assessment of pilots across Microflite's operational fleet.
- Provide leadership and support to pilots, fostering a culture of safety, professionalism and operational excellence.
- Support the development, review and implementation of flight training programs, operational procedures and training standards.
- Maintain currency and proficiency across Microflite's operational activities and contribute to the continued development of operational capability.
- Ensure training and operational activities are conducted in accordance with applicable CASA regulations, company procedures and organisational standards.

What You'll Bring:
- Grade 2 Flight Instructor Rating
- Australian Citizen or Permanent Resident.
- Minimum 2000 PIC hours, with extensive operational helicopter experience and demonstrated flight training experience.
- Strong operational background in firefighting, aerial work or other specialised helicopter operations.
- Strong leadership, communication and mentoring skills, with a professional and safety-focused approach.
- Low Level Rating with Sling Load endorsement.

Desirable Experience
- Aerial shooting experience, particularly in alpine or mountainous environments.
- Aerial Application Rating and Instructor Endorsement – Helicopter Firefighting
- CASA Flight Examiner Approval
- Experience operating in challenging terrain and demanding operational environments.
- Multi-engine helicopter qualifications and experience.

What We Offer:
Employee Wellbeing Comes First — Access our confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP), plus the day-to-day support of a leadership team that prioritises staff wellbeing and work-life balance.

Career Progression and Development — Grow your career with access to internal training and development initiatives, plus opportunities to advance into new roles as the company continues to expand.

Work with a Leading Name in Australian Aviation — Join a fast growing, highly respected aviation company where your contributions directly support our operational excellence.

Supportive and Inclusive Team Culture — Be part of a friendly and professional workplace where collaboration, respect, and a positive attitude are genuinely valued.

Attractive Remuneration — Competitive pay based on your skills and experience.

If you're an experienced helicopter instructor who is passionate about developing pilots, maintaining high safety standards and contributing to a progressive aviation organisation, we'd love to hear from you.
