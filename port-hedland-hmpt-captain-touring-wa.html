Port Hedland HMPT Captain - Touring-Port Hedland, WA

Port Hedland, Port Hedland, Karratha & Pilbara WA · Posted 2026-08-24 · 👁 … views

ABOUT AURIGA AVIATION
Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide. With over 140 years of combined operational experience, Auriga Aviation is committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance, and reliability. Our unwavering focus ensures exceptional service delivery across all operations.

Auriga Aviation is seeking a suitably qualified single-pilot HMPT Line Captain to join our Flight Operations team in Port Hedland. The role is touring to Port Hedland.

We are seeking a Captain who demonstrates an exceptional commitment to safety and operational excellence, coupled with a collaborative mindset and the ability to communicate clearly and lead effectively.

Key Responsibilities
- Serve as the Captain for H135 helicopter, ensuring the highest standards of safety and professionalism.
- Conduct marine pilot transfers and various aerial work operations with a focus on safety and professionalism on an equal-time roster.
- Collaborate with our passionate team to deliver outstanding customer experiences.
- Engage in continuous training and development to enhance your skills and advance your career.

Minimum Qualifications and Experience
- 2000 hrs total time Helicopter;
- 1000 hrs PIC Helicopter;
- 100 hrs Offshore/Marine experience;
- 100 hrs EC135
- Instrument Rating MEH with 2D endorsement
- NVIS Rating
- 50 hours NVIS flight time

Desired Qualifications:
- Current HUET, DG Awareness
- Flight Instructor Rating Grade 2

What We Offer:
Join an organisation that continues to experience growth and success across Australia. Supportive and engaging environment where you can develop your aviation career in an environment that promotes professional training and development for aspiring individuals.
