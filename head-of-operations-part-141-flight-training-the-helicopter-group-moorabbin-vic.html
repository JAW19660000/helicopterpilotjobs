Head of Operations (HOO) - Part 141 Flight Training-Moorabbin VIC

Moorabbin, VIC · Posted 2026-11-07 · 👁 … views

The Helicopter Group is seeking an experienced helicopter instructor to join the team as Head of Operations (HOO) for its CASR Part 141 flight training activities, based at Moorabbin, Victoria.

The successful applicant will be responsible for overseeing flight training operations, maintaining instructional standards and ensuring compliance with CASA regulations.

Requirements
- Australian CPL(H)
- Helicopter Flight Instructor Rating with Grade 1 Training Endorsement
- Appropriate qualifications and experience to satisfy CASA’s Part 141 HOO requirements
- Sound knowledge of CASR Parts 61 and 141
- Strong leadership, communication and organisational skills

Previous experience in a senior flight training or operational management position will be highly regarded.

Employment arrangements
Remuneration negotiable based on experience and qualifications.

How to apply
Forward your CV, relevant qualifications and a brief summary of your experience to info@helicoptergroup.com. All enquiries will be treated confidentially.

Apply: info@helicoptergroup.com
