Line Pilot (AW139)-Melbourne VIC

Essendon Airfield, Melbourne VIC · Posted 2026-10-30 · 👁 … views

Victoria Police Air Wing operates single-pilot, multi-engine AW139 NVIS & IFR helicopters on a wide range of tasks including search and rescue, tactical deployment of Special Operations Group personnel, general support to policing and surveillance. The aircraft are fully NVG equipped with fast-rope devices, winch, FLIR and downlink. Pilots take part in winching, fast roping / rappelling, airborne sniper operations, NVG operations and tactical police operations. Based at Essendon Airfield, Melbourne. One full-time, ongoing position; applications welcome from across Australia and internationally.

Rostering
CASA fatigue management conditions. 10 and 12 hour shifts: 12 hour shifts are two day shifts followed by two night shifts; 10 hour shifts average 80 hours per fortnight with 6 rostered rest days.

Salary
$155,601 – $164,913 + superannuation + $57,374 Flying Operations Allowance (depending on experience). Nine weeks of leave each year, Emergency Services Super Fund, flexible work options.

Must have
- Current Commercial Helicopter Pilot Licence
- Command Multi Engine (Helicopter) Instrument rating
- Current Class 1 aviation medical
- Minimum 2,000 helicopter hours with turbine experience
- Minimum 1,500 Pilot in Command hours (helicopter)
- Night Visual Flight Rules (NVFR) rating
- Low Level Rating (LL)

Desirable
- Winch and rappel endorsement with winch operations experience
- NVIS rating or experience
- AW139 type rating and experience
- Police and/or SAR experience
- ATPL(H)

Other requirements
- Australian citizen (or permanent resident / NZ Special Category Visa holder); National Security Vetting required
- Pre-employment checks including fingerprint and misconduct screening
- Join Victoria Police as a sworn police officer after initial training at the Police Academy, Glen Waverley
- Current Victorian drivers licence
- Shift work, intrastate and interstate travel
- Medical and psychological testing for shortlisted applicants

How to apply
Apply through Careers Vic (ref VG/20032778K) or SEEK. Applications are not accepted by email or other platforms. Include your resume and the completed application form attached to the ad. Applications close midnight, Thursday 12 November 2026. Enquiries: Rob Rogel rob.rogel@police.vic.gov.au or 0438 892 042; VPS Recruitment VPSRECRUITMENT@police.vic.gov.au or (03) 8335 6004.
