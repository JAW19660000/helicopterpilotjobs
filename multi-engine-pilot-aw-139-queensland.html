Multi-Engine Pilot (AW 139)-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-02-23 · 👁 … views

Full time, touring opportunity. Serve a purpose in our community. Join an experienced team. About the role: We are seeking expressions of interest from qualified AW139 Helicopter Pilots to join our Aviation & Critical Services team in support of our Queensland Health operation on Horn Island. Babcock MCS conducts medical transport and emergency service operations throughout Australia. As this is a Touring position, successful applicants will conduct these flying operations in a 15 day on / 13 day off roster pattern. Whilst the role is aligned with the QH contract on HID, pilots may be deployed to other Babcock locations to support operations on a needs basis. About you - Essential: AW139 type rating and 100+hrs command on type; CASA CPL(H) (ATPL(H) preferred); Class 1 Medical; Instrument Rating with MEH endorsement and minimum 3 renewals; 2500+ hrs total helicopter; 1500+ hrs PIC helicopter; 1200hrs turbine time helicopter; 100+ hrs night PIC; NVIS rating; Low-level rating with winch endorsement. Desirable: B412 type rating and 100+hrs command on type; H145 type rating and 100+hrs command on type. Your future at Babcock - what we offer you: Agile working policies offering flexible start and finish times, a mix of office and home working, or condensed working weeks; Work life integration policy offering attractive leave entitlements including wellbeing leave, volunteer leave, paid parental leave, defence reservist leave; Career development through internal development programs, self-led learning modules through LinkedIn Learning and access to study leave; beRewarded platform offering discounts across retail, health, entertainment, travel, plus novated leasing options and corporate shares plan; weRecognise program recognising outstanding work in line with company Principles. Who we are: Babcock Australasia combines the best talent and in-country expertise while leveraging the rich 130+ year global heritage of Babcock International Group, a FTSE 100 international defence company. Our team of 1,800+ experienced staff ensure critical services and assets are readily available, affordable, and future proof. We welcome applications from candidates from all backgrounds and experience; however, due to the defence orientation of this role, the successful candidate must be eligible to obtain an appropriate level of Commonwealth Personnel Security Clearance, as defined in the AGSVA guidelines. This position requires working with, or exposure to, US and AU export controlled technology; compliance with the US International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) and the AU Defence Trade Controls Act 2012 will be assessed. Babcock Australasia is a Circle Back Initiative Employer - we commit to respond to every applicant.

Apply via SEEK or Babcock website: https://www.seek.com.au/job/90489679 or https://jobs.babcockinternational.com/Babcock/job/Adelaide-Multi-Engine-Pilot-SA-5000/1366593233/
