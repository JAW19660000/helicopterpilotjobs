Helicopter (Rotary Wing) Pilot - IFR Desirable-Greymouth NZ

Greymouth, West Coast NZ · Posted 2026-10-29 · 👁 … views

GCH Aviation is a family-owned business operating emergency medicine, commercial and scenic helicopter flight services, certified flight training, and fixed wing air ambulance, corporate jet and FBO operations from bases in New Zealand and the South Pacific. It is seeking a Helicopter (Rotary Wing) Pilot for its Greymouth base, with bases also in Christchurch and Nelson. The successful applicant will travel from time to time to cover operations at other bases. The current roster follows the Kelly Shift pattern, staying on base in Greymouth when on duty. Because the Greymouth base is a small, tight-knit team, the successful applicant must reside on the West Coast. Full time.

Requirements
- Helicopter CPL and Class 1 medical
- Multi-engine endorsement
- Clean criminal record to obtain an Airport Identity Card
- At least 2,000 hours rotary wing time as PIC
- Unrestricted night rating with 50 hours night
- H145 type rating, although other similar twin engine ratings considered
- NVG endorsement
- Highly desirable: IFR rating (RW) with at least 100 hours IFR time

Desirable but not essential
- Helicopter ATPL
- Previous HEMS experience
- Previous New Zealand South Island flying experience
- 20 hours rotary PIC IMC
- Winch endorsement
- Instructor endorsement with ME
- Flight examiner endorsement with ME
- Unrestricted drivers licence

About you
Self-motivated, a quick learner and an excellent communicator, with initiative, flexibility and a team-first attitude. GCH offers a competitive remuneration package including fully funded Southern Cross Health Insurance.
