Flying Operations Manager - Australia

Australia (Perth, Western Australia) · Posted 2026-05-18 · 👁 … views

Perth, Western Australia. Permanent, full time. The company: PHI is a global leader in helicopter services. In the Asia Pacific (APAC) region, PHI takes on some of the most challenging assignments in aviation to deliver high quality helicopter support for energy customers and search and rescue authorities. We are one of the safest helicopter operators in the world. Every flight we take is guided by our core values of Safe, Efficient, Quality, Service. About the role: We have an exciting opportunity for a Flying Operations Manager to join our dynamic and growing Flying Operations team. You will be the primary operational liaison between Flying Operations, Operations, Scheduling, Senior Base Pilots and other internal stakeholders. Reporting to the Head of Flying Operations, you will play a pivotal role in providing support to assist with decision-making and ensure consistent implementation. Responsibilities of the role include: Operational Management and oversight; Support Workforce planning & Personnel management; Crew member administration and training coordination (Part 133/138/141/142); Provide support to the Senior Base Pilots in Australia; Act as the liaison and coordinator between flying operations and other departments; Provide support to ensure regulatory compliance; Participate in internal and external audits; Provide administration support to the flying operations management team; Support the application of the company FRMS by providing roster-risk inputs, fatigue risk assessment support and trend monitoring (e.g., using Air Maestro), in coordination with Scheduling. To be considered for this role, you will need: Demonstrated leadership or coordination experience within rotary-wing operations; Proven experience in operational personnel management, training coordination, scheduling, compliance tracking and stakeholder liaison in a regulated environment; Working knowledge of CASA operational frameworks relevant to the operator (Part 91/119/133/138/141/142) and/or international offshore flying regulations; Experience working within FRMS frameworks and roster risk considerations; High competence with administrative systems, document control and reporting. About you: Works cooperatively with others; Embraces change; Sets and communicates clear, measurable standards; Expresses ideas respectfully; Excellent written and verbal communication skills. By joining our team, you will work in a rewarding and professional environment, with a diverse team of people sharing ideas and experiences. If this position sounds right for you, please register through our PHI Career Centre and follow the steps to complete an application. PHI Aviation is an equal opportunity employer and encourages applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islanders. Defence force experience is desirable, and veterans are encouraged to apply. We respectfully request no agency submissions. Applications close Wednesday, 17 June 2026. PHI reserves the right to commence shortlisting and remove this job advertisement prior to this date.
