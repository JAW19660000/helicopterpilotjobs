Flight Training Manager-Brisbane QLD

Brisbane QLD · Posted 2026-09-04 · 👁 … views

About LifeFlight
LifeFlight Australia is a leading aeromedical organisation dedicated to saving lives through rapid response medical services. Operating a fleet of helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, LifeFlight delivers critical care and emergency retrievals across Queensland and beyond. In addition to its lifesaving missions, LifeFlight runs a world-class training academy, equipping aviation and medical professionals with the skills needed for high-pressure environments.

About the Role
LifeFlight is seeking a Flight Training Manager to join the LifeFlight Training Academy, currently based in the Brisbane Airport precinct. This is a central role responsible for planning, coordinating and overseeing contracted aviation training and checking delivered under LifeFlight's Part 142 organisation. You'll ensure training is delivered safely, efficiently and in compliance with regulatory, operational and contractual requirements.

Working closely with Flight Operations, Rostering, Engineering & Maintenance, instructors, examiners, simulator providers and external training organisations, you'll provide the strategic oversight and coordination that enables our training teams to focus on delivering exceptional training outcomes.

In this role, you will:
- Lead the planning and delivery of Part 142 training and checking programs, coordinating schedules, instructors, examiners, aircraft and simulator resources while proactively managing competing operational priorities
- Maintain training compliance by monitoring currency, qualifications and completion records within Air Maestro and other systems
- Coordinate training partnerships by managing simulator bookings, liaising with external providers and building strong relationships with operational leaders, instructors and examiners
- Drive continuous improvement by analysing training outcomes, identifying trends and risks, supporting audits and enhancing training systems
- Support operational growth by planning training requirements for new contracts and contributing to manuals, SOPs, safety initiatives and operational projects
- Provide trusted advice and reporting on training planning, regulatory requirements, compliance and organisational readiness

This is a permanent, full-time opportunity based at the LifeFlight Training Academy in Brisbane, with a planned transition to our new purpose-built LifeFlight Training Academy on the Gold Coast in 2028.

About You
You're an organised and collaborative aviation training professional who enjoys bringing people, resources and planning together to deliver successful training outcomes.

You'll bring:
- Experience coordinating complex aviation training programs, schedules and resources
- Strong organisational, communication and stakeholder management skills
- The ability to manage competing priorities while maintaining exceptional attention to detail
- Experience developing, implementing or overseeing training systems, reporting or learning programs
- A collaborative approach and the ability to build effective relationships across operational, training and compliance teams

Highly regarded:
- Experience managing aviation training within a regulated environment, particularly CASA Part 142 or Part 141 organisations
- Strong knowledge of CASA regulations, aviation training compliance and operational standards
- Experience using Air Maestro or similar training management systems
- Experience coordinating simulator operations or working with external training providers
- Experience supporting aviation contract mobilisation, Flight Examiners, Instructors and training partners
- A background in quality assurance, continuous improvement or audit activities

This position is only open to Australian Citizens, Permanent Residents, or those with unrestricted work rights in Australia. Background checks including a national police check are required.

Applications close on Thursday, 17 September 2026.
