BK117/H145 SPIFR NVG HEMS/SAR Pilot – New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2026-05-20 · 👁 … views

EMS Pilot – Taieri Base. Otago, Taieri Airfield, Dunedin, New Zealand. Full Time, HEMS / Air Ambulance, Pilot. To be negotiated. Start: Wednesday, 1st of July 2026. Application Closes: Friday, 19th of June 2026.

EMS Pilot – Helicopters Otago, Taieri Base. Helicopters Otago is seeking an experienced and motivated EMS Pilot to join our Taieri-based air ambulance crew. Operating in some of New Zealand's most dynamic and challenging environments, our service provides critical aeromedical response across the lower South Island. We are looking for a professional pilot who thrives in a high-performance team environment and is committed to operational excellence, and continuous improvement.

About the Role: As an EMS Pilot, you will: Conduct air ambulance and rescue missions across mountainous, coastal, and remote terrain; Operate in a 4 on 4 off roster environment; Work closely with management, paramedics and other crew; Maintain the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and decision-making; Participate in training, development, and other tasks as necessary; Carry out pilot-maintenance on helicopters and equipment; Maintain our facilities and gear to the highest standards. You will be required to live within a reasonable distance from the base in order to meet requirements for night mission dispatch times.

Minimum Requirements: CPL(H) or ATPL(H) with current Class 1 Medical; Minimum 3,000 hours total helicopter time; Minimum 2,000 hours PIC helicopter; Multi-engine experience; Unrestricted night & NVG ratings; Instrument Rating (Helicopter) – SPIFR; Mountain flying experience; Excellent CRM and communication skills; Eligibility to live and work in New Zealand.

Highly Regarded: EMS and SAR experience; BK117 / EC145 / H145 ratings & experience; Previous single-pilot IFR operations.

What We Offer: Competitive remuneration; Structured roster; Modern, well-equipped aircraft; Advanced training systems including simulator access; Supportive, high-performing team culture; The opportunity to operate in one of the most spectacular flying environments in the world.

If you are a professional, safety-focused pilot ready to contribute to a high-performing aeromedical team, we would like to hear from you. Applications including CV and cover letter should be submitted to: andy@heliotago.co.nz. Applications close: 12th June.

Apply by email: andy@heliotago.co.nz
