Multi-Engine Pilot (AW139, Touring)-Horn Island QLD

Horn Island, QLD · Posted 2026-11-05 · 👁 … views

Babcock Mission Critical Services is seeking qualified AW139 helicopter pilots to join its Aviation & Critical Services team in support of its Queensland Health operation based on Horn Island, Queensland. This is a touring position on a 15 days on / 13 days off roster, supporting aeromedical transport and emergency service operations throughout Far North Queensland and the Torres Strait. Pilots may also be deployed to other Babcock locations as required. Full time.

Essential
- AW139 type rating and 100+ hours command on type
- CASA CPL(H) (ATPL(H) preferred)
- Current Class 1 medical
- Instrument rating with MEH endorsement and minimum three renewals
- Minimum 2,500 hours total helicopter time
- Minimum 1,500 hours PIC helicopter
- Minimum 1,200 hours turbine helicopter time
- Minimum 100 hours night PIC
- NVIS rating
- Low Level rating with winch endorsement
- Comply with contract vaccination requirements

Desirable
- B412 type rating and 100+ hours command on type
- H145 type rating and 100+ hours command on type

What Babcock offers
15/13 touring roster; competitive remuneration and benefits; wellbeing, volunteer, parental and defence reservist leave; learning and development including study assistance; employee discounts and recognition programs. Successful applicants may be required to complete reference, police, qualification and right-to-work checks.
