Helicopter Pilots - Karratha and Port Hedland

Not specified · Posted 2025-12-09 · 👁 … views

ABOUT AURIGA AVIATION Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide.

With over 140 years of combined operational experience, Auriga Aviation is committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance, and reliability. Our unwavering focus ensures exceptional service delivery across all operations.

Auriga Aviation is seeking suitably qualified Helicopter Pilots to join our flight operations team in Karratha and Port Hedland Western Australia. This role will deliver unique opportunities for motivated individuals wishing to accelerate their Helicopter Pilot career.

ROLE OVERVIEW The successful applicants will fly the AS350 in air transport operations in Port Hedland or Karratha, Western Australia. Given the nature of the operation, the role is residential and uniquely suitable for pilots wishing to advance their flying careers through future opportunities as First Officers on the Airbus H135, and future Captain positions.

We are looking for enthusiastic and self-motivated professionals, with a strong focus on safety and customer service. The right candidate will enjoy a supportive team environment, but equally be able to work unsupervised.

QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE PRE-REQUISITES - Australian CPL (H) - 1000 hours helicopters - 500 PIC hours helicopters - Current Class 1 Australian Medical - 100 hours turbine

Desirable Experience/Attributes: - AS350 experience - Maritime and offshore experience - Experience in confined area landings and low-level operations

What Auriga Aviation Offers You Take your career to the next level with multiple opportunities for motivated individuals. Auriga Aviation offers a supportive and engaging environment where you can develop your flying career. Auriga Aviation holds a CASR 141 Training Organisation and CASR 119 Check and Training system and advocates an environment that promotes professional training and development.

Auriga Aviation offers above-award remuneration and employment conditions. Unit accommodation in Port Hedland or Karratha is provided.

Suitably experienced candidates are encouraged to apply as soon as practicable as recruitment processes will begin immediately. At Auriga, we are committed to providing equal opportunities to all our employees. We believe that diversity is a strength, and we encourage all individuals to apply, regardless of their gender, race, religion, or background.
