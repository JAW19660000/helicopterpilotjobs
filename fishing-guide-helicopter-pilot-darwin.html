Fishing Guide/Helicopter Pilot - Darwin

Darwin · Posted 2025-10-09 · 👁 … views

Helifish, part of the Morris Aviation Group is seeking a dynamic pilot/fishing guide to join our team in Darwin NT. This position requires a passion for fishing, exceptional customer service and the ability to withstand the hot and humid conditions the NT is known for on a daily basis.

Applicants must have the following essential attributes:
- Fishing experience
- CPL(H)
- R44 endorsement
- ASIC
- Class 1 Medical
- Dangerous Goods Awareness Certificate
- Have or have the ability to obtain NT Weapons license
- Drivers Licence (manual)

Desirable attributes:
- HUET
- GTE
- B206 endorsement
- Low Level Rating

Remuneration will be in line with the Modern Air Pilots Award. All applicants must have considerable fishing experience to apply for this position. The position is available for an immediate start. This is a great opportunity for a start in the industry.

How to apply: If you would like to be part of our team, forward a current resume and covering letter that addresses the role's essential criteria "LINE PILOT – Helifish" to resumes@nautilusaviation.com.au

Apply via email to resumes@nautilusaviation.com.au
