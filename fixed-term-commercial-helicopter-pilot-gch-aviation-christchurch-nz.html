Fixed Term Commercial Helicopter Pilot (Summer Cover)-Christchurch, NZ

Christchurch Central, Canterbury, New Zealand · Posted 2026-09-16 · 👁 … views

GCH Aviation, owner/operators of Garden City Helicopters, are looking for an experienced Commercial Helicopter Pilot to join our team from October 2026 to March 2027.

GCH Aviation also operates the Nelson Marlborough and Canterbury West Coast Rescue Helicopters, the New Zealand Flying Doctors Service, the GCH Jet Centre (FBO) & Jet Operations, Kaikoura Helicopters and Coastwide Helicopters.

GCH Aviation is proudly family owned, with opportunities to be involved across the wider organisation and contribute in areas of personal interest and expertise. At GCH Aviation, every member of the team contributes to the wider operation, so our pilots are expected to be hands-on and willing to assist wherever required to ensure the smooth and safe running of operations. This includes being willing and available to travel and work at any GCH Aviation base when required.

Remuneration will be discussed with the successful applicant and will reflect skills and experience in line with the company remuneration matrix.

We are looking for a pilot who has experience in:
- VIP Charter
- Part 133 Operations
- Flight Training
- Powerline construction and maintenance support
- Scenic flights and tourism experiences
- Passenger and charter transfers
- Commercial aerial project work and more

We are looking for a helicopter pilot with:
- A current NZCAA Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter)
- Minimum 1000 hours PIC helicopter time preferred
- Experience in, or willingness to undertake, passenger work and scenic flights
- Willingness to assist in office, sales and ground support duties
- Experience flying Cabri, Robinson, AS350, EC130
- A valid NZCAA Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Strong customer service skills, team focus and flexibility
- The ability to pass NZCAA, Aviation Security and Police vetting checks
- Willingness to work shift work across a seven-day roster in accordance with the Company Flight & Duty and Fatigue Management Schemes, including weekends and Public Holidays as well as covering our commercial bases across Wellington, Greymouth and Kaikoura when required

Desirable:
- BK117 Rating
- Instructor Rating
- R44 Rating
- External load experience preferred (200 hours+)
- HUET certification
- Overwater flying experience
- Firefighting, powerline, exploration and drilling experience
- Multi-engine helicopter experience

Please upload your cover letter and CV highlighting your current ratings, flying hours, skills and experience and why you think you'd be right for this role.
