Agricultural Helicopter Pilot-Helicopters Otago Mosgiel NZ

Mosgiel, Otago, NZ · Posted 2026-11-25 · 👁 … views

Helicopters Otago is seeking an agricultural helicopter pilot to join its aviation operation at Mosgiel, Otago, supporting efficient and safe aerial spraying and bucketwork application services. The role involves operating aircraft in agricultural environments and delivering reliable outcomes for clients and farming operations. Full time.

Key responsibilities
- Conduct agricultural aviation flights safely and efficiently
- Apply agricultural products accurately in accordance with operational instructions and aviation regulations
- Follow all safety, compliance, maintenance and environmental procedures

What Helicopters Otago is looking for
- A current Commercial Pilot Licence
- A Grade 1 or Grade 2 Agricultural Rating
- A strong commitment to aviation safety, regulatory compliance and professional operating standards
