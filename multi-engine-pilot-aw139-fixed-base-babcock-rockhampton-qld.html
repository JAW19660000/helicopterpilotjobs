Multi-Engine Pilot (AW139, Fixed Base)-Rockhampton QLD

Rockhampton, QLD · Posted 2026-11-05 · 👁 … views

Babcock Mission Critical Services is seeking applications from qualified multi-engine helicopter pilots to join its Aviation & Critical Services team in support of the Capricorn Helicopter Rescue Service based in Rockhampton, Queensland. This is a fixed base opportunity on the AW139, supporting aeromedical and rescue operations across Central Queensland. Full time.

Essential
- AW139 type rating
- CASA CPL(H) (ATPL(H) preferred)
- Current Class 1 medical
- Instrument rating with MEH endorsement
- NVIS rating
- Low Level rating
- Minimum 2,500 hours total helicopter time
- Minimum 1,500 hours PIC
- Minimum 1,200 hours turbine helicopter time
- Minimum 100 hours night PIC

Desirable
- EMS or SAR experience
- Winch endorsement
- AW139 command experience

What Babcock offers
Competitive remuneration and benefits; wellbeing, volunteer, parental and defence reservist leave; learning and development including study assistance; employee discounts and recognition programs. Successful applicants may be required to complete reference, police, qualification and right-to-work checks.

How to apply
Apply through SEEK (reference SF#77161) using the Apply button.
