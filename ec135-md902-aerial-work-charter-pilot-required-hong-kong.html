EC135/MD902 Aerial Work/Charter Pilot Required-Hong Kong

Hong Kong · Posted 2025-10-16 · 👁 … views

FULL-TIME HELICOPTER / LONG-LINE PILOT POSITION. We are hiring!!! Applicants must meet the following requirements: CPL(H) or ATPL(H) with Class 1 medical; English ICAO level 5 or above; 3000 hours (PIC) total helicopter time; 500 hours multi-engines helicopter time; 1000 hours of vertical reference experience with longlines of 100'-200' including precision work like core drill placement and/or in proximity of overhead powerlines. Applicants should have: The ability to work independently and in a multi-cultural environment; A strong emphasis on safety; A customer service mindset; Professional attitude. Considered an asset if applicants have: Previous check and training experience; Experience on MD902, EC135 or similar type; Overhead powerlines aerial work experience; Night rating and flying experience.

Apply via email: hr@heliservices.com.hk (send full resume)
