Helicopter Pilot - H135 Captain - HMPT - Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-06-22 · 👁 … views

About Auriga Aviation: Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide. With over 140 years of combined operational experience, Auriga Aviation is committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance, and reliability. Our unwavering focus ensures exceptional service delivery across all operations.

Auriga Aviation is seeking a suitably qualified H135 Captain to join our Flight Operations team in Gladstone, on a fixed-term basis, until July 2027. This role will deliver unique opportunities for motivated individuals wishing to accelerate their Helicopter Pilot career. We are seeking a Captain who demonstrates an exceptional commitment to safety and operational excellence, coupled with a collaborative mindset and the ability to communicate clearly and lead effectively.

Key Responsibilities: Serve as the Captain for H135 helicopter, ensuring the highest standards of safety and professionalism; Conduct marine pilot transfers and various aerial work operations with a focus on safety and professionalism on an equal-time roster; Collaborate with our passionate team to deliver outstanding customer experiences; Engage in continuous training and development to enhance your skills and advance your career.

Minimum Qualifications and Experience: Valid CASA Part 61 Commercial Pilots Licence (Helicopter) with a type rating on H135 aircraft; 2,500 hours helicopter; 1,500 hours Command Helicopter; 500 hours PIC Multi-engine Helicopters; 100 hours PIC EC135; Instrument Rating Multi-engine Helicopter (IR MEH).

Desired Qualifications: NVIS Rating; Low Level Rating; Prior offshore vessel landing experience; Prior Airbus Helionix experience.

What We Offer: Take your career to the next level with multiple opportunities for motivated individuals; Join an organisation that continues to experience growth and success across Australia; Supportive and engaging environment where you can develop your aviation career; Competitive remuneration and employment conditions that provide a work life balance.

Ready to Take Flight? If you're ready to join a dynamic team and embark on an exciting journey we want to hear from you. Recruitment to this position will commence immediately. Auriga are committed to providing equal opportunities to all our employees; we believe diversity is a strength and encourage all individuals to apply regardless of gender, race, religion, or background.
