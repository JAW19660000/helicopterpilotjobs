Pilot Required-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2025-08-22 · 👁 … views

Join Our Mission-Driven Aviation Team – Full-Time Pilot Wanted! Since 1976, Surf Life Saving Queensland (SLSQ) Aviation has been a vital part of protecting and serving communities across South East Queensland. Operating a fleet of EC135 and Bell 429 helicopters from the Gold Coast to the Sunshine Coast—our mission goes beyond flying. We deliver comprehensive aviation solutions, including training, assessment, and engineering support, while championing a culture of safety, education, and empowerment.

We're looking for a full-time pilot to join our close-knit, purpose-driven team based at our Gold Coast hangar but will be required to travel to our other bases on occasion. This position will be working 40 hours on a 4 days on, 4 days off roster.

Why SLSQ? - Contribute to an organisation that saves lives! - Increase your take home pay with Salary Packaging - Paid parental leave available - Employee assistance program EAP for health and wellbeing - Onsite parking

What You'll Do: - Piloting EC135 and Bell 429 helicopters in both VFR and IFR operations, including NVIS - Participating in regular rosters with standby periods - Being an active contributor to our Safety Management System (SMS) - Helping to shape and refine our Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) - Delivering excellence and professionalism with every mission to ensure exceptional service and customer satisfaction

About You: Required Qualifications & Experience: - Australian Commercial Helicopter Pilot Licence - Single Pilot Class 1 Medical Certificate - 2000+ total helicopter flight hours - 1500+ command helicopter hours - 1000+ turbine helicopter hours - 200+ night helicopter hours - NVIS rating with 3 renewals - IFR rating with 3 renewals - Low level rating

Highly Desirable Extras: - EC135 type rating - Bell 429 type rating with 100 hours - Winch rating - Strong technical knowledge and troubleshooting ability - Professional, personable, and a collaborative team player - Discretion and professionalism on social media - Calm under pressure with a positive, can-do attitude and excellent communication skills—both written and verbal.

About SLSQ: Surf Life Saving Queensland (SLSQ) is a vibrant organisation dedicated to saving lives and promoting beach safety across Queensland. Established in 1930, SLSQ supports over 36,000 members across 57 surf lifesaving clubs. With more than 600 employees, SLSQ delivers lifesaving services, community education, surf sports, and training programs that engage over 462,000 people.

Surf Life Saving Queensland (SLSQ) is committed to creating a fair, inclusive, and representative organisation. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds, including women, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, culturally and linguistically diverse individuals, people who identify as LGBTQIA+ and people with disability. If you need assistance or adjustments to fully participate in the application process, please submit the accessibility form linked on the ad.

Ready to Fly With Purpose? Apply now with a resume and cover letter, to take your aviation career to new heights—while helping us keep Queensland safe from the sky.
