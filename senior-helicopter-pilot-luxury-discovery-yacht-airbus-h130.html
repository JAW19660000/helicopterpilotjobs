Senior Helicopter Pilot – Luxury Discovery Yacht (Airbus H130)

Not specified · Posted 2026-03-04 · 👁 … views

Location: Global Operations
Operating Regions: Australia, New Zealand, Europe, USA, Canada, Greenland, Antarctica
Aircraft: 2 × Airbus H130
Contract Type: Rotational / Permanent
Start Date: TBA

Overview
A prestigious luxury discovery yacht is seeking an experienced Senior Helicopter Pilot to manage and operate two Airbus H130 helicopters in support of worldwide expedition and guest operations. This role combines elite aviation standards with high-end yachting, operating in some of the most remote and environmentally challenging regions on Earth. The successful candidate will demonstrate exceptional airmanship, leadership, and adaptability, with proven experience in maritime and expedition-style helicopter operations.

Key Responsibilities
- Act as Pilot-in-Command for Airbus H130 operations from a yacht-based helideck
- Conduct passenger transport, scenic flights, reconnaissance, logistics, and expedition support
- Operate safely in remote, polar, mountainous, and maritime environments
- Manage helicopter operations across multiple jurisdictions and regulatory frameworks
- Ensure compliance with aviation authorities (CASA, FAA, EASA, TC, etc.)
- Oversee aircraft maintenance coordination with engineers
- Lead flight planning, risk assessments, weather analysis, and fuel management
- Maintain meticulous operational, technical, and safety documentation
- Uphold the highest standards of guest experience, discretion, and professionalism
- Mentor or supervise secondary pilots when required

Required Qualifications & Experience
- Valid ATPL(H) or CPL(H)
- Airbus H130 type rating with a minimum of 100 hrs
- Minimum 3,000–4,000 total helicopter flight hours (flexible for exceptional candidates)
- Demonstrated experience in: Maritime / shipborne helicopter operations; Remote and expedition flying; Cold weather and/or polar operations (highly desirable)
- Proven offshore deck landing experience
- Strong CRM, decision-making, and leadership skills
- Valid Class 1 Medical

Highly Desirable
- Previous yacht or superyacht aviation experience
- Experience operating in Antarctica or Arctic regions
- Instructor or examiner rating

Personal Attributes
- Calm and decisive under pressure
- Exceptional situational awareness
- Professional, discreet, and guest-focused
- Adaptable to extended periods in remote locations
- Strong communicator within multicultural teams

Compensation & Benefits
- Highly competitive salary commensurate with experience
- Rotational schedule
- All travel, accommodation, and onboard living expenses covered
- Opportunity to operate in some of the world's most extraordinary destinations
- Long-term career potential within an elite expedition program

Application Process
Qualified candidates are invited to submit the following documentation: CV/Resume, Summary of flight hours by aircraft type, Two professional references. All applications will be treated with strict confidentiality.
