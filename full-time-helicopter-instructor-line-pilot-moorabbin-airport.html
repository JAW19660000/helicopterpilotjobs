Full-Time Helicopter Instructor/Line Pilot - Moorabbin Airport

Moorabbin Airport · Posted 2025-12-11 · 👁 … views

The Helicopter Group has an exciting opportunity for a Full Time Grade 3 Instructor/Line Pilot to join our organisation at Moorabbin Airport Victoria. Established in 1994 our fleet includes H130, H125/AS350, B407, B206, R44 and H300. Our clientele consists primarily of flight training and charter/aerial work.

Required Skills & Qualifications Minimum Qualifications: - CASA Part 61 CPL(H) license - Grade 3 Instructor Rating - Class 1 Medical - Current ASIC & Dangerous Goods - Proficiency in Part 141 legislation - Current legal right to work in Australia

Desirable Qualifications: - Turbine experience - Grade 1 or 2 Instructor Rating - H300 experience - R44, B206, B407, H130, H125/AS350 - Gas Turbine Endorsement - Low Level Training Endorsement - Design Feature Training Endorsement

Remuneration Salary will be at or above award based on experience and qualifications.

Note: Please do not apply if you do not already hold a Flight Instructor Rating.

Apply via email to info@helicoptergroup.com
