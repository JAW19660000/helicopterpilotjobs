HOFO Deputy Head of Flying Operations-Brisbane QLD

Brisbane QLD · Posted 2026-09-18 · 👁 … views

Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide. With over 140 years of combined operational experience, Auriga Aviation is committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance, and reliability.

We're looking for an experienced and motivated Deputy Head of Flight Operations (D/HOFO) to join our team. In this critical role, you'll support our Head of Flight Operations to establish scalable foundations, drive operational excellence, and foster a positive culture across our organisation.

The Opportunity
As Deputy HOFO, you will support a national operation, working closely with the HOFO and cross-functional teams to ensure the safe, compliant, and efficient conduct of flight operations. You'll help ensure compliance of manuals and documentation in addition to establishing systems and processes that support sustainable growth, focusing on continuous improvement and collaboration.

We are looking for an established or aspiring leader with a deep understanding of helicopter flight operations and a passion for building robust, scalable processes. The ideal candidate has proven experience in a managerial role within the aviation industry, with strong skills in regulatory compliance, safety management, and operational excellence.

Key responsibilities include:
- Conduct of Flight Operations management, including process and procedure review, roster oversight, and technical advice to the SMS
- Conduct of Line Pilot duties
- Conduct of CASR Part 141 Flight Training
- Conduct of Training and Checking duties

Essential:
- Meet the requirements to be appointed as alternate HOFO and alternate Head of Operations (HOO) under CASR Parts 119 and 138 respectively
- Strong working knowledge of CASA regulations as they relate to Air Transport and Aerial Work Helicopter operations
- CPL or ATPL licence (Helicopter)
- Multi-Engine Helicopter Instrument Rating (MEH IR)
- Low Level Rating
- NVIS Rating
- Minimum of 2,500 hours total flight time (minimum 500 Multi Engine PIC)

Desirable:
- Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) experience
- Type Ratings on Airbus H135 and/or H145 helicopters
- Flight Instructor Rating (FIR)
- Flight Examiner Rating (FER)
- Previous appointment as HOFO or HOO in a helicopter operation
- Experience with Air Maestro (or similar) aviation software
- Tertiary qualifications in business, project or aviation management

Apply now with your resume and cover letter outlining your suitability for the role.
