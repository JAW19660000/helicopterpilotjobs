Captain Pilot Bell 412 (B-412)-Indonesia

Indonesia · Posted 2026-07-30 · 👁 … views

Job Title: Captain Pilot Bell 412 (B-412). Department: OPERATIONS. Reporting to: CHIEF PILOT ROTARY WING. Location: Within Indonesia. Principal Accountabilities: Provide piloting services on the Company Helicopter to ensure safe and efficient Helicopter charter assignments; Assess, monitor, and provide feedback on Bell 412 (B-412) performance to enable the Engineers to troubleshoot and repair engine faults and obtain optimum; Provide professional advice to the client on Bell 412 (B-412) aircraft support operations to ensure safe and speedy operations. Professional Requirements: Graduated from Aviation School; Holding License: ICAO ATPL Instrument; Holding Type Rating: B 412 (current); Total Hours: minimum 3000 hours; Total Hours in Command: minimum 1500 hours; Total PIC multi-engine time: minimum 1000 hours; Total on type minimum 1000 hours; External Load: minimum 400 hours; Horizontal Reference External Load: minimum 100 hours; Vertical Reference Long Line: minimum 200 hours; Preferably has TRI/TRE qualification and experience; Class I Medical Certificate (current); Minimum Level 4 ICAO English Proficiency (current); Thorough knowledge of DGCA CASR; Valid license, passport, and medical certification; Willing to be placed in a remote area with minimal facilities. Non Technical Requirements: Manage relationship & diversity; Ability to troubleshoot, work alone, and in a team; Passed medical test; Non smoker; No color blind.
