H135 Corporate/VIP Pilot – Indonesia

Rote, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia · Posted 2026-06-02 · 👁 … views

H135 HELICOPTER PILOT. Company: SGI. Location: Rote, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia. Employment Type: Contract. We are currently seeking a highly skilled and safety-conscious H135 Helicopter Pilot to join our flight operations team. The ideal candidate will be a professional pilot with extensive experience in multi-engine turbine operations and a commitment to maintaining the highest standards of safety and operational excellence. Applicants must demonstrate a professional, courteous, and service-oriented demeanour appropriate for VIP passenger operations. The candidate must have a good safety culture and be able to operate independently. This position will require relocation to Indonesia.

Key Responsibilities: Perform flight operations in accordance with company SOPs, DGCA regulations, and client requirements; Ensure the safe and efficient operation of the H135 helicopter for Luxury Corporate Transport; Maintain accurate flight documentation and mission reporting; Collaborate with ground crews and maintenance teams to ensure the aircraft is mission ready.

Requirements: Valid CPL-H (Commercial Pilot License) or ATPL-H; Current H135 Type Rating, with min 100hrs on type; Minimum 2000 hours total helicopter time; Minimum 500+ hours on multi-engine helicopters; Proven experience in remote area operations; Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate; Proficient in English (ICAO Level 4 minimum); Strong situational awareness, decision-making skills, and ability to operate in challenging environments; Professional, disciplined, and safety oriented attitude.

What We Offer: Competitive salary and benefits package; Opportunity to operate advanced aircraft in a professional and challenging environment; A solid commitment to flight safety and continuous professional development.

How to Apply: If you meet the requirements and are ready to take on this challenge, please send your updated CV, License copies, and Logbook summary to talent.hub@sgi-aero.com, Subject: Application for H135 Pilot – [Full Name].

Apply by email: talent.hub@sgi-aero.com (Subject: Application for H135 Pilot – [Full Name])
