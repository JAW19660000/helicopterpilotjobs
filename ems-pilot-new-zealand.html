EMS Pilot-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2026-02-17 · 👁 … views

EMS Pilot. Queenstown, New Zealand. Full Time, HEMS / Air Ambulance, Pilot. To be negotiated. Immediate. Application Closes: Tuesday, 31st of March 2026. EMS Pilot - Helicopters Otago Queenstown Base: Helicopters Otago is seeking an experienced and motivated EMS Pilot to join our Queenstown-based air ambulance crew. Operating in some of New Zealand's most dynamic and challenging environments, our service provides critical aeromedical response across the lower South Island. We are looking for a professional pilot who thrives in a high-performance team environment and is committed to operational excellence, and continuous improvement. About the Role: As an EMS Pilot, you will: Conduct air ambulance and rescue missions across mountainous, coastal, and remote terrain; Operate in a 4 on 4 off roster environment; Work closely with management, paramedics and other crew; Maintain the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and decision-making; Participate in training, development, and other tasks as necessary; Carry out pilot-maintenance on helicopters and equipment; Maintain our facilities and gear to the highest standards. You will be required to live within a reasonable distance from the base in order to meet requirements for night mission dispatch times. Minimum Requirements: CPL(H) or ATPL (H) with current Class 1 Medical; Minimum 3,000 hours total helicopter time; Minimum 2,000 hours PIC helicopter; Multi-engine experience; Unrestricted night & NVG ratings; Instrument Rating (Helicopter) - SPIFR; Mountain flying experience; Excellent CRM and communication skills; Eligibility to live and work in New Zealand. Highly Regarded: EMS and SAR experience; BK117 / EC145 / H145 ratings & experience; Previous single-pilot IFR operations. What We Offer: Competitive remuneration; Structured roster; Modern, well-equipped aircraft; Advanced training systems including simulator access; Supportive, high-performing team culture; The opportunity to operate in one of the most spectacular flying environments in the world. About Queenstown: Based in Queenstown, this role offers a great lifestyle balance - world-class mountains, lakes, and outdoor recreation at your doorstep, while flying meaningful, lifesaving missions.

Apply via email: andy@heliotago.co.nz (applications close 31st March)
