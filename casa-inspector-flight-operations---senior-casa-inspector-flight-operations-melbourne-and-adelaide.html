CASA Inspector (Flight Operations)/ Senior CASA Inspector (Flight Operations)-Melbourne and Adelaide

Melbourne and Adelaide · Posted 2026-05-01 · 👁 … views

Ongoing (Permanent). $150,381 - $187,972. Melbourne, Adelaide. The Role: CASA is recruiting for Inspector (Flight Operations) / Senior CASA Inspector (Flight Operations) positions, with two vacancies available, one based in Adelaide and one based in Melbourne. Experienced Commercial Pilot Licence holders (Fixed Wing and Rotorcraft) are encouraged to apply. As a member of CASA's Aviation Technical Workforce (ATW), a Flying Operations Inspector (FO1) works under direction and guidance from a Manager and National Manager. The role sits within a team of inspectors and other related staff, who work collaboratively to deliver on CASA's strategic operational priorities and work plans, including the National Oversight Plan (NOP). An FO1 is expected to work reasonable additional hours beyond the ordinary hours of work, which may include work on weekends or outside the span of hours; however, this is regarded as the exception and where additional hours are worked, they will be compensated in accordance with CASA's enterprise agreement. The FO1 is responsible for activities including: Exercise decision making on a range of routine regulatory service and surveillance activities; Work collaboratively with colleagues; Actively participate in work programs delivering CASA's strategies and priorities; Work professionally with regulated entities, government departments, the aviation sector and the public; Conduct oversight and education of industry examiners; Identify non-compliance or offences under civil aviation legislation; Assess and analyse industry information; Contribute to maintaining a positive health and safety culture at CASA; Positively engage in and respond to change. In addition, the FO2 is responsible for exercising decision making on a range of complex regulatory service and surveillance activities. Ideal Candidate: Mandatory - Ability to work independently under guidance from managers; Ability to exercise initiative, analysis, problem solving, judgement, and decision-making; Developed negotiation, facilitation, influencing and communication skills; Demonstrated ability to actively engage in effective stakeholder management; Ability to identify and assess emerging issues and trends; A demonstrated ability to seek out diverse perspectives; Understanding of aviation industry, civil aviation legislation, and relevant national and international aviation standards; Demonstrated knowledge and skill in the conduct of regulatory assessment. In addition, the FO2 ideal candidate must also demonstrate: Ability to work under direction from CASA; Ability to exercise initiative on complex matters; Well-developed negotiation, facilitation, influencing and communication skills; Experience in coaching colleagues; In-depth understanding of the aviation industry, civil aviation legislation. Desirable: Flight Instructor Rating with a current Pilot Instructor Rating proficiency check or equivalent; Recent experience with a Part 141 or 142 operator including training and checking experience; Auditing experience; An Australian Class C driver's licence. Eligibility requirements: prospective CASA employees must undergo pre-employment screening including a police record check; must be an Australian citizen with the ability to obtain and maintain the required level of security clearance; perform or be available to perform safety sensitive aviation activities; undertake and pass a pre-employment alcohol and drug test; 8+ years of appropriate experience including holding a Commercial Pilot Licence (Fixed Wing or Helicopter Category Rating); Hold a current class 1 Medical certificate; Civil or military equivalent qualifications relevant to the role; Supervisory experience in aviation in a key person role or similar; Understanding of Australia's Civil Aviation Act 1988 and relevant CASRs and CARs; Hold, or be eligible to hold, an ASIC. About Our Division: The purpose of Regulatory Oversight Division (ROD) is to deliver upon aviation safety obligations for the provision of surveillance and regulatory services to authorisation holders. For further information contact Owen Richards, 03 95182737, owen.richards@casa.gov.au.
