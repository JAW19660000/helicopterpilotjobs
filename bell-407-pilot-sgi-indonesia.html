Bell 407 Pilot - VRLL Operations-Indonesia

Indonesia · Posted 2026-09-28 · 👁 … views

Sayap Garuda Indah (SGI), one of Indonesia's leading aviation companies, is seeking experienced Rotary Wing Pilots for precision long-line operations. This role offers the opportunity to fly in challenging terrains and serve remote communities across Indonesia. SGI especially welcomes candidates who are ready to relocate to Indonesia and committed to working in remote areas.

Base: Indonesia
Join date: Immediate opening

Requirements:
- Valid CPL-H (or ATPL-H)
- Current Bell 407 Type Rating with 100hr on type
- Minimum 1,500+ hours total helicopter time
- Minimum 500+ hours on single-engine turbine helicopters
- Minimum 300 hr VRLL experience of which 100hr must be precision VRLL; high altitude operational experience (8,000+ ft) preferred
- Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Proficient in English (ICAO Level 4 minimum)
- Proven track record of adhering to high safety standards and operational discipline

Submit your CV: talent.hub@sgi-aero.com
