Seasonal Helicopter Pilot for Surf Life Saving WA

Western Australia (Busselton) · Posted 2026-06-27 · 👁 … views

Job summary: Helistar Aviation is looking for seasonal pilots to support operations in Busselton, Western Australia, starting late August. Pilots will patrol stunning beaches and provide essential services for Surf Life Saving Western Australia. Applicants should have a CASA CPL H, minimum 1000 hours flying helicopters, and strong communication skills. Accommodation is available at the company residence in Busselton when on duty. Qualifications: Right to live and work in Australia; Minimum 1000 hours flying helicopters; Must have a CASA CPL H and Class 1 Medical. Responsibilities: Fly daily, patrolling beautiful beaches in Australia; Support valuable work conducted by Surf Life Saving Western Australia; Overnight accommodation at company's residence when rostered. Skills: High level written and verbal communication skills; 1000 hours flying helicopters; 500 hours helicopter turbine; Drivers Licence; Nil accident history in the last 5 years. Education: CASA CPL H; CASA Class 1 Medical; Dangerous Goods Awareness Course. Tools: Red ASIC; Low Level Rating; HUET.
