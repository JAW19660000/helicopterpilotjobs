Chief Pilot - Rotary Wing-Papua New Guinea

Papua New Guinea · Posted 2026-11-09 · 👁 … views

Hevilift is an established aviation operator in Papua New Guinea, providing rotary-wing and fixed-wing charter services across PNG. Hevilift is seeking an experienced Chief Pilot – Rotary Wing to lead its rotary-wing flight operations and provide professional and operational leadership to its pilots and aircrew. Full time. (Advertised by Air Navigators Pty Ltd, Brisbane.)

The role
A senior aviation leadership position responsible for the safe, compliant and effective operation of Hevilift’s rotary-wing flying activities.
- Lead and oversee the Company’s rotary-wing flight operations
- Ensure all operations comply with aviation legislation, regulatory approvals, Company manuals and procedures
- Provide leadership, direction and professional oversight to pilots, crewmen and loadmasters
- Oversee crew rostering, licensing, qualifications, recency, flight and duty limitations and records
- Establish and oversee check and training programs for pilots and aircrew
- Monitor pilot proficiency in normal and emergency operating procedures
- Maintain flying qualifications and currency to provide operational flying support when required
- Provide leadership and guidance to Lead Pilots across operational bases
- Monitor and respond to safety reports, incidents and aviation safety matters
- Liaise with aviation regulatory authorities and external agencies
- Work with Engineering to support aircraft availability and continued airworthiness
- Support the Safety Management System, audit processes and corrective actions
- Visit remote operational bases and provide operational oversight
- Provide regular operational reporting to senior management

About you
- Current Air Transport Pilot Licence – Helicopter, or an equivalent licence capable of recognition by the relevant aviation authority
- Minimum of 5,000 hours flying experience
- Endorsement on at least one helicopter type in the fleet, i.e. Bell 212/412, BK117 or AS332
- Previous aviation management or senior flight operations experience
- Previous check and training experience
- Strong knowledge of aviation legislation, regulatory compliance and flight safety
- Demonstrated knowledge of aviation risk assessment and risk management
- Proven ability to lead pilots and operational teams to high professional and safety standards
- Strong communication, leadership and operational decision-making capabilities
- PNG, Pacific, remote or similar operational experience would be advantageous

Touring roster & benefits
- 4 weeks on / 4 weeks off touring roster
- Flights provided to and from PNG
- Full accommodation, meals and laundry services provided for the duration of each tour

How to apply
Apply on SEEK (job 95755836) with your CV outlining your helicopter types and flying hours, licences and endorsements, check and training experience, and previous flight operations leadership or management appointments.
