Job Title: H145 D2 Captain. Location: Papua New Guinea. Contract Type: Fixed-Term. Work Schedule: 4 weeks on, 4 weeks off. Aircraft Type: Airbus H145 D2. Job Overview: A reputable aviation operator is seeking experienced H145 D2 Captains to support utility operations in mountainous tropical environments. This role requires strong vertical reference and VFR flying skills, with a focus on safety and operational excellence. Key Responsibilities: Operate Airbus H145 D2 helicopters in VFR conditions; conduct utility missions including vertical reference long line operations; safely navigate and operate in mountainous terrain; maintain high standards of flight safety and crew coordination; communicate effectively with team members and ground support. Requirement: Proven VFR utility flying experience; demonstrated vertical reference long line proficiency; experience operating in mountainous terrain under VFR conditions; strong command of English (written and verbal); ability to work in austere tropical environments.
