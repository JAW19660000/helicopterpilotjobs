Qualified Flying Instructor (A-Cat, AH-64)-Townsville QLD

Townsville, QLD · Posted 2026-11-06 · 👁 … views

Boeing Defence Australia (BDA) is seeking an experienced Qualified Flying Instructor (Category A) to join the AH-64 Apache sustainment team in Townsville, North Queensland. A foundational team bringing this latest Australian Defence Force capability online. Permanent, full time.

The role
- Deliver AH-64E airborne, synthetic and ground-based instruction to support pilot development
- Oversee and conduct AH-64E general flying operations
- Maintain all flying currency and qualifications required by Army Aviation Orders, Instructions and Publications
- Champion a robust safety culture
- Manage instructional and administrative tasks; contribute to improving training methods

Essential
- Hold or have held an AUS / UK / US military AH-64E qualification
- Hold, or in the previous 3 years have held, a rotary wing instructor rating equivalent to ADF Category A QFI, with: at least 2,000 hours total turbine rotary wing (at least 1,500 PIC); at least 250 hours AH-64E (at least 100 PIC); at least 700 hours instructional experience, of which at least 500 is aircraft instruction
- Military command Night Vision Device (NVD) qualification or civilian equivalent
- Fluent spoken and written English
- Able to pass an ADF aviation medical, ideally without limitation
- Able to obtain and maintain a Defence security clearance (Non-Vetted Level 1)

Desirable
- Military Instrument Rating Assessor or civilian equivalent
- All qualifications required by AAvnTC SOPs
- Instructional experience on synthetic devices
- Previous military flying instruction experience
Highly desirable: AUS / UK / US AH-64E V6 qualification; military standards or instructor assessor role (or civilian equivalent)

Security
Australian applicants: ability to obtain and maintain a Defence security clearance. Non-Australian applicants: willingness to undertake Australian citizenship and obtain and maintain a clearance. Relocation support to Townsville may be available.

How to apply
Apply on the Boeing Careers page (job reference BOE/1945910) or via SEEK (job 95730989). Include a brief cover letter and a current CV. Applications close 20 November 2026.
