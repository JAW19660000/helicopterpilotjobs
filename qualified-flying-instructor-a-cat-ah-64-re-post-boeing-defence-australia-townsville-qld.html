Qualified Flying Instructor (A-Cat, AH-64) RE-POST-Townsville QLD

Townsville, QLD · Posted 2026-11-24 · 👁 … views

RE-POST – this role has been re-advertised by Boeing Defence Australia.

Boeing Defence Australia (BDA) is seeking an experienced Qualified Flying Instructor (Category A) to join the AH-64 Apache sustainment team in Townsville, North Queensland, a rare opportunity to be part of a foundational team bringing this latest Australian Defence Force (ADF) capability online. Permanent role, full time, with benefits and an annual bonus.

BDA Qualified Flying Instructors deliver instruction across all aspects of flight training – airborne, synthetic and ground – upholding the benchmark of quality and safety.

What you’ll get to do
- Deliver high-quality AH-64E airborne, synthetic and ground-based instruction to support pilot development
- Oversee and conduct AH-64E general flying operations in accordance with established protocols
- Maintain all flying currency and professional qualifications as mandated by Army Aviation Orders, Instructions and Publications
- Champion a robust safety culture
- Manage associated instructional and administrative tasks
- Contribute to the evolution of training methodologies and team performance

Essential
- Must hold or have held an AUS / UK / US military AH-64E qualification
- Hold, or in the previous 3 years have held, a rotary wing instructor rating equivalent to ADF Category A QFI, with: at least 2,000 hours total turbine rotary wing flying experience, with at least 1,500 hours PIC; at least 250 hours total AH-64E, with at least 100 hours PIC; and at least 700 hours of instructional experience, of which at least 500 hours is aircraft instructional
- Military command Night Vision Device (NVD) qualification or civilian equivalent
- Fluent in spoken and written English
- Ability to pass an ADF aviation medical, ideally without limitation
- Ability to obtain and maintain a Defence security clearance (Non-Vetted Level 1)

Desirable
- Military Instrument Rating Assessor or civilian equivalent
- All qualifications required by AAvnTC SOPs
- Previous instructional experience on synthetic devices
- Previous military flying instruction experience

Highly desirable
- Hold or have held an AUS / UK / US AH-64E V6 qualification
- Hold or have held a military standards or instructor assessor role, or civilian equivalent

Security requirement
Australian applicants: ability to obtain and maintain a Defence security clearance. Non-Australian applicants: a willingness to undertake Australian citizenship and obtain and maintain an Australian Defence security clearance.

Location and benefits
Townsville, QLD. Relocation support may be available.

How to apply
Apply via the SEEK listing with a brief cover letter outlining your skills and experience, and a current CV that includes your work history and qualifications.
