HMPT Co-Pilot-Western Australia

Western Australia · Posted 2026-05-06 · 👁 … views

Auriga Group is a safe, focused, customer-centric, and dedicated team of Marine and Aviation professionals located across Australia. Auriga Group comprises of three operational divisions, which provide services across 20 locations nationally. These operating divisions are across five business entities, with a plan to grow. Auriga Aviation: As one of the key operating divisions, Auriga Aviation has earned a strong reputation as an industry leader in the delivery of specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services throughout Australia. We operate a modern and well‑maintained fleet of Airbus helicopters, supporting the maritime industry with safe, efficient, and highly specialised aviation solutions. Our services support a diverse customer base and are delivered across multiple coastal and offshore locations nationwide. Safety, operational integrity, and customer satisfaction underpin every aspect of our aviation operations. Current Opportunities: Auriga Aviation is currently seeking an experienced Helicopter Co-Pilot based in Karratha to join our growing team. If you're a motivated, self‑driven aviation professional with a strong commitment to safety and operational excellence, this opportunity could be ideal for you. You'll enjoy working as part of a collaborative team while also having the confidence and capability to operate independently when required. If this sounds like you, we encourage you to apply. HMPT Co-Pilot – Mandatory Qualifications and experience: Australian Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter) - CPL(H); Minimum 1000 hours helicopter; Minimum 500 hours PIC helicopter; SEH Instrument Rating; Low Level Helicopter Rating; Current Class 1 Australian Aviation Medical Certificate. HMPT Co-Pilot – Desirable Qualifications and experience: Previous Multi-Engine Helicopter; EC135 Type Rating; NVIS Rating; Previous maritime experience; Previous multi-crew experience.
