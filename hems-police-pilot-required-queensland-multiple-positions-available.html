HEMS/Police Pilot Required-Queensland (Multiple Positions Available)

Queensland · Posted 2026-06-24 · 👁 … views

MULTIPLE PERMANENT POSITIONS AVAILABLE - APPLY NOW! Applications will be assessed on a regular basis. Join Queensland Government Air (QGAir) and take advantage of 12.75% superannuation and Salary Sacrifice up to $17,000 reportable fringe benefit amount for QGAir Rotary Wing Employees. COMPETITIVE RENUMERATION PACKAGE to be negotiated with the successful candidates. Considering a move to Cairns to join QGAir? Relocation assistance is available, and we encourage applications from qualified helicopter pilots eager to relocate and start their new role with us.

About Us: Queensland Government Air (QGAir) forms part of the Queensland Police Service (QPS), Aviation Capability Group (ACG) and provides government aviation services specialising in both rotary and fixed wing operations in support of the QPS and our partner agencies in regular and emergency response operations across Queensland. Our commitment to safety, efficiency and innovation has established us as a leader in government aviation services.

The Role: Join QGAir's dynamic rotary wing team in Cairns and make a real difference to the lives of those in need. We are seeking experienced helicopter pilots to fly single pilot, instrument flight rules (IFR) in multi-engine AW139 helicopters. You'll be part of a highly coordinated, multi-crew environment conducting emergency medical, search and rescue and counter-disaster operations. Your role will involve supporting aeromedical and various police and government related missions ensuring each operation is conducted safely and efficiently.

What We Offer: Competitive salary package; 12.75% superannuation; Salary Sacrifice up to $17,000 reportable fringe benefit amount; The chance to be part of a dedicated and compassionate team that makes a difference every day; A supportive and dynamic work environment that values teamwork and excellence; Work/life balance; Career growth and development opportunities; Relocation assistance available.

What We're Looking For - Mandatory requirements: Hold a current Australian Commercial Pilots Licence (CPL - Helicopter); Completion of Air Transport Pilots Licence Subjects (Helicopter); Low Level Rating; Multi Engine Command Instrument Rating - 3 Renewals; Minimum 2500 hours total time; Minimum 1500 hours Pilot in Command (PIC) Helicopter; Minimum 200 hours Night PIC Helicopter; Minimum 500 hours Multi-Engine PIC Helicopter; Minimum 1200 hours Turbine Helicopter; Hold or be eligible to hold an Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC); Current Class 1 Aviation Medical.

Highly desirable: Hold a current Australian Air Transport Pilots Licence (ATPL); Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS) Rating; Minimum 100 hours NVIS Operations; AW139 Type Rating; Hold a current Australian Passport.

Suitably qualified candidates are encouraged to apply now! Applications to remain current for 12 months. Job Ad Reference: QLD/693095/26. Closing Date: Thursday, 24th September 2026.
