Full-Time Helicopter Pilot (R44)-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2025-11-25 · 👁 … views

Bekaa Air is seeking a professional Robinson R44 pilot for our tours, charter, aerial work, and scenic operations based at Archerfield Airport, Brisbane. We operate a mixed fleet including the Airbus H125/AS350, Bell 206L3, and R44RII. Due to high demand and continued growth, we are looking for an additional pilot to join our full-time team. About the Role - Operations & Flying: This is a varied role in a small, close-knit team. It is not a purely flying position; in addition to your piloting duties, you will be integral to our day-to-day operations. These responsibilities are shared across the team and include: Managing client bookings, emails, and phone enquiries; Delivering top-tier customer service; Assisting with future projects and operational development. We are looking for a professional who takes pride in delivering an outstanding customer experience, values immaculately presented aircraft, and contributes to a team that supports one another. Required Skills & Qualifications: A high degree of competence and confidence with ATC is essential, as a large portion of our flights operate in and out of Class C airspace. Minimum Qualifications: Australian (CASA) CPL(H); Class 1 Medical; Current ASIC & Dangerous Goods; 200+ hours Total Time; 20+ hours on Robinson R44; Commercial Experience; High standard of personal presentation and attention to detail; Demonstrated ability to conduct tours independently with a strong focus on customer service; Proficiency operating within Class C airspace. Remuneration & Application: Salary will be based on experience and qualifications and will be at or above the award. Applicants must have a current legal right to work in Australia. Please note: Only applicants who meet all the minimum qualifications listed above will be considered.

Apply via email: careers@bekaaair.com.au (send Cover Letter and CV)
