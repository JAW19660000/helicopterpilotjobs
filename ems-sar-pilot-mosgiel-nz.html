EMS/SAR Pilot-Mosgiel, Otago, NZ

Mosgiel, Otago, New Zealand · Posted 2026-09-04 · 👁 … views

Helicopters Otago is seeking expressions of interest for EMS/SAR pilots at our Taieri Base.

About the role
As an EMS/SAR Pilot, you will conduct air ambulance and rescue missions across mountainous, coastal, and remote terrain, operating in some of New Zealand's most dynamic and challenging environments. You will work in a 4 on 4 off roster environment, working closely with management, paramedics and other crew while maintaining the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and decision-making.

Key responsibilities
- Conduct air ambulance and rescue missions as pilot-in-command
- Work closely with management, paramedics and other crew
- Maintain the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and decision-making
- Participate in training, development, and other tasks as necessary
- Carry out pilot-maintenance on helicopters and equipment
- Maintain facilities and gear to the highest standards
- Live within a reasonable distance from the base to meet requirements for night mission dispatch times

About you
Minimum Requirements
- CPL(H) or ATPL (H) with current Class 1 Medical
- Minimum 2,000 hours total helicopter time
- Night rating
- Excellent CRM and communication skills
- Mountain flying experience
- Eligibility to live and work in New Zealand

Highly Regarded
- Multi-engine experience
- NVG rating and experience
- Instrument Rating (Helicopter) - SPIFR
- EMS and SAR experience including hoist operations
- BK117 / EC145 / H145 ratings & experience

Benefits
- Competitive remuneration
- Structured roster
- Modern, well-equipped aircraft and facilities
- Advanced training systems including simulator access
- Supportive, high-performing team culture
- The opportunity to operate in one of the most spectacular flying environments in the world

With bases at Taieri Airport and Queenstown, Helicopters Otago operates across the lower South Island and beyond, with opportunities to support operations at both locations.

Safety is at the heart of everything we do, and we're seeking a professional team player who will actively contribute to our strong safety culture and Safety Management System.

We offer a competitive remuneration package, professional development opportunities, working alongside a highly experienced and supportive team, and the chance to support our community doing life-saving work.
