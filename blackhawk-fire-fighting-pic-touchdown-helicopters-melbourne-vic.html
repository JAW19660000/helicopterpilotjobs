Blackhawk Fire-fighting PIC-Melbourne VIC

Melbourne VIC · Posted 2026-09-13 · 👁 … views

About the role
Touchdown Helicopters is seeking an additional contractor FAA licensed S70/H60 rated PIC for the Australian summer. The role will be based in Victoria, Australia, with a start date in November 2026 and a commitment until at least the end of March 2027.

Key responsibilities
- Operate H60 helicopters as Pilot in Command
- Perform long lining operations from H60
- Perform tanking operations from H60

About you
- FAA licensed S70/H60 rated PIC
- Proficient with long lining from H60
- Proficient with tanking from H60
- Must meet all Australian Arena requirements

About us
Touchdown Helicopters is a Wollongong, NSW, Australia based company that currently operates a fleet of 11 helicopters, including 3 x H60's, 1 x UH1H, 4 x AS350 (SD1/B2/B3), and 3 x R44's.
