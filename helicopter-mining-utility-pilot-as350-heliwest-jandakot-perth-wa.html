Helicopter Mining Utility Pilot (AS350)-Heliwest Jandakot Perth WA

Jandakot, Perth WA · Posted 2026-11-20 · 👁 … views

Heliwest is a Western Australian-based helicopter company conducting a diverse range of aviation operations, including Search and Rescue (SAR), Helicopter Emergency Medical Services (HEMS), Air Transport, Aerial Work, Training and Mining Support. Its fleet includes BK117, EC135, AS350 and B206 helicopters.

The role
Heliwest is seeking expressions of interest from experienced helicopter pilots to join its mining and utility operations in Western Australia. This full time, touring position offers the opportunity to operate across a diverse helicopter fleet, supporting the mining and resources sector with utility flying, sling load operations and general aerial work. The position is ideally suited to candidates based in Perth, although interstate applicants will also be considered.

Key responsibilities
- Conduct safe and efficient AS350 helicopter operations in support of mining and utility activities
- Undertake sling load, low-level and general aerial work as required
- Support mining and resources clients in remote locations
- Conduct thorough flight planning, risk assessments, pre-flight inspections and post-flight documentation
- Ensure all flying complies with CASA regulations, company procedures and safety management requirements
- Maintain a high standard of professionalism, communication and customer service
- Work collaboratively with operations teams and ground crews

Essential requirements
- Minimum 2,000 hours total helicopter flight time
- Minimum 100 hours on AS350
- Minimum 500 hours turbine experience
- Current low-level rating/endorsement
- Sling endorsement
- Strong safety awareness, sound judgement and professional communication skills
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in remote environments
- Availability for a touring roster, ideally from Perth

About the Vertical Aviation Group
The group encompasses Heliwest, Heli-Muster NT (HM Air) and Vertical Aviation Energy, the recently acquired Energy Division of Meridian Helicopters, providing helicopter and fixed-wing flight services, engineering and training to the mining, pastoral, government, industrial and tourism sectors across WA, SA, QLD and the NT. Interested in opportunities at other locations or operations? Express your interest to the group via the SEEK listing.

How to apply
Submit your resume and a current summary of your flight experience, including relevant aircraft hours, ratings and endorsements, via the SEEK listing.
