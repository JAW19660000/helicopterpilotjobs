Touring Firefighting Pilot (S-70/UH-60 SIC)-Stormhawk Aviation Offshore

Various offshore locations (touring roster) · Posted 2026-11-26 · 👁 … views

Stormhawk Aviation LP is an aviation services organisation focused on delivering safe, reliable and efficient operations in challenging environments. The company supports rotary-wing missions, with a strong emphasis on technical excellence and regulatory compliance, and values licensed professionals who maintain high standards of safety, quality and performance.

The role
This full-time, on-site touring firefighting pilot opportunity is based in various offshore locations on a touring roster and focuses on aerial firefighting operations with the Sikorsky S-70/UH-60. Positions are available for Second in Command (SIC). Pilots will support water and retardant drops, bucket operations and hover-fill techniques, as well as personnel and cargo transport in support of firefighting and emergency response, in accordance with regulatory requirements, aircraft flight manuals and company standard operating procedures. The SIC supports the commander of the aircraft with crew duties, monitoring, navigation, communications and checklist management in a multi-crew environment. Operations may be conducted in mountainous terrain, in adverse weather and, where approved, at night.

Day to day
- Pre-flight planning, weather and risk assessment, fuel management, and pre-flight and post-flight inspections
- Close coordination with ground crews, incident management teams, engineers and operations and logistics staff
- Maintaining accurate flight and training records
- Participating in safety briefings and recurrent training, and promptly reporting hazards, incidents and near misses under the Safety Management System
- Adapting to the demands of remote touring and field deployments

Qualifications
- FAA licence, with a current Class 1 or equivalent aviation medical certificate
- SIC: appropriate helicopter licence with multi-engine, multi-crew experience and a current or obtainable S-70/UH-60 type rating or SIC qualification; preference to pilots with turbine twin experience
- Aerial firefighting experience, including bucket work and/or tank operations; external load and long-line experience required, and preference to those with prior SAR, EMS or utility experience in multi-engine helicopters
- Instrument rating (helicopter) preferred
- Solid understanding of aeronautical principles as they apply to aircraft performance, flight safety and operations in high and hot conditions
- Knowledge of relevant aviation regulations, wildland fire behaviour and fire weather, and familiarity with Incident Command System (ICS) procedures
- Experience in remote or field operations with a strong focus on safety, compliance and reliability
- Effective communication, teamwork, CRM and problem-solving skills
- Willingness to work a touring roster; a current passport is required, and the ability to obtain the appropriate work authorisation across various countries
