Line Pilot - HMPT Captain-Newcastle NSW

Newcastle NSW · Posted 2026-10-02 · 👁 … views

Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide.

With over 140 years of combined operational experience, Auriga Aviation is committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance, and reliability.

Auriga Aviation is seeking a suitably qualified HMPT Captain to join our Flight Operations team working from Newcastle, NSW. Reporting to the Head of Flight Operations, this position is responsible for the safe and efficient operation of company aircraft while maintaining compliance with CASA regulations, company procedures, and Auriga Aviation's Safety Management System.

Key Responsibilities:
- Act as Captain on company-operated helicopters conducting HMPT operations from Newcastle
- Conduct helicopter operations by day, night, IFR and NVIS in accordance with company procedures and CASA regulations
- Perform pre-flight planning, aircraft inspections, navigation, and operational duties to the highest safety standards
- Conduct marine pilot transfers and other rotorcraft operations as required under the AOC
- Support base operations and administrative activities as required
- Maintain accurate flight logs and aircraft records
- Actively contribute to Auriga Aviation's Safety Management System
- Participate in recurrent training, checking, and professional development programs

Minimum Qualifications and Experience:
- Current Australian ATPL(H) or CPL(H)
- Current CASA Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Demonstrated proficiency operating in IMC and NVIS environments
- Extensive understanding of CASA regulations and aviation operational compliance requirements
- MEH Instrument Rating
- NVIS Rating Helicopter
- 2000 hours Total Time Helicopter
- 1500 hours PIC Helicopter
- 500 hours ME Helicopter
- 150 hours Instrument Flight Time
- 150 hours Night Flight Time
- Type Rating EC135
