Helicopter Pilot Required-New Zealand

New Zealand (Te Anau/Tuatapere) · Posted 2026-03-05 · 👁 … views

Take your aviation career to new heights in one of the most iconic flying environments in the world. Fiordland Helicopters is locally owned and operated, with bases in Te Anau and Tuatapere. Specialising in commercial and tourism operations across Fiordland. From precision long line and commercial operations through to tourism and hunting transport, we operate at the highest professional standard. We are currently inviting applications from highly experienced helicopter pilots to join our team in Te Anau (Te Anau airport) with some requirement to cover Tuatapere. This is a full-time position, rostered 5 day work weeks. We are looking for more than just flying hours. We are seeking a pilot with a positive attitude, strong work ethic and a genuine love for what you do. As an ambassador for our brand you will need to enjoy sharing your love of flying and Fiordland with people from around the world. The successful applicant will have: NZ citizenship, residency, or legal right to work in New Zealand; Availability to work weekends, public holidays, and peak season (Dec-Apr); Excellent communication and prioritisation skills; Strong teamwork and adaptability; A positive, personable and professional character. Minimum Requirements: 4000+ hours - helicopter; Rated on AS350 and Hughes 500; Strong commercial and longline experience; Proven mountain flying experience including snow operations. Required (or ability to obtain): Current First Aid Certificate; CAA Dangerous Goods Certification; NZ Class 1 Driver's Licence; NZTA Dangerous Goods endorsement. What we Offer: Competitive remuneration; Professional development opportunities; Access to exclusive landing locations and high-end tourism experiences; A strong operational focus on safety and excellence; The opportunity to live and work in one of the most spectacular aviation environments in the world.

Apply through SEEK: https://www.seek.com.au/job/90732843
