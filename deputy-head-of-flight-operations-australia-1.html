Deputy Head of Flight Operations-Australia

Australia · Posted 2025-09-03 · 👁 … views

Are you ready to play a key role in shaping the future of a growing national helicopter operation? We're looking for an experienced and motivated Deputy Head of Flight Operations (HOFO) to join our team. In this critical role, you'll support our Head of Flight Operations to establish scalable foundations, drive operational excellence, and foster a positive culture across our organisation. About Auriga Aviation: Auriga Aviation has established itself as an industry leader in the provision of specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services in Australia. Auriga Aviation operates a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters in the provision of services to the maritime industry, providing services to a diverse customer base across multiple locations throughout Australia. Role Overview: As Deputy HOFO, you will support a national operation, working closely with the HOFO and cross-functional teams to ensure the safe, compliant, and efficient conduct of flight operations. You'll help ensure compliance of manuals and documentation in addition to establishing systems and processes that support sustainable growth, focusing on continuous improvement and collaboration. You will be a strong champion for a culture of safety, respect, reliability and resilience founded upon accountability, professionalism, and teamwork. We are looking for an established or aspiring leader with a deep understanding of helicopter flight operations and a passion for building robust, scalable processes. Mandatory Requirements/Qualifications: Meet the requirements to be appointed as alternate HOFO and alternate Head of Operations (HOO) under CASR Parts 119 and 138 respectively; Strong working knowledge of CASA regulations as they relate to Air Transport and Aerial Work Helicopter operations; CPL or ATPL licence (Helicopter); Multi-Engine Helicopter Instrument Rating (MEH IR); Low Level Rating; NVIS Rating; Minimum of 2,500 hours total flight time (minimum 500 Multi Engine Command). Desirable Experience/Attributes: Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) experience; Type Ratings on Airbus H135 and/or H145 helicopters; Flight Instructor Rating (FIR); Flight Examiner Rating (FER); Previous appointment as HOFO or HOO in a helicopter operation; Experience with Air Maestro (or similar) aviation software; Tertiary qualifications in business, project or aviation management. Why Join Us: In addition to great pay and conditions, working at Auriga Aviation offers a unique and rewarding opportunity to be part of a dynamic, growing organisation where you can make a tangible impact. Suitably qualified candidates are encouraged to submit their application by 5pm AEST, Tuesday 16th September 2025, noting progress will favour early submission. Submit your cover letter and resume covering the above selection criteria.

Apply via SEEK: https://www.seek.com.au/job/86891632
