Rotary Wing Pilot-NSW

NSW (Bankstown Airport, Sydney) · Posted 2026-05-07 · 👁 … views

Helitreck is seeking an experienced and motivated full-time helicopter pilot to join our growing operations team based out of Bankstown Airport, Sydney. This role operates on a 2 weeks on / 2 weeks off rotation, with opportunities to work across a variety of operations and locations throughout Australia. Our operations primarily focus on: Firefighting and aerial fire operations; External load and long line work; Commercial lifting operations including installation of solar panels, extractor fans, air conditioning systems and rooftop equipment. Helitreck operates a fleet including: BK117; Bell 206; Bell 205. Due to the seasonal nature of aerial firefighting operations, roster flexibility is essential. During peak fire season, shorter breaks may be required, with longer breaks typically available during quieter winter periods. About the Role: The successful applicant will: Conduct fire, charter and utility helicopter operations across Australia; Operate in remote and varied environments as required by the company; Participate in external load and long line operations; Maintain high operational and safety standards; Work closely with clients, crew, and operational personnel. Minimum Requirements: Applicants must hold: Australian CPL(H); Minimum 2,000 hours Helicopter Pilot in Command; Minimum 1,000 hours turbine experience; Fire H Rating; Minimum 100 hours fire experience; Long line experience; HUET certification. Desirable Qualifications & Experience: Preference will be given to applicants with: Night flying experience; Winch endorsement/experience; BK117 experience; Bell 205 experience; Bell 206L experience. What We're Looking For: Strong work ethic and professionalism; Ability to adapt to changing operational requirements; Team-focused attitude; Commitment to safety and operational excellence; Flexibility to travel and work away from base as required. If you're an experienced rotary wing pilot looking to join a dynamic operation with diverse flying opportunities, we'd love to hear from you.

Helicopter Pilot - Fire Operations - Expression of Interest

Not specified · Posted 2026-02-04 · 👁 … views

McDermott Aviation, Australia's largest privately-owned heli-aviation company, is seeking experienced, IFR/NVG-qualified helicopter pilots for contract positions during the upcoming Australian Fire Season. With over 40 years of experience in aerial firefighting, heavy lifting, and emergency operations, we operate a fleet of 50+ aircraft across some of the world's most challenging environments.

The Role
As a pilot with McDermott Aviation, you will operate a diverse fleet of helicopters on missions including:
- Aerial firefighting
- Emergency response and medical support
- Specialised IFR and NVG missions

You will be responsible for:
- Ensuring safe and compliant operations under CASA, FAA, and company regulations.
- Conducting thorough pre- and post-flight inspections.
- Monitoring weather, flight conditions, and operational risks.
- Collaborating effectively with crew, engineers, and support staff to maintain mission readiness.
- This role requires flexibility, adaptability, and strong decision-making in fast-paced, high-risk environments, including night and instrument flight operations.

Who We're Looking For
We are seeking professional pilots who are:
- CASA CPL(H) and FAA CPL(H) holders
- IFR-rated, with multi-engine experience preferred
- NVG-qualified, with recent NVG operational experience
- Minimum 1500 hours total flight time, including 500 hours turbine experience
- At least 100 hours on medium-class helicopters (AS355, AS365, B212/412, B214, B205, or similar)
- In possession of a current medical certificate
- Hold a valid passport and can pass a pre-employment DAMP test
- Additional desirable experience: firefighting, SAR, EMS, or other high-intensity operations.

Why Join McDermott Aviation?
- Operate a modern, diverse fleet in demanding missions
- Join a highly skilled, professional team of pilots, engineers, and support staff
- Work in dynamic, challenging environments globally
- Gain exposure to advanced IFR/NVG operations and specialised aerial missions

Due to high volumes of applications, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
