Helicopter Pilot - Fire Operations - Expression of Interest

Not specified · Posted 2026-02-04 · 👁 … views

McDermott Aviation, Australia's largest privately-owned heli-aviation company, is seeking experienced, IFR/NVG-qualified helicopter pilots for contract positions during the upcoming Australian Fire Season. With over 40 years of experience in aerial firefighting, heavy lifting, and emergency operations, we operate a fleet of 50+ aircraft across some of the world's most challenging environments.

The Role
As a pilot with McDermott Aviation, you will operate a diverse fleet of helicopters on missions including:
- Aerial firefighting
- Emergency response and medical support
- Specialised IFR and NVG missions

You will be responsible for:
- Ensuring safe and compliant operations under CASA, FAA, and company regulations.
- Conducting thorough pre- and post-flight inspections.
- Monitoring weather, flight conditions, and operational risks.
- Collaborating effectively with crew, engineers, and support staff to maintain mission readiness.
- This role requires flexibility, adaptability, and strong decision-making in fast-paced, high-risk environments, including night and instrument flight operations.

Who We're Looking For
We are seeking professional pilots who are:
- CASA CPL(H) and FAA CPL(H) holders
- IFR-rated, with multi-engine experience preferred
- NVG-qualified, with recent NVG operational experience
- Minimum 1500 hours total flight time, including 500 hours turbine experience
- At least 100 hours on medium-class helicopters (AS355, AS365, B212/412, B214, B205, or similar)
- In possession of a current medical certificate
- Hold a valid passport and can pass a pre-employment DAMP test
- Additional desirable experience: firefighting, SAR, EMS, or other high-intensity operations.

Why Join McDermott Aviation?
- Operate a modern, diverse fleet in demanding missions
- Join a highly skilled, professional team of pilots, engineers, and support staff
- Work in dynamic, challenging environments globally
- Gain exposure to advanced IFR/NVG operations and specialised aerial missions

Due to high volumes of applications, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
