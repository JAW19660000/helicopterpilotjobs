As the owner and operator of the Auckland Westpac Rescue Helicopter and Northland Westpac Rescue Helicopter services, we provide critical emergency air ambulance, rescue, and medical transport services across Te Tai Tokerau and Tāmaki Makaurau. Every day, our crews respond to people facing some of the most challenging moments of their lives, when every moment counts. Operating around the clock, 365 days a year, our work reaches from remote rural communities and rugged coastlines to busy urban centres. Behind every mission is a dedicated team united by a shared commitment to making a meaningful difference for patients, whānau, and communities across the Northern Region of Aotearoa New Zealand. As an AW169 Co-Pilot, you'll play a vital role in supporting life-saving rescue and aeromedical operations. Working alongside experienced pilots, aircrew officers, intensive care paramedics, doctors, and emergency services personnel, you'll help deliver safe, professional, and effective aviation services when communities need them most. The opportunity | Ngā whakaritenga o te mahi: We are seeking a professional and safety-focused AW169 Co-Pilot to join our Flight Operations team based in Whangārei. This is an exciting opportunity to become part of one of New Zealand's leading rescue helicopter organisations, operating in a challenging and rewarding environment where no two days are the same. Working within a highly skilled multidisciplinary team, you'll support rescue, retrieval, and aeromedical missions while maintaining the highest standards of aviation safety, operational excellence, and patient focus. What you'll do: Support safe and compliant AW169 flight operations across rescue, retrieval, and aeromedical missions; Operate in accordance with Civil Aviation Authority regulations, company procedures, and operational standards; Conduct NVIS, VFR, IFR, and hoist operations as authorised; Work closely with pilots, aircrew officers, clinical crews, and support teams; Contribute to a strong safety culture and safe operational decision-making; Participate in training, checking, and professional development activities; Maintain operational readiness and regulatory compliance requirements; Deliver exceptional professionalism while representing Northern Rescue Helicopter throughout the region. Desirable: Instrument flight experience; Night Vision Goggle (NVG) experience; Hoist operations experience; Helicopter Emergency Medical Services (HEMS) operational experience; Experience working in diverse and multicultural environments. Why join us? This is your opportunity to join an organisation that genuinely changes lives. You'll work with a passionate and highly skilled team committed to delivering world-class rescue and aeromedical services across the Northern Region. In return, you'll enjoy meaningful work, ongoing professional development, and the satisfaction of knowing your efforts directly support better outcomes for patients, whānau, and communities. Ready to apply? If you're an experienced helicopter pilot looking for a rewarding career where your skills can help save lives, we'd love to hear from you.
