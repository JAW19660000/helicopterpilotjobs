Your Opportunity: This role will be based for Serco in Abu Dhabi working for a UAE Government Customer - Australian Citizens preferred (visa costs covered). Responsible for conducting acceptance test flights, pre/post phase test flights, ground/flight maintenance operational checks, ground/flight evaluation and troubleshooting. Specific Requirements: Minimum of 5 Years Aviation Maintenance; FAA, EASA, etc. or equivalent military training certificates (military or commercial aircraft maintenance school or course required); Graduate of AMOC Phase I course and the concerned aircraft MTPC (AMOC Phase II) or equivalents; Must be able to attain and maintain a local military flight physical (class I), equivalent to the FAA (class II); Minimum 1000 hours first pilot time (PIC) and 200 hours MTP in Bell 407; Must have strong knowledge of FAA, EASA, etc. and/or equivalent Army maintenance practices including TAMMS-A, Aeronautical Equipment Maintenance Management Procedures, and ATP Aviation Maintenance; Proven technical experience on air platforms from UAE, US, Australia and/or South Korea; Must be able to pass hands-on flight evaluation; Valid Pilot license with Test Pilot rating.
