AW139 SAR Copilot-East Sale, VIC

AW139 SAR Copilot
Permanent, Full-time
East Sale, VIC

Role Summary
We are currently seeking applications from suitably qualified and experienced pilots for an AW139 Copilot position within our Onshore Operations, supporting the Royal Australian Air Force Search and Rescue (RAAF SAR) contract based in East Sale, VIC with some touring as required.

Essential Criteria:
- Valid CPL(H)
- Helicopter Instrument Rating
- Minimum 500 flying hours (H)
- Minimum 100 multi-engine hours (H)
- Minimum 100 flying hours Pilot-In-Command (PIC) (H)
- Ability to obtain and maintain a CASA Class 1 Medical and Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC)

Desirable:
- ATPL (H)
- AW139 type rating
- Low Level Rating
- Previous SAR/EMS experience

Key Attributes:
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills
- Self-motivated with a results-oriented approach
- Positive attitude and strong team orientation
- Excellent collaboration and relationship-building skills
- Ability to work in a dynamic team environment

Job advert closes: Sunday 13 September 2026, 4:00pm (AWST)

Apply via SEEK (https://au.seek.com/job/94459825) or the CHC Careers portal (https://careers-chccrew.icims.com/jobs/7352/aw139-sar-copilot/job)
