Chief Rotary Pilot-Australia

Australia · Posted 2026-01-06 · 👁 … views

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR: We are looking for a Chief Rotary Pilot to join our team in Australia. The Rotary Chief Pilot will report to the Director of Flight Operations, supervising the conduct of the Helicopter Flight operations within Coulson Aviation Australia. It is highly desirable the successful candidate will live in the Sydney region, NSW. The role is based onsite at our Sydney Headquarters.

The Chief Pilot duties and responsibilities include: - Monitoring and maintaining and reporting to the chief executive officer on, the operator's compliance with the provisions of the civil aviation legislation and the operator's exposition that apply to all flying operations; - Ensuring that the operator's flight crew are provided with the information and documentation necessary to properly carry out their responsibilities; - Ensuring the proper allocation and deployment of the operator's aircraft and the operator's personnel, for use in the operator's operations; - Maintaining a complete and up-to-date reference library of operational documents, including but not limited to the following - Operations Manual, CAA, CAOs, CASRs CARs, and AIP - Setting and maintaining the standards for the operations in accordance with this operations manual; - Developing, managing and maintaining the operator's dangerous goods manual (if any) and ensuring that flight crew are trained in the handling of dangerous goods as required by the Regulations - Ensuring the proper allocation and deployment of aircraft and personnel for use in the operations; - Ensuring that Coulson Aviation flight crew member personnel are provided with the information and documentation necessary to properly carry out their responsibilities; - Managing the maintenance and continuous improvement of the operator's fatigue risk management system; - Ensuring the Coulson Aviation flight crew member personnel understand the safety policy; - Ensuring that training and checking of the operator's operational safety critical personnel (other than flight crew) conducted by or for the operator is conducted in accordance with the operator's operations manual. - Arranging flight crew rosters and flying schedules - Maintaining records of licences and ratings held by each flight crew member, including: Validity; Recency; Type ratings and any applicable licence restrictions - Maintaining a system to record flight crew duty and flight times to ensure compliance with duty and flight time limitations - Liaison with the Authority - Arranging and compiling Revisions to the Operations Manual and associated documents.

REQUIREMENTS: - Previous senior flight operations management experience. - Extensive regulatory knowledge to enable Coulson Aviation to conduct Coulson Aviation operations - A Valid CASA ATPL with B412 type rating. - Class 1 medical. - A minimum of 500 hours as PIC on the B412. - Low level experience or previous firebombing experience preferred.

We would like to thank all applicants in advance for their interest; however, only those selected for an interview will be contacted. We support diversity, equity and a workplace that is free from harassment and discrimination. We are committed to providing accommodation for people with disabilities. We value a diverse and inclusive workplace, encouraging applications from individuals from all cultural backgrounds, including Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. The successful applicant will be required to complete a National Police History Check.
