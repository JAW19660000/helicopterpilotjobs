Helicopter Pilot (Mustering)-Kimberley WA

Kimberley, WA (Fossil Downs & Liveringa Stations) · Posted 2026-10-25 · 👁 … views

Hancock Agriculture is seeking a full-time Helicopter Pilot to join its agricultural operations in the Kimberley, Western Australia, flying between Fossil Downs and Liveringa Stations and assisting with all helicopter operations on station: mustering, property checks and ferries to servicing in Broome. Each station runs 2 x R22 helicopters, with an R44 at Fossil Downs. Reporting to the General Manager WA, the pilot provides aerial services across station operations, predominantly mustering, and where qualified fire fighting, incendiary, sling operations and ferrying staff and stores when cut off by road. This is not a FIFO position: the pilot is based on station and integrates with station staff.

Skills and experience
- CASA Commercial Pilot Licence Helicopter: PPL(H) or CPL(H)
- Single-Engine Helicopter (SEH) class rating
- R22 endorsement, with low-level rating and aerial mustering endorsement
- R44 endorsement (desirable)
- Minimum 600 hours aerial mustering experience
- Current Class 1 medical certificate and ASIC
- Genuine experience in the movement, handling and processing of cattle
- Fencing, stock water and general maintenance skills; competent operation of station plant and machinery
- Strong work ethic, safety commitment and good understanding of animal welfare practices

What is offered
Competitive remuneration, ongoing internal flight training and a place in a supportive station team.

How to apply
There are two ways to apply: (1) through the Hancock Agriculture careers page using “Apply with SEEK”, or (2) by contacting Stacey Ford, Hancock Agriculture, on Stacey_Ford@hancockagriculture.com.au or 0439 849 611. Include your cover letter and current resume with flying hours listed.
