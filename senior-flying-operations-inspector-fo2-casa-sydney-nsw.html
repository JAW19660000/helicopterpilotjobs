Senior Flying Operations Inspector (FO2)-Sydney NSW

Sydney NSW (Hybrid) · Posted 2026-10-27 · 👁 … views

The Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA) is recruiting a Senior Flying Operations Inspector in Sydney NSW (hybrid: up to 50% working from home), an ongoing (permanent) full-time position in its Regulatory Oversight Division. The inspector exercises regulatory decision-making as a delegate, including flight assessments and oversight of authorisation holders, conducts oversight and education of industry examiners, and contributes to examiner and instructor proficiency checks, surveillance, compliance reporting and approvals. Salary $172,600 to $187,972 plus 15.4% superannuation. Applications close 11 November 2026 (11:30 pm AEDT). CASA job reference 171_10/26.

Eligibility
- 8+ years of appropriate experience including holding an ATPL (Fixed Wing) or a Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter Category Rating)
- Current Class 1 medical certificate
- Civil or military equivalent qualifications, e.g. flight examiner, instructor rating and/or training and checking pilot
- Supervisory experience in a key person role or similar (e.g. HOFO, HOO, Head of Training and Checking) or an equivalent role with a national aviation authority
- Australian citizen able to obtain and maintain the required security clearance; ASIC eligible
- Understanding of the Civil Aviation Act 1988, CASRs and CARs
- The delegate may consider equivalent experience in place of part of the 8-year requirement

Desirable
- Flight Instructor Rating with a current Pilot Instructor Rating proficiency check or equivalent
- Recent Part 141 or 142 operator experience including training and checking
- Auditing experience
- Australian Class C driver’s licence

How to apply
Apply through the CASA careers portal (job ref 171_10/26) or via the SEEK ad. For further information contact Paul Musumeci (CASA).
