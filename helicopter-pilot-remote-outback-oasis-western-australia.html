Helicopter Pilot - Remote Outback Oasis-Western Australia

Western Australia · Posted 2026-02-03 · 👁 … views

About us: Mount Hart Wilderness Lodge offers unique accommodation and adventurous outback helicopter and 4WD experiences along the famous Gibb River Road. With its unbeatable proximity to iconic Kimberley landmarks, Mount Hart is the perfect gateway to explore the West Kimberley including the Horizontal Falls (just 40 mins away by helicopter) and Buccaneer Archipelago, Twin Flame Falls, Bell Gorge, Tunnel Creek, Charnley River Gorge and much more.

The Role: The Mount Hart team are actively recruiting a qualified and experienced Helicopter Pilot to join our operations. Based from our remote location, 50km off the Gibb River Road and 240 kms from Derby, this unique opportunity is perfect for an adventure-seeking, motivated Pilot looking to explore the lifestyle change that the Kimberley can provide.

Flying over some of the most spectacular icons the region has to offer, the Pilot will be responsible for providing an informative and unforgettable experience through a variety of scenic flights and landed experiences to our guests.

- Competitive remuneration package PLUS flying bonus - Work as part of a committed & passionate team - Remote based location role

Essential Criteria - Full Australian living and working rights - CASA CPL (H) - 500+ hours minimum Helicopter Pilot in Command Time on R44 - ASIC - Dangerous goods Awareness Course - Full Australian Drivers License (C Class Manual minimum) - Nil accident history - A genuine passion for the outdoors and adventure - Good interpersonal relationship skills - Current CASA Class 1 medical certificate - A strong work ethic and commitment to working safely

Highly Desirable - Low Level Rating - HUET - Previous flight experience in the Kimberly (1 year experience)

If exploring gorges, wildlife, fishing and camping, is your kind of work… join us on our exciting journey. Apply today.
