Rotary Wing Pilot-NSW

NSW (Bankstown Airport, Sydney) · Posted 2026-05-07 · 👁 … views

Helitreck is seeking an experienced and motivated full-time helicopter pilot to join our growing operations team based out of Bankstown Airport, Sydney. This role operates on a 2 weeks on / 2 weeks off rotation, with opportunities to work across a variety of operations and locations throughout Australia. Our operations primarily focus on: Firefighting and aerial fire operations; External load and long line work; Commercial lifting operations including installation of solar panels, extractor fans, air conditioning systems and rooftop equipment. Helitreck operates a fleet including: BK117; Bell 206; Bell 205. Due to the seasonal nature of aerial firefighting operations, roster flexibility is essential. During peak fire season, shorter breaks may be required, with longer breaks typically available during quieter winter periods. About the Role: The successful applicant will: Conduct fire, charter and utility helicopter operations across Australia; Operate in remote and varied environments as required by the company; Participate in external load and long line operations; Maintain high operational and safety standards; Work closely with clients, crew, and operational personnel. Minimum Requirements: Applicants must hold: Australian CPL(H); Minimum 2,000 hours Helicopter Pilot in Command; Minimum 1,000 hours turbine experience; Fire H Rating; Minimum 100 hours fire experience; Long line experience; HUET certification. Desirable Qualifications & Experience: Preference will be given to applicants with: Night flying experience; Winch endorsement/experience; BK117 experience; Bell 205 experience; Bell 206L experience. What We're Looking For: Strong work ethic and professionalism; Ability to adapt to changing operational requirements; Team-focused attitude; Commitment to safety and operational excellence; Flexibility to travel and work away from base as required. If you're an experienced rotary wing pilot looking to join a dynamic operation with diverse flying opportunities, we'd love to hear from you.
