AW139 Co-Pilot-Victoria

Victoria · Posted 2025-11-20 · 👁 … views

AW139 Co-Pilot. Sale, Victoria (Esso, Longford). Flight Operations. Full-time, Residential Roster. The company: PHI is a global leader in helicopter services. In the Asia Pacific (APAC) region, PHI takes on some of the most challenging assignments in aviation to deliver high quality helicopter support for energy customers and search and rescue authorities. We are one of the safest helicopter operators anywhere in the world, as every flight we take is guided by our core values of Safe, Efficient, Quality, Service. About the role: We have an exciting opportunity for a qualified AW139 Co-Pilot to join our dynamic growing team, as part of the PHI team, operating under the Esso AOC, from the Longford facility. Reporting to the Head of Flight Operations you will play a pivotal role in the success of the team. To be considered for this role, you will ideally need: Hold or the ability to obtain a CASA CPL or ATPL (H) with Instrument Rating; AW139 Type Rating (desirable); 500 multi engine helicopter experience; 100 hours PIC helicopter; 50 hours on AW139. Additionally, the successful applicant will have: Sound judgement; Initiative; Excellent communication skills; Excellent relationship management skills; The ability to work well in a team. All applicants must have the right to live and work in Australia and successful applicants will be required to reside within commuting distance (vehicle) of Sale, Victoria. By joining our team, you will work in a rewarding and professional environment, with a diverse team of people sharing ideas and experiences. We offer an employee benefits package that includes a competitive salary and access to a comprehensive wellbeing platform. If this position sounds right for you, please register through our PHI Career Centre and follow the steps to complete an application. If you have any questions about this position, please contact: hradmin@phi-int.com. Applications close Friday, 19 December 2025. PHI reserves the right to commence shortlisting and remove the job advertisement prior to this date.

Apply via PHI Career Centre: https://phi-int.currentjobs.co/Job (questions to hradmin@phi-int.com)
