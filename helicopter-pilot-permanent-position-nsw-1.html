Helicopter Pilot - Permanent Position-NSW

NSW · Posted 2026-04-14 · 👁 … views

Commercial Helicopters has delivered proven, high quality helicopter services since 1990. Operating Australia wide, Commercial Helicopters brings extensive knowledge and practical experience across every sector in which it works. The company specialises in firefighting, aerial agriculture, mine support, exploration, survey, powerline and utility support, mustering, and feral animal control. Commercial Helicopters is accredited to Gold level under the Basic Aviation Risk Standard (BARS) and holds ISO 14001 Environmental Management certification. Combined with an approved company risk management system and in-house staff training, these standards have helped establish Commercial Helicopters as an industry leader in the services it provides. With a modern and capable fleet, highly qualified long-term staff, substantial operational infrastructure, and wholly owned subsidiary companies providing aviation fuel support and helicopter maintenance, Commercial Helicopters offers clients a reliable, integrated, and professional service.

About the Role: Commercial Helicopters is currently seeking expressions of interest for a permanent Utility/Fire base pilot to join the team at its Mudgee NSW base. You will be a team orientated and motivated individual with proven experience working autonomously in a similar role. You will be part of a diverse and highly experienced team providing aviation support to Commercial Helicopters government and private operations. Flexible and willing to travel whilst receiving an attractive renumeration package you will experience a diverse range of operations whilst being an integral part of the Commercial Helicopters team.

Your role will require the following minimums: 2000hrs Total time; 500hrs Turbine; 100hrs on AS350 and B206; 100hrs Fire experience; 100 Long line experience; Low level endorsement; HUET.

Preferred or willing to obtain (company provided upskilling available for the right candidate): Aerial AG Rating with experience (highly desirable); Aerial culling (highly desirable); Chemcert; Forklift licence; Rigging/Dogging certificate.

If you're ready to take the next step in your career with a company that has proven it places and values its people first before anything else then apply now. For further information or a confidential discussion about the role please contact our friendly team on 1300 206 212. Applications by COB 1st May 2026.
