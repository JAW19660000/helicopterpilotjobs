Flying Operations Inspector (Rotary)-All CASA Office Locations (advertised Perth WA)

All CASA office locations, Australia (this listing advertised in Perth WA, Hybrid) · Posted 2026-09-17 · 👁 … views

As a member of CASA's Aviation Technical (AT) job family, a Flying Operations Inspector (FO1) works under direction and guidance from a Manager and National Manager. The role sits within a team of inspectors and other related staff, who work collaboratively to deliver on CASA's strategic operational priorities and work plans, including the National Oversight Plan (NOP).

Key responsibilities of the flying operations inspector (FO1) include:
- Decision making, including as a delegate, on a range of routine regulatory service and surveillance activities which may include tasks associated with flight assessments and/or the oversight of authorisation holders
- Working collaboratively with colleagues, sharing knowledge, and managing personal performance in accordance with CASA's values, policies and practices
- Actively participating in work programs that deliver CASA's strategies and priorities, including initial approvals, compliance assurance and reporting, regulatory improvement and stakeholder engagement
- Working professionally with regulated entities, government departments, the aviation sector and the public, drawing on expertise to provide advice and technical guidance
- Conducting oversight and education of industry examiners, and technical input to professional development programs and examiner/instructor proficiency checks
- Identifying non-compliance under civil aviation legislation and presenting findings through high quality reports with clear recommendations
- Assessing and analysing industry information, engaging with stakeholders and investigating potential scenarios to develop compliance strategies

A more senior classification (FO2, Senior CASA Inspector) is also available through this recruitment, working under limited guidance on more complex regulatory and surveillance activities, including operational change approvals.

Mandatory qualifications, licences and experience:
- 8+ years of appropriate experience including holding an Air Transport Pilot's Licence (Fixed Wing) or Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter Category Rating)
- Civil or military equivalent qualifications relevant to the role, e.g. flight examiner, instructor rating, and/or training and checking pilot
- Supervisory experience in aviation in a key person role or similar (Head of Flying Operations, Head of Operations, Head of Training and Checking, or equivalent with a National Aviation Authority)
- Understanding of Australia's Civil Aviation Act 1988, relevant CASRs and CARs, guidance material and other legislation
- Hold, or be eligible to hold, an Australian Security Identification Card (ASIC)

Desirable qualifications, licences and experience:
- Flight Instructor Rating with a current Pilot Instructor Rating proficiency check or equivalent
- Recent experience with a Part 141 or 142 operator including training and checking experience
- Auditing experience
- An Australian Class C driver's licence

Eligibility requirements: applicants must be Australian citizens with the ability to obtain and maintain the required level of security clearance, must be willing to undergo a police record check, must be available to perform safety sensitive aviation activities, and must pass a pre-employment alcohol and drug test.

Please note: applicants must specify in their application which classification they are applying for, FO1 or FO2. Only one position will be filled through this recruitment process; a merit pool may also be established for future vacancies.

This role is advertised across all CASA office locations around Australia (this particular listing shown as Perth WA, Hybrid). Ongoing (permanent) position, closing 4 October 2026, Job Ref 131_09/26.
