Rotary Wing Co-Pilot - Brisbane

Brisbane · Posted 2026-02-02 · 👁 … views

About LifeFlight: LifeFlight Australia is a leading aeromedical organisation dedicated to saving lives through rapid response medical services. Operating a fleet of helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, LifeFlight delivers critical care and emergency retrievals across Queensland and beyond. In addition to its lifesaving missions, LifeFlight runs a world-class training academy, equipping aviation and medical professionals with the skills needed for high-pressure environments. About the Role: We have an opening for a Rotary Wing Co-Pilot to join our team based in Brisbane. This role operates on a 4 on 4 off roster and is covered by the LifeFlight Australia Limited Rotary Wing Pilots Enterprise Agreement 2024. As a Rotary Wing Co-Pilot, your key accountabilities are to: Perform the duties of Co-Pilot according to the LifeFlight Australia (LFA) roster, adhering to published administrative procedures and company policies. Follow rostered and on-call duties as per company policies, including adherence to the Operations Manual. Ensure compliance with safety protocols and statutory requirements for the safe, effective, and efficient operation of company aircraft. Identify, manage, and appropriately refer operational and safety risks in accordance with company policy and procedures. Implement and actively participate in the safety management system, workplace health and safety system, and risk management system. Monitor the currency of identified items nearing expiry and bring them to the attention of the appropriate manager. Ensure the serviceability and maintenance of operational ancillary equipment. Attend, participate in, and successfully complete all company operational, safety, fitness, and training sessions. Undergo necessary training for current and new business tasks and assist in mentoring new staff. Assist with media and public relations activities. Observe LifeFlight SOP's and policies, maintaining crew coordination and cooperation in the multi-crew environment. About you: Strong communication skills; A safety-first attitude; Enthusiasm and a drive to achieve and maintain the highest level of competency within aero-medical operations; CPL (H); CIR ME; NVFR(H); Minimum hours: 500 hours rotary wing, 100 hours Command Rotary Wing; Dangerous Goods Certificate; HUET/EBS Certified; Class 1 medical; NVIS Rating; AW 139 type rating is an advantage; Must live within Brisbane metropolitan area. Pay and conditions are in accordance with the LifeFlight Australia Limited Rotary Wing Pilots Enterprise Agreement 2024, Work Practice 1. Salary Packaging Options: employees can allocate up to $15,900 per Fringe Benefit Tax (FBT) year towards general living expenses. Note: the successful applicant will be required to undergo background checks including a national police check. This position is only open to Australian Citizens, Permanent Residents, or those with unrestricted work rights in Australia. Eligible candidates must be fully vaccinated against MMR, Varicella, Pertussis, Hepatitis B and will be required to hold or obtain a Working with Children Blue Card (Queensland). Applications close on Thursday, 16 February 2026.
