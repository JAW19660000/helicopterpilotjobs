Seasonal Fire Fighting Pilot - Huey / Squirrel-QLD & NSW

QLD & NSW · Posted 2026-10-03 · 👁 … views

JOB OPENING!

With the dry outlook for the summer and the very busy start we have openings for a seasonal Huey and or Squirrel fire fighting pilots.

Pilots must be able to work in Australia, meet fire experience minimums and be experienced in longline operations. We are happy to discuss training an experienced Squirrel pilot into the Huey as the season progresses as well. Work would be in Qld and NSW with a flexible roster.

Please send resumes and references to russell@1800helicopter.com.au, and if you have been in touch before please resend an application.

Apply: russell@1800helicopter.com.au
