Touring H125 Helicopter Pilot (PIC)-Stormhawk Aviation South Sumatra Indonesia

South Sumatra, Indonesia · Posted 2026-11-26 · 👁 … views

Stormhawk Aviation LP is an aviation services organisation focused on delivering safe, reliable and efficient operations in challenging environments. The company supports rotary-wing missions, with a strong emphasis on technical excellence and regulatory compliance, and values licensed professionals who maintain high standards of safety, quality and performance.

The role
This full-time, on-site touring H125 helicopter pilot role is for a Pilot in Command (PIC), based in South Sumatra, Indonesia, on a touring roster, focusing on Airbus H125 (AS350 B3e) operations. The pilot will fly utility, external load, passenger and cargo transport and aerial work missions, and may support firefighting operations, in accordance with regulatory requirements, the aircraft flight manual and company standard operating procedures.

Day to day
- Pre-flight planning, weather and performance assessment, fuel management, and pre-flight and post-flight inspections
- Close coordination with engineers, flight operations and logistics teams
- Maintaining accurate flight and training records
- Participating in safety briefings and recurrent training, and promptly reporting hazards, incidents and near misses under the Safety Management System

Qualifications
- Valid commercial licence (helicopter) recognised by the relevant aviation authorities, with a current Class 1 or equivalent aviation medical certificate
- Current type rating on the Airbus H125/AS350
- Significant long-line experience, at least 1,500 hours
- Experience in external load and long-line operations; preference to pilots with aerial firefighting (bucket work), utility, survey or similar aerial work experience
- Solid understanding of aeronautical principles as they apply to aircraft performance, flight safety and operations in high and hot conditions
- Experience in remote or field operations with a strong focus on safety, compliance and reliability
- Effective communication, teamwork, CRM and problem-solving skills
- Willingness to work a touring roster in South Sumatra, Indonesia; a current passport is required, and the ability to obtain the appropriate work authorisation for Indonesia
