Pilot Required-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-05-16 · 👁 … views

Helicopter Pilot – Charter. Location: Horn Island (Torres Strait). Basis: Fixed Term Contract | Immediate Start. Applications Close: 31st May 2026.

About Nautilus Aviation: For over 25 years, Nautilus Aviation has been a leader in the Australian aviation industry, delivering high-quality, safe, and reliable services. Our diverse operational scope covers search and rescue, aerial firefighting, disaster relief, specialised aerial work, tourism, corporate charter, and flight training. We operate one of Australia's largest and most versatile helicopter fleets—including the B412, B212S, H130T2, H125, B407, B206/B206L, and R44—tailoring solutions to meet the unique needs of our customers. Our team is driven by a commitment to professional excellence and safety.

The Role: We are seeking experienced Helicopter Pilots to join our teams at Horn Island. This is an immediate vacancy focusing on charter operations. This is an opportunity to work within a professional environment, operating well-maintained turbine aircraft in some of Australia's most unique flying environments.

Essential Qualifications & Experience - Licensing & Legal: CASA Commercial Pilot License (Helicopter); Right to live and work in Australia; CASA Class 1 Medical; ASIC (current); Manual Driver's Licence.

Flight Experience: 2,000 hours Minimum Total Time (Helicopter); 1,500 hours Minimum Pilot in Command (PIC); 250 hours Minimum PIC Turbine; 100 hours Minimum Time on Type (B206, B206L, or B407).

Ratings & Endorsements: Gas Turbine Engine (GTE); Low Level Rating; Sling Rating.

Operational Requirements: HUET (current); Dangerous Goods Awareness Course; Proficient knowledge of Australian aviation legislation; Proven high-level written and verbal communication skills; Nil accident history.

Remuneration: Salary is negotiable and will be determined based on the successful candidate's experience level.

Compliance & Culture: Nautilus Aviation values diversity. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples are encouraged to apply. All employees must comply with the Nautilus Aviation Drug and Alcohol Management Plan (DAMP) as required by CASA. Random workplace testing, Criminal Record Checks, and Working with Children Checks may apply.

How to Apply: To apply, please forward your current resume and a cover letter explicitly addressing the essential criteria listed above.

Apply by email: resumes@nautilusaviation.com.au (Subject: LINE PILOT – Horn Island; Closing Date: Tuesday, 31st May 2026)
