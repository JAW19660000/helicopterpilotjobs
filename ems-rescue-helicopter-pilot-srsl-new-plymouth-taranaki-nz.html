EMS/Rescue Helicopter Pilot-New Plymouth, Taranaki NZ

New Plymouth, Taranaki, NZ · Posted 2026-11-19 · 👁 … views

Search & Rescue Services Limited (SRSL) is a high-performing team of paramedics, pilots and aircrew dedicated to delivering lifesaving care across the Central and Lower North Island, 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Through critical medical transport and rescue operations, SRSL upholds the highest standards in both aviation and paramedicine, supported by a strong commitment to continuous training and professional excellence.

The position
A full time EMS/Rescue Helicopter Pilot to join the team at the New Plymouth base, making a real difference for the communities served.

What’s on offer
- A competitive salary
- Great work life balance
- The opportunity to contribute and give back to the community
- Meaningful work with a team of highly skilled, experienced and passionate professionals

Minimum skills / qualifications
- NZ CPL(H)
- 2,000 hours total time PIC (or equivalent)
- Unrestricted night VFR rating
- Full NZ Class 1 medical certificate
- Twin turbine engine experience
- Hoist/sling experience
- IFR / SPIFR (FW rating minimum)

Preference given to applicants with
- H145 rating
- NZ ATPL(H)
- EMS experience
- NVIS experience
- CRM
- Mountain experience
- HUET
- Dangerous Goods Certificate
- Air Maestro experience

A Police Check is required as part of employment. SRSL is an equal opportunity employer committed to diversity and inclusion.

How to apply
Forward your CV and cover letter to Tarrin Terry, Director, People & Culture, Search & Rescue Services Limited, via the SEEK listing. Applications close 2 December 2026.
