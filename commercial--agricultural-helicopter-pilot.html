Commercial/ Agricultural Helicopter Pilot

Christchurch, New Zealand · Posted 2026-06-17 · 👁 … views

Christchurch Helicopters (CHL) is a family owned and operated aviation organisation with over 20 years of experience in the industry, based out of Christchurch Airport and operating in conjunction with sister organisations Pacific Aircraft Services and VADIS Geomatics. Christchurch Helicopters provides a wide range of aviation services and is CAANZ Part 141, 135 and 137 certificated. CHL completes a number of services in the aviation industry including pilot training, agricultural ops, fire fighting, lifting, hunting, filming, aerial survey, Lidar survey, scenic flight/charter, and other commercial ops. Although our main base is in Christchurch and predominately operate throughout Canterbury, CHL also operates throughout NZ on various projects. CHL operates a number of aircraft including Guimbal Cabri G2, MD500E, MD520N, EC120, AS350 B2, AS350 B3E & AS355 F2.

The Position: Christchurch Helicopters are looking for a hard working commercial & ag pilot to invest themselves into the agricultural & commercial departments at Christchurch Helicopters. The role is full time Monday to Friday with occasional weekend work when required. The majority of work is within Canterbury however you may be required to travel for specific operations. Join our fantastic team and enjoy a variety of operations with room to grow within the organisation.

What we are looking for: NZ CPL(H); Agricultural rating; Experience on the aircraft we operate; Motivated to help grow the business & seek opportunities; Basic computer skills; Clean, tidy and looks after equipment; Responsible & safe pilot; Team player.

Remuneration arrangement to be discussed offering a salary plus bonus structure. Additional training will be provided for suitable applicants. Christchurch Helicopters is happy to discuss the position which can be adapted for the right applicant, including full time/part time options.

Apply by email/website: https://christchurchhelicopters.co.nz
