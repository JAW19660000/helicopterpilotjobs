Bell 407 Pilot - Precision Long-Line (VRLL) Operations

Base: Indonesia
Join Date: Immediate Opening

Sayap Garuda Indah (SGI), one of Indonesia's leading aviation companies, is seeking experienced Rotary Wing Pilots for precision long-line operations. This role offers the opportunity to fly in challenging terrains and serve remote communities across Indonesia.

We especially welcome candidates who are ready to relocate to Indonesia and committed to working in remote areas.

Requirements:
- Valid CPL-H (or ATPL-H)
- Current Bell 407 Type Rating with 100hr on type
- Minimum 1,500+ hours total helicopter time
- Minimum 500+ hours on single-engine turbine helicopters
- Minimum 300 hr VRLL experience of which 100hr must be precision VRLL. High altitude operational experience (8000+ft)
- Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Proficient in English (ICAO Level 4 minimum)
- Proven track record of adhering to high safety standards and operational discipline

Email your resume to: talent.hub@sgi-aero.com (Sayap Garuda Indah / SGI Aero); www.sgi-aero.com
