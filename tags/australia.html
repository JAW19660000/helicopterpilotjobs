Role Summary
We are looking for suitably qualified and experienced Pilots to be considered for the position of EMS Captain for our Department of Fire and Emergency Services (DFES) Contract. This is a casual position, with coverage required at both Jandakot and Bunbury.

Qualifications, Skills & Attributes
Suitable applicants will require the following minimum qualifications and experience:
- Valid ATPL (H)
- Valid IR-C-ME(H) with a minimum of 3 renewals
- 3,000 flying hours (H)
- 2,000 flying hours Pilot-In-Command (PIC) (H)
- 1,200 flying hours multi engine command (H)
- Current AW139 Type Rating, with 100 PIC hours on type
- NVIS rating
- 200 hours night flying
- Low Level rating with Winch Endorsement
- Ability to successfully complete a Single Pilot IPC and EMS line check in the AW139
- CASA Class 1 Medical
- ASIC
- Previous single pilot SAR/EMS experience

Applicants must be an Australian citizen and be able to hold and maintain applicable clearances as required for the position.
