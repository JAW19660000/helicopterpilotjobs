Grade 1/2 Flight Instructor-Archerfield Brisbane QLD

Archerfield, Brisbane QLD · Posted 2026-10-07 · 👁 … views

V2 Helicopters is a growing and forward-thinking rotary aviation business based in Brisbane. Due to continued expansion, we are seeking passionate and professional Flight Instructors to join our team. This is an exciting opportunity to be part of a dynamic training environment with modern aircraft, strong industry connections, and a commitment to high-quality aviation training.

About the role
You will be responsible for delivering high-quality flight and theory instruction in accordance with CASA regulations, while supporting student progression and maintaining the highest standards of safety and professionalism. As V2 operates 7 days a week, some weekend work will be required. We offer all our instructors a competitive salary commensurate with experience. (Part time)

Key responsibilities
- Deliver flight and/or theory instruction to students
- Ensure training is conducted in accordance with CASA and company procedures
- Monitor student progress and provide structured feedback
- Maintain accurate training records and documentation
- Uphold a strong safety culture and professional training environment

About you
- Commercial Pilot Licence – Helicopter (CPL(H))
- Flight Instructor Rating (FIR)
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Professional attitude and commitment to safety
- Gas Turbine Endorsement (highly desirable)
- Experience on Bell 206 and/or Airbus AS350 (H125) (highly desirable)

Benefits
- Competitive remuneration (based on experience)
- Supportive and professional team environment
- Opportunities for career progression and upskilling
- Exposure to a diverse range of training and operational activities
