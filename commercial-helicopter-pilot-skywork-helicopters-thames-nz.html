Commercial Helicopter Pilot-Thames, Waikato NZ

Thames, Waikato NZ · Posted 2026-10-21 · 👁 … views

Skywork Helicopters is seeking an experienced Helicopter Pilot to support its Thames operation. There will be an onboarding period at its Warkworth facility. The role offers variety and team camaraderie, with the opportunity to develop and progress your general aviation skills. Full time.

Key responsibilities
- Operate helicopter services including lifting, surveying, fire fighting, charter and agricultural services across New Zealand
- Support the Thames operation with professionalism and dedication
- Maintain clean, tidy aircraft and associated role and support equipment
- Work as part of a team in a hard-working, enthusiastic environment

About you
- Commercial Pilot’s Licence and Class 1 Medical
- Grade 2 or 1 Ag AS350B3 rating
- Current passport and NZ driver licence
- NZ residency or the right to work in NZ
- Minimum 1,500 hours rotary total time, 500 hours turbine
- Minimum 500 hours lifting time
- Minimum 100 hours tacho on type AS350 (logbook verified)
- Professionally organised approach in all aspects of the job

About Skywork
Family owned and operated, with bases in Whangarei, Thames, Taranaki and its head office and hangars in Warkworth, providing lifting, surveying, fire fighting, charter and agricultural services New Zealand wide.
