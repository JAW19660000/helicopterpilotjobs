CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor-Townsville, QLD

Townsville, QLD · Posted 2026-09-09 · 👁 … views

About the role
Boeing Defence Australia is seeking a highly skilled CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor (QFI) to join our Chinook Integrated-Services Support (CISS) contract in Townsville. This position is embedded within the School of Army Aviation (SAA) Chinook Wing, playing a critical role in the training and readiness of aviation personnel. This position requires a dedicated professional capable of delivering high-level instruction across airborne, synthetic, and ground-based environments while maintaining the highest standards of flight safety and operational excellence.

Responsibilities
As a CH-47F Qualified Flying Instructor (QFI), you will:
- Instructional Delivery: Execute high-quality CH-47F airborne, synthetic, and ground-based instruction to support pilot development
- Flight Operations: Oversee and conduct CH-47F general flying operations in accordance with established protocols
- Compliance & Currency: Proactively maintain all flying currency and professional qualifications as mandated by Army Aviation Orders, Instructions and Publications
- Safety Leadership: Champion a robust safety culture, promoting a secure and effective work environment for all team members
- Operational Support: Manage associated instructional and administrative tasks to ensure seamless training workflows
- Continuous Improvement: Actively contribute to the evolution of training methodologies and team performance

Experience/Qualifications
Essential Criteria:
- CH-47F QFI (Category C)
- CH-47F NVIS Command
- Group 4 Command Instrument Rating
- CASA CPL-H (or higher)
- Current CLASS 1 Medical
- CASA ASIC
- Ability to obtain and maintain a NV1 Defence security clearance

Desirable Criteria:
- CH-47F QFI (Category B/A)
- Group 4 Instrument Rating Assessor
- CH-47F TFPS Instructor Operator Simulator
- CH-47F Maintenance Pilot qualification
- Simulator Reference Pilot

Eligibility
Applicants must be Australian citizens to meet Defence security requirements and must be willing to undergo the standard government vetting process, including a full life background check. BDA will sponsor and support the required clearance process.

Benefits
- Competitive Salary & Bonuses: Enjoy a competitive salary along with yearly bonuses
- Access various paid leave entitlements to support your personal and family needs
- Financial Discounts: Take advantage of discounted health insurance premiums, travel, and retail shopping, along with a nationwide Fitness Passport
- Financial Benefits: Explore options for novated leasing and salary sacrifice for superannuation
- Career Development: Receive both informal and formal tailored mentorship to accelerate your career growth and design promotional pathways
- Recognition Program: Participate in our recognition award program, which acknowledges professional achievements through a points and payment scheme

About the company
With more than 4,500 team members across Boeing Australia, BDA plays a critical role in supporting Australia's security and strengthening its partnership with the ADF through advanced systems integration and defence capability.
