Station Helicopter Pilot (R22) 2027-Cooee Hills Station NT

Cooee Hills Station, near Daly Waters NT · Posted 2026-11-15 · 👁 … views

A&E Pastoral is seeking a helicopter pilot for Cooee Hills Station (formerly Amungee Mungee), a 330,000-hectare cattle property running around 25,000 head of Brahman and Brahman-cross cattle, supported by extensive water infrastructure, laneway systems and ongoing property development. In addition to the home station, the business operates lease blocks near Alice Springs, offering both the tropical savannas of the Top End and the arid landscapes of Central Australia. The station is approximately one hour from Daly Waters, NT. Commencing 2027. Advertised by Anna Brown Recruitment.

The role
Flying the station’s R22 helicopter, you can expect approximately 600 flying hours annually, undertaking aerial mustering and a range of property support activities.
- Aerial mustering and cattle work using the station R22
- Aerial water point inspections
- Ferrying the helicopter for servicing and maintenance as required
- Assisting with general station duties when not flying
- Working alongside the ground crew to support the overall station operation

About you
Someone who enjoys being part of a team, is happy to get involved in all aspects of station life and understands that working on a cattle station involves much more than flying.
- Current CASA CPL(H) or PPL(H)
- Minimum 500 hours flying experience in an R22
- Previous cattle experience
- Good communication skills and the ability to work effectively as part of a team
- A genuine interest in cattle work and station life

What’s on offer
- Private accommodation and meals
- Approximately 600 flying hours per year
- Opportunities to work across properties in the Top End and Central Australia

How to apply
Email your cover letter and resume to jobs@annabrown.com.au or call for more information: Anna on 0427 644 527 or Kate on 0428 731 447.
