Helicopter Pilot / Grade 1 Flight Instructor-Queensland

South East Queensland · Posted 2026-07-07 · 👁 … views

Location: South East Queensland. Employment Type: Full-Time. Company: Executive Helicopters.

Join Executive Helicopters: Executive Helicopters is a leading commercial helicopter operator providing a diverse range of aviation services across South East Queensland and South West Queensland. With operational bases in both regions, we deliver professional helicopter services to commercial, government and private clients, with safety, reliability and customer service at the core of everything we do. We are seeking an experienced Commercial Helicopter Pilot who also holds a Grade 1 Flight Instructor Rating to join our team based at our South East Queensland base. This position is primarily a line pilot role with the added responsibility of conducting internal training, proficiency checks and pilot development within the company. It is not a dedicated flight training position. Travel between our operational bases and client locations may be required from time to time.

About the Role: Reporting to the Chief Pilot, the successful applicant will be responsible for: Conducting commercial helicopter operations in accordance with company procedures and CASA regulations; Delivering internal pilot training, proficiency checks and standardisation activities; Supporting pilot mentoring and ongoing professional development; Maintaining the highest standards of operational safety and professionalism; Completing operational documentation and company reporting requirements; Contributing to a positive safety culture and continuous improvement within the organisation; Representing Executive Helicopters professionally with clients and stakeholders.

Essential Requirements: Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter) (CPL(H)) or Air Transport Pilot Licence (Helicopter) (ATPL(H)); Grade 1 Helicopter Flight Instructor Rating; Minimum 3,000 hours total helicopter flight time; Current Class 1 Aviation Medical Certificate; Current Flight Review and Instructor Proficiency Check; Sound knowledge of CASA regulations and operational standards; Excellent communication and leadership skills; A strong commitment to safety, professionalism and teamwork; The ability to travel as required to support operations across both bases.

Desirable Experience: Charter operations; Aerial work; Utility and sling operations; Fire, emergency services or government contract operations; Experience operating Airbus AS350 and EC130 helicopters; Operational leadership or pilot mentoring.

What We Offer: A varied and rewarding flying role within a respected commercial helicopter operation; Competitive remuneration commensurate with experience; Diverse operational flying across South East and South West Queensland; A supportive team environment with a strong focus on safety and professionalism; Opportunities to contribute to operational excellence and pilot development.

Apply Now: If you are an experienced commercial helicopter pilot with a passion for operational excellence and developing fellow pilots, we would like to hear from you. Please submit your cover letter, resume and a summary of your flying experience, qualifications and endorsements. Applications will be treated in strict confidence.
