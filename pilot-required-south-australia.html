Pilot Required-South Australia

South Australia · Posted 2025-09-30 · 👁 … views

Helifarm is a diverse, well-established South Australian helicopter company that has been in operation for more than 30 years. We deliver a wide variety of aerial work services for many clients across southern Australia (mainly SA, Victoria & WA). We are looking for an experienced, positive and practical pilot to join our team. Essential: 1,500+ hrs PIC; Air work experience; Experience in R44's and B206 or AS350. Desirable experience: Mustering / aerial culling; Low-level surveys; Fire work; Sling work; Aerial application; Work in remote areas. Great team. Flexibility & variety. Full-time position available for the right person, good salary, training opportunities & leave entitlements. To be based in South Australia - preferably in the southeast. To start between November and March 2026. Please include contact details for two or three referees.

Apply via email: ering@helifarm.com.au
