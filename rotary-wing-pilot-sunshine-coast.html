Rotary Wing Pilot - Sunshine Coast

Sunshine Coast · Posted 2026-02-11 · 👁 … views

About LifeFlight: LifeFlight Australia is a leading aeromedical organisation dedicated to saving lives through rapid response medical services. Operating a fleet of helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, LifeFlight delivers critical care and emergency retrievals across Queensland and beyond. About the Role: We are seeking an experienced Rotary Wing (RW) Pilot interested in a Pilot-In-Command (PIC) opportunity to join our team based on the Sunshine Coast. This role operates on a 4 on / 4 off roster and is covered by the LifeFlight Australia Limited Rotary Wing Pilots Enterprise Agreement 2024. As a Rotary Wing PIC, your key accountabilities are to: Act as the Pilot-In-Command (PIC) according to the LifeFlight Australia (LFA) roster; Follow rostered and on-call duties as per company policies; Be prepared to operate as a PIC supporting the Q Health contract; Ensure compliance with safety protocols and statutory requirements; Identify, manage, and appropriately refer operational and safety risks; Implement and actively participate in the safety management system, workplace health and safety system, and risk management system; Monitor the currency of identified items nearing expiry; Ensure the serviceability and maintenance of operational ancillary equipment; Attend, participate in, and successfully complete all company operational, safety, fitness, and training sessions; Undergo necessary training for current and new business tasks and assist in mentoring new staff; Assist with media and public relations activities; Maintain effective crew coordination and cooperation in the multi-crew environment. About You: Strong communication skills; A safety-first attitude; ATPL (H); CIR ME with minimum 3 renewals (IPCs); NVFR(H); Minimum 2500 hours rotary wing; 1500 hours PIC Helicopter; 1200 hours Turbine Helicopter; 100 hours Instrument Flight; 100 hours Night PIC; AW139 Type Rating; 100 hours PIC on AW139 preferred; Dangerous Goods Certificate; HUET/EBS Certified; Class 1 medical; NVIS Rating; Previous EMS/SAR experience; Able to operate and proficient in both single pilot and multi pilot operations; Must live within 40-minute drive of Sunshine Coast Airport. Note: the successful applicant will be required to undergo background checks including a national police check. This position is only open to Australian Citizens, Permanent Residents, or those with unrestricted work rights in Australia. Eligible candidates will need to be fully vaccinated against MMR, Varicella, Pertussis, Hepatitis B and will be required to hold or obtain a Working with Children Blue Card (Queensland). Applications close on Thursday, 25 February 2026.

Apply via LifeFlight careers page: https://www.lifeflight.org.au/careers/aviation-careers/
