Senior Helicopter Flight Instructor-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2025-11-21 · 👁 … views

About Us
Utility Helicopters has embarked on an exciting journey of growth, with a strong focus on elevating our impact and setting new standards in aviation. We are recognised as the leader in helicopter training in Australia. It is our mission to deliver exceptional pilot training grounded in over 80 years of collective flying experience. We offer exclusive, specialised instruction in a tailored learning environment that prioritises safety, quality, and excellence, alongside our aerial work operations.

Guided by our vision to be a leader in aviation training, we're recognised for producing confident, skilled pilots who are setting the standard for excellence in the industry. Our culture is vibrant, welcoming and feels like family. We're a diverse and inclusive community where students from all walks of life come together to learn, grow, and succeed. We're passionate about creating a fun, supportive and safe environment where everyone feels valued and empowered to reach their fullest potential. We genuinely care about each student's journey and success, prioritising their well-being as we work to build a strong foundation for their future.

About the Role
We are seeking a full-time Grade 1 or 2 Senior Helicopter Flight Instructor to join our team and continue our contributing to our legacy of excellence. Reporting directly to the Director/Head of Operations.

In this pivotal role, you will lead and deliver high-quality flight instruction to our students, overseeing both theoretical and practical training with a strong commitment to aviation standards and safety regulations. As Senior Instructor, you'll play a key role in coordinating the team of instructors and providing mentorship to the junior instructors, conducting advanced training courses, and working closely with management to enhance training programs and drive continuous improvement initiatives.

Additionally, you'll serve as a culture champion, actively fostering and preserving the unique, supportive environment that makes our team special. Your leadership will help ensure that our positive, growth-focused culture continues to thrive for both instructors and students alike.

Key Responsibilities
- Deliver helicopter flight training in line with CASA regulations.
- Lead and support a team of Helicopter Instructors, providing coaching and mentorship to enhance their skills and performance.
- Conduct pre-flight and post-flight briefings, ensuring students understand goals and receive feedback.
- Assess student progress, adapting training to individual needs and safety standards.
- Lead and mentor Flight Instructors, supporting professional development and performance.
- Provide exceptional customer service, fostering a positive experience for students and clients.
- Ensure training and flight paperwork is accurately completed and organised.
- Monitor and maintain aircraft safety compliance and maintenance schedules.
- Track industry and CASA updates, ensuring policies align with current standards.
- Conduct EOM (End of Month) reporting for operations and training compliance.
- Promote a culture of safety through WH&S practices, training, and reporting.

About You
Required:
- CASA Commercial Pilot License – Helicopter
- Instructor Rating Grade 2 or higher
- Grade 1 Medical and ASIC
- Possess excellent communication skills, the ability to engage students in a practical and classroom setting, and have a strong understanding of helicopter theory concepts
- Be a confident and patient person, committed to providing a supportive learning environment for our students
- Ability to ensure a clean, organised environment, actively overseeing and supporting the team in maintaining aircraft and equipment to high standards.
- Professionally organised and approach in all aspects of job functionality
- Excellent communication skills; able to engage and articulate effectively with individuals at all levels, from students to senior management.

Advantageous:
- Mustering Training Endorsement
- Night Rating Training Endorsement
- Sling Rating Training Endorsement
- Design Feature Gas Turbine Training Endorsement
- Low-Level Training Endorsement
- Experience in the development and implementation of teaching and assessment plan

What's in it for you
- Access to our Flight Training Development Program (grading progression, further ratings and endorsements), the opportunity to conduct scenic flights and enjoy discounted rates on private hire for your own adventures!
- Support with developing your leadership capabilities
- A vibrant and welcoming culture, become part of the Utility family! Plenty of fun social activities.
- Commitment to implementing further engagement initiatives to continue to make Utility a great place to work!
- Work with a diverse group of individuals who share a common passion for aviation. We foster a collaborative and supportive environment, where expertise is valued, and everyone's input is encouraged.

Ready to apply?
If you're a dedicated instructor who's passionate about aviation, committed to student success, and eager to step into a leadership-focused role, we'd love to hear from you. This is a Monday to Friday full-time position, with occasional weekend flexibility required.
