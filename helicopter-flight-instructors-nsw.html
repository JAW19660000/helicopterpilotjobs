Helicopter Flight Instructors-NSW

NSW · Posted 2025-10-24 · 👁 … views

Aeropower is a leader in airborne electrical infrastructure, Maintenance and construction throughout Australia and internationally. We also operate two busy flight schools in Redcliffe, QLD and Bankstown, NSW.

We have an opening for Flight Instructors to join our busy flying school based at Bankstown Aerodrome, NSW. Part time and casual positions are available. This position involves regular weekend work.

Minimum Requirements: - CASA Flight Instructor rating with Grade 1, 2 or 3 training endorsement. - Current Class 1 aviation medical. - Current ASIC.

Preferred: - Cabri G2 experience - Low Level Training Endorsement - Experience operating independently without direct supervision (G2 and G1).

The ideal candidate is motivated and enthusiastic about training and passing on their knowledge and skills to the next generation of pilots. A firm grasp of the rules, regulations and CPL(H) theory knowledge is essential.

Applications via seek or to hoo@aeropower.com.au

Apply via SEEK (job 88081031) or email hoo@aeropower.com.au
