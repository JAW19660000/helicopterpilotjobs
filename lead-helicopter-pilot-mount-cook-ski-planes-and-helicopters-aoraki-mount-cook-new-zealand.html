Lead Helicopter Pilot - Mount Cook Ski Planes and Helicopters-Aoraki Mount Cook, New Zealand

Aoraki Mount Cook, New Zealand · Posted 2026-05-06 · 👁 … views

Mount Cook Ski Planes & Helicopters have an exceptional opportunity for an experienced and respected aviation professional to step into a Lead Base Pilot position in one of New Zealand's most dynamic alpine environments. This is a senior role suited to a mature, career-focused pilot seeking long-term stability, leadership responsibility, and the opportunity to shape and support a high-performing operation. Based at Mount Cook Aerodrome, you will lead day-to-day flying operations at base level, setting the standard for professionalism, safety, and operational excellence across the pilot group. About the role: Operating a fleet of AS350 and EC130 helicopters, with a strong focus on tourism, our operations include: Scenic flights with glacier landings; Heli-hiking and climbing support; Aerial work including long-lining; Charter, filming and photography. As Lead Base Pilot, your responsibilities will extend beyond flying to include: Providing strong, visible leadership to the pilot team; Mentoring and developing line pilots; Acting as the key pilot liaison with external stakeholders; Relevant experience around NZ CAA regulatory compliance and working within a mature aviation Safety Management System; Operational planning, rostering and continuous improvement; Promoting a professional, safety-first culture across all operations. You will play a critical role in maintaining operational standards while fostering a positive, cohesive team environment. About you: You are an experienced helicopter pilot with a calm, professional approach and a reputation for sound judgement. You bring a leadership mindset and take pride in supporting others to succeed. You will demonstrate: Strong leadership and mentoring capability; Excellent communication and interpersonal skills; Confidence engaging with industry stakeholders; A high level of professionalism and personal presentation; The ability to make sound decisions in a dynamic operational environment; A long-term commitment to the role and location. Minimum requirements: CAA NZ CPL(H); Current Class 1 Medical; 3000+ hours total time (guideline, experience-dependent); Significant AS350/H130 experience; Proven mountain flying and alpine landing experience; Demonstrated operational maturity and decision-making capability. Highly desirable: Instructors rating; Previous experience in the Aoraki / Westland National Parks or similar environment; Prior leadership or senior pilot experience; Experience with long-line and external load operations; Exposure to audit, compliance, or SMS processes. About the INFLITE Group: We are part of the INFLITE Group—an organisation committed to excellence and recognised as a leader in New Zealand's tourism industry. With continued growth and investment across multiple bases, we offer long-term career opportunities within a stable and forward-looking business. Our brands include Heletranz Helicopters, Mount Cook Ski Planes & Helicopters, Nelson Tasman Air, and Skydive operations across the South Island. What we offer: A senior leadership position within a respected operation; Long-term, stable employment in a world-class environment; A professional, supportive team culture; Competitive remuneration package aligned with experience. Please note: Applicants must hold a CAA NZ licence and have the legal right to work in New Zealand. If you're looking to step into a role where your experience, leadership, and judgement truly matter, we'd like to hear from you. Please send a current CV with cover letter via the application link below. PLEASE NOTE: Applicants who do not have a CAA NZ licence, work rights in NZ or the minimum requirements may not be responded to.
