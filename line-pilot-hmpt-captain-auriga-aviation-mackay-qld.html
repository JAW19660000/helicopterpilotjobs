Line Pilot | HMPT Captain-Mackay QLD

Mackay, Mackay & Coalfields QLD · Posted 2026-09-22 · 👁 … views

Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide.

Auriga Aviation is seeking suitably qualified HMPT Captains to join their Flight Operations team working from Mackay, Queensland. Reporting to the Head of Flight Operations, this position is responsible for the safe and efficient operation of company aircraft conducting Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer operations.

Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) operations in accordance with company procedures and CASA regulations
- Ensure the safe and efficient operation of company aircraft
- Maintain a high standard of airmanship, professionalism and customer service
- Complete all required documentation, flight and duty records accurately and on time
- Participate in training, checking and ongoing professional development
- Work collaboratively with the wider Flight Operations team to support scheduling and operational requirements

Minimum Qualifications:
- ATPL(H) or CPL(H)
- Minimum 2,000 hours total helicopter time
- Type Rating on EC135 or BK117 D-2
- Current Class 1 Medical
- Multi-Engine Helicopter Instrument Rating highly regarded

Key Skills and Attributes:
- Strong communication and teamwork skills
- High attention to detail and commitment to safety
- Ability to work variable rosters including nights
- Professional presentation and customer focus
- Sound understanding of CASA regulations relevant to HMPT operations

What We Offer:
Auriga Aviation offers a supportive team environment, a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, and the opportunity to build a career with a recognised leader in Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer services across Australia.

Apply now with your resume and cover letter outlining your suitability for the role.
