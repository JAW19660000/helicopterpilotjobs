B407 VRLL Aerial Work Pilot – Indonesia

Indonesia (Bali) · Posted 2026-06-02 · 👁 … views

B407 HELICOPTER PILOT. Company: SGI. Location: Bali, Indonesia. Employment Type: Contract. SGI is currently looking for a professional and experienced Bell 407 VRLL Pilot to strengthen our flight operations team. As a Bell 407 Pilot, you will play a critical role in delivering safe and efficient aerial support for our operations, ranging from utility missions to personnel transport in various environments. Key Responsibilities: Operate the Bell 407 helicopter in compliance with DGCA regulations, company SOPs, and safety standards; Conduct safe flight operations in a diverse and challenging terrain; Ensure rigorous pre-flight and post-flight inspections; Maintain clear communication with flight dispatch, maintenance, and ground support teams; Demonstrate exceptional decision-making in remote and variable weather conditions. Requirements: License: Valid CPL-H (or ATPL-H); Rating: Current Bell 407 Type Rating with 100hr on type; Total Flight Time: Minimum 1,500+ hours total helicopter time; Turbine Time: Minimum 500+ hours on single-engine turbine helicopters; Experience: Minimum 300 hr VRLL experience of which 100hr must be precision VRLL, High altitude operational experience (8000+ft); Medical: Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate; Language: Proficient in English (ICAO Level 4 minimum); Safety Mindset: Proven track record of adhering to high safety standards and operational discipline. What We Offer: Competitive compensation package in line with industry standards; Exposure to diverse and challenging flight missions; A collaborative work environment that prioritizes safety and professional integrity.

Apply via email: talent.hub@sgi-aero.com (Subject: Application for B407 Pilot – [Full Name])
