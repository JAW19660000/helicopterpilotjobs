Mustering Pilot Required-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-07-22 · 👁 … views

Experienced Mustering Pilot. Join the Team at Diamond Helicopters. Operating a fleet of Robinson R22s and an R44, we provide helicopter services across North West Queensland. While our core business is aerial mustering, we also undertake a range of air work throughout the year, making this a varied and rewarding role. This role offers more than just great flying. Based in the Gulf, you'll be part of a genuine outback lifestyle, with the opportunity to explore some of Australia's premier cattle country and enjoy some of the best fishing the region has to offer.

The Role: Primarily aerial mustering; Robinson R22 and R44 operations; Sling/underslung operations; General air work as opportunities arise; Opportunity to assist with station work between flying when required.

Essential Requirements: Current CPL(H); Current Class 1 Medical; Current R22 endorsement; Current mustering endorsement; Proven aerial mustering experience; Strong work ethic and commitment to safe operations; Ability to work independently and as part of a team in remote environments.

Desirable: R44 endorsement; Sling endorsement; Station experience.

What We Offer: Competitive remuneration based on experience; Long-term opportunity with a growing helicopter business; A varied role with more than just seasonal mustering; Flying across some of Australia's premier cattle country; Genuine outback lifestyle; Start date negotiable.

We're looking for someone who takes pride in their flying, enjoys working as part of a small team, and is keen to build a long-term career with Diamond Helicopters. For a confidential discussion or to apply, please contact: Jack Clarke, M: 0488 117 593, E: jack@diamondhelicopters.com.au
