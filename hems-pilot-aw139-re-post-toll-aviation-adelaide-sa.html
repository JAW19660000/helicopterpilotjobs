HEMS Pilot (AW139) RE-POST-Adelaide SA

Adelaide, SA · Posted 2026-11-27 · 👁 … views

RE-POST – this role has been re-advertised by Toll Aviation.

Toll Aviation, part of the Toll Group, operates on a global scale and is looking for an experienced pilot to join the State Police, Ambulance and Rescue Aviation Services (SPARAS) contract based in Adelaide, SA. Toll Aviation’s team of pilots, aircrew, engineers and safety managers has wide experience in a diverse range of operations in challenging conditions, including remote, maritime and mountainous locations, in all weather, day or night, providing safe, reliable, mission-critical aeromedical and police services to South Australia.

The role
A line pilot position on Toll Aviation’s upcoming HEMS contract in Adelaide, SA. Reporting to the Senior Contract Pilot, the position works on an equal time roster. Commencement is expected in March 2027, and employment is pursuant to the relevant Toll Aviation Pilot’s Enterprise Agreement. Full time.

Qualifications / experience
- Excellent crew resource management and communication skills
- CASA Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter) or ATPL(H) with the appropriate endorsements and ratings to act as pilot in command of all HEMS operations authorised by the Operator’s certificate
- 2,000 hours total flying experience
- 1,500 hours pilot in command
- 500 hours PIC turbine rotary wing
- NVFR rating with minimum 200 hours at night
- NVIS Rating Grade 1 with minimum 40 hours NVIS as PIC
- Multi-engine command instrument rating with minimum 3 renewals
- CASA Class 1 medical
- Comprehensive night, winch and IFR experience in a multi-crew environment

Highly desirable
- AW139 type endorsement with minimum 100 hours PIC relevant experience on type
- EMS/SAR experience on AW139

Also required
- Medical and police checks
- Hold, or be able to obtain, an Australian Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC)
- Ability to apply for and obtain an NV1 security clearance if required
- Variations from the minimum qualification and experience requirements remain at the State’s discretion, case by case

You will be rewarded with an attractive salary and the security of joining a global organisation willing to explore opportunities both within Australia and internationally.
