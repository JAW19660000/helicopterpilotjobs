S92 Offshore Touring Copilot — Fixed Term Contract

Who we are: Offshore Services Australasia (OSA) is the leading supplier of helicopter services to the Australian Offshore industry. We operate multiple helicopter types from several bases throughout Australia providing support to several major oil and gas producers.

About the Role: We are currently seeking applications from suitably qualified and experienced pilots for S92 Copilot positions within our Offshore Operations.

Essential Criteria:
- Valid CPL(H)
- S92 Type Rating
- Helicopter Instrument Rating
- Minimum 1000 flying hours
- Minimum 500 multi-engine hours
- Minimum 100 flying hours Pilot-In-Command (PIC)
- Ability to obtain and maintain a CASA Class 1 Medical and Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC)

Desirable:
- ATPL(H)
- Low Level Rating
- Offshore helicopter flying experience in topographical area

Key Attributes:
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills
- Self-motivated with a results-oriented approach
- Positive attitude and strong team orientation
- Excellent collaboration and relationship-building skills
- Ability to work in a dynamic team environment

Applicants must have the legal right to work in Australia and meet any clearance requirements relevant to the role.
