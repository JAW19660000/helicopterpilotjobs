Multi-Engine IFR Rotary Wing Pilot — Marine Operations. Aero Logistics. Newcastle, Newcastle, Maitland & Hunter NSW. Aviation Services (Manufacturing, Transport & Logistics). Expression of Interest. Aero Logistics is Australia's leading provider in scenic, passenger charter, and specialised aerial work aircraft. BARS Gold Standard and ISO Accredited, we pride ourselves on our core values – Safety, Value and Integrity, upholding them to all aspects of our operations, ensuring our customers receive the highest standards of service. With a diverse fleet, including the AS350B3, Bell 407, Bell 206/L, and multi engine capabilities, Aero Logistics offers exciting career opportunities for Pilots at all stages of their career. Providing specialist aircraft services across firefighting, aerial work, and marine operations, we are at the forefront of specialised aviation aerial services in Australia. We are currently seeking Expressions of Interest from highly experienced and qualified Multi-Engine IFR Rotary Wing Pilots with specific expertise in marine and ship-based operations, to become a part of our Rotary Wing team based in Newcastle, NSW. We are seeking a dynamic and progressive team player who thrives on challenges and excels in a demanding, high-tempo operational environment. What Will I Be Doing? Reporting directly to the Rotary Wing Head of Flying Operations & Head of Operations, you will be supporting them by: Conducting multi-engine IFR rotary wing operations in the marine environment; Performing ship deck landings and associated maritime tasking; Conducting Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS) operations; Maintaining precise operational and flight records; Participating in ongoing operational readiness and currency requirements; Supporting continuous improvement in rotary wing pilot operations. Let's Hear About You. We are particularly interested to hear from you if you have: Minimum Requirements: CASA CPL(H); Night VFR qualification with proficiency on VOR, ADF, and GPS; CASA Multi-Engine Command Instrument Rating (MECIR); Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS) Rating; 1,500+ total flying hours — Rotary Wing; 1,000+ hours PIC — Rotary Wing; 500+ hours PIC — Turbine; 50+ flying hours on type (EC135); 100+ hours PIC Night Flight, with a minimum of 50 hours flown under NVIS; HUET / EBS; Full, unrestricted working rights for Australia. Desirable Qualifications and Experience: Ship/deck landing experience — minimum 10 ship landings by day; Ship/deck landing experience by night; Willingness to relocate to the Newcastle/Hunter Valley NSW region. Let's Reveal The Perks: Full Time Employment position available; Roster negotiable; Dynamic and progressive environment; Company culture centred around safety, values, integrity, continuous improvement, collaboration and innovation; Commencing salary set in line with skills and experience, annual reviews available via Performance Review process; Uniform provided. We are committed to being an employer of choice. We will do what we can to help you develop your skills, and make a difference, right from the start. We have a flexible workplace and focus on all aspects of your physical and mental health and safety. We want you to be with us for the long-term, so providing you with rich career experiences and ongoing development is our priority. Choose the Aero Logistics Way. We know that our people will shape the future of the business. That's why we're passionate about providing a workplace that empowers our staff to be safety, value and integrity focussed. When you join the team at Aero Logistics, you're joining a group of people with a commitment to make the business even better. We're proud of the aviation services we provide, the work we do, and know you will be too! If you are a highly experienced and qualified Multi-Engine IFR Rotary Wing Pilot with expertise in marine and ship-based operations, looking to contribute to the operation of a diverse and extensive fleet, we would love to hear from you! Your positive attitude, senior experience and organisation skills will be valued to build and maintain strong working relationships with rotary wing operations and business leaders. Join Aero Logistics and be part of a Team Dedicated to Excellence in Aviation Aerial Service: Upload a Cover Letter, mentioning the specific role you are applying for and share your experiences relating to the role's needs; Upload a current brief Resume – include details of aircraft types flown, total applicable flight hours and relevant experience; Successful applicants must have a commitment to safety and operational procedures; Applicants must have no restrictions to live and work Full Time in Australia; As part of Aero Logistics' recruitment process, prior to an offer of employment being made, recommended candidates may be subject to a medical assessment which may include alcohol and other drugs testing. Please Note: This is an Expression of Interest only. Submissions will be reviewed and shortlisted candidates contacted as opportunities arise. Only shortlisted candidates may be contacted. We appreciate your patience throughout the process. We will not be accepting applications from recruitment consultants/agencies.
