EMS/Rescue Helicopter Pilot-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2026-03-06 · 👁 … views

Rescue Helicopter Pilot | Kaiurungi Toparere Rauora. Who are we? At Search & Rescue Services Ltd we are a high-performing team of paramedics, pilots and aircrew dedicated to delivering lifesaving care across the Central and Lower North Island, 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Through critical medical transport and rescue operations, we uphold the highest standards in both aviation and paramedicine, supported by a strong commitment to continuous training and professional excellence. Join an organisation where safety, clinical and operational excellence, and genuine service to our communities sit at the heart of everything we do.

The nature of the Position: We are looking for a fulltime EMS/Rescue Helicopter Pilot to join our team at our Taranaki Base on an equal-time 4 on 4 off 12 Hr roster. This is an exciting opportunity to use your skills to make a real difference for the communities that we serve.

We offer: A competitive salary; Great work life balance; The opportunity to contribute and give back to the community; Meaningful work with a team of highly skilled, experienced and passionate professionals.

Essential skills/experiences for the Role - minimum: NZ CPL(H); 2000 hours total time PIC (or equivalent); Unrestricted night VFR rating; Full NZ Class 1 medical certificate; Twin turbine engine experience; Hoist/Sling experience; IFR / SPIFR (FW Rating min).

Preference will be given to applicants with: H145 rating; NZ ATPL(H); EMS experience; NVIS experience; CRM; Mountain experience; HUET; Dangerous Goods Certificate; Air Maestro experience.

As part of your employment, you will be required to complete a Police Check. SRSL is an equal opportunity employer, committed to diversity and inclusion within our workforce.

Apply by email: Tarrin Terry, Director People & Culture, tarrin.terry@srsl.org.nz (Applications close 20 March 2026)
