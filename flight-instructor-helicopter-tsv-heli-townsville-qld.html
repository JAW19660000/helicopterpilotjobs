Flight Instructor, Helicopter-Townsville QLD

Townsville, Northern QLD · Posted 2026-10-08 · 👁 … views

TSV Heli is a CASA authorised pilot training facility with our main operating base in Townsville, North Queensland. The Flight Training School operates from our established, private facility adjacent to the city’s main airport. We offer rotary and fixed wing flight training, from ab-initio to advanced, as well as charter and aerial work. We are supported by an in-house engineering department.

What we are looking for
A professional pilot with a passion for mentoring and supporting the next generation of aviation professionals.

What we offer
- Supported internal training
- Relaxed, professional environment
- Well maintained, modern fleet encompassing R22, R44, AS350, and EC135
- Diverse training environments & line pilot opportunities
- Aerial work and Charter opportunities
- Ongoing Professional Pilot development
- The ability to be home most nights and weekends, occasional travel may be required

Flight Instructor mandatory requirements
- CPL(H)
- Class 1 Medical
- Current ASIC
- Flight Instructor Rating
- Working with Children - Blue Card (or ability to obtain)
- HUET
- DAMP
- Dangerous Goods
- Australian Citizen or Permanent Resident

TSV Heli is an equal opportunity employer and encourages applicants from all genders and ethnic backgrounds to apply.
