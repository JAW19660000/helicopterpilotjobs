Helicopter Pilot Exams

Free theory exams and proficiency checks for helicopter pilots.

Free

No charge to sit any exam.

Works offline

Your exam is a single file you open in your browser. No internet needed once you have it.

Marked straight away

Multiple-choice questions are scored instantly. Written answers are reviewed by the examiner.

How it works

  1. Email your name and the exam you need to jason.walburn@bigpond.com.
  2. You’re emailed your personal exam file, with your username and password.
  3. Save the file to your computer and open it in your browser. Sign in and answer the questions.
  4. When you finish, email your result to the examiner.

Terms and privacy

Terms

  • Free: the exams are free to sit. They are provided by Jason Walburn.
  • Personal exam file: your exam file and login are for you only. Please don’t share them.
  • Training and checking only: results are for training and checking purposes. They are not a CASA licence, rating or approval.
  • Your rights: nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Privacy

  • What I collect: your name, email address, the exam you request and the result you email back.
  • How it’s used: to supply your exam, mark it and keep training and checking records. I don’t sell your details or share them, except where the law requires.
  • On your computer: your answers are saved in your own browser while you sit the exam.
  • Visit counts: this site uses Google Analytics and a simple tap counter to measure visits. They do not collect your name or email address.
  • Questions or deletion: email jason.walburn@bigpond.com to see, correct or delete your details.
