Flight Instructor/Pilot Required-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2025-08-24 · 👁 … views

About Wanaka Helicopters Group: Wanaka Helicopters Group is a family-owned commercial operator and flight training school based in Wanaka, New Zealand. With experience of over 40 years, we specialise in scenic and luxury flights to some of the most beautiful and remote locations in New Zealand. As a family business, we value all employees as an extension of our family. Our pilots are our ambassadors, privileged to share their aviation journeys and our spectacular backyard with guests and students from around the world. Alongside tourism, we provide commercial, agricultural, and charter services and operate the largest and most experienced helicopter flight training school in Australasia. We offer both private training and a Diploma in Aviation and are a NZQA certified provider, we operate under CAA Parts 119/135/137 and 141. Our group of companies includes: Wanaka Helicopters - scenic and luxury flights, commercial work and flight school; Backcountry Helicopters - based in Makarora, tourism-focused scenic flights and commercial work; Learn to Fly NZ - fixed-wing training facility offering both private and commercial licences. The opportunity: We are offering a rare opportunity to join our team of professional, friendly, charismatic, and highly skilled pilots as a full-time Instructor/Pilot. This role requires a passion for aviation and teaching, along with a positive, team-focused attitude and commitment to excellence. About you, Requirements: NZ CPL (H) with current Class 1 medical; Minimum 350 hours PIC (Helicopter); C, B or A category instructor rating; Type ratings on R22 & Cabri (R44, MD500, and AS350 preferred); Full driver's licence. Preferably with: CTO/ATO operations experience, especially in mountainous terrain; Flight and ground instruction experience; HUET certification; Current First Aid certification; CRM qualifications. Key selection criteria: Strong instruction and teaching skills with the ability to set a professional example; Experience as a flight and ground instructor; Commitment to developing skills in flight training and Part 119/135 operations; Proven business acumen and IT competency; Ability to work independently and manage time effectively; Excellent organisational skills and forward planning abilities; In-depth knowledge of relevant CAA rule parts; Strong customer service orientation with the ability to work with diverse clientele; Flexible, 'can-do' attitude, willing to assist in all areas of the business as required. What we offer: Attractive salary package, commensurate with experience; Opportunity to live, work and fly in Wanaka, New Zealand's most stunning region; A supportive, family-oriented, and close-knit workplace culture. How to apply: If you're ready to take the next step in your aviation career and join our growing team, please apply via seek with your CV and Cover Letter or for more information, contact Pete or Jon on 03 443 1085. Applications close 14 September 2025, unless filled prior.

Apply via SEEK: https://www.seek.co.nz/job/86662149 (or contact Pete or Jon on 03 443 1085)
