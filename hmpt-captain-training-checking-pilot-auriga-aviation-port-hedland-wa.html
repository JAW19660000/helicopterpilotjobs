HMPT Captain / Training and Checking Pilot-Port Hedland WA

Port Hedland WA · Posted 2026-10-02 · 👁 … views

Auriga Aviation is a recognised leader in delivering specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services across Australia. We operate a modern fleet of Airbus helicopters, providing safe and efficient solutions to the maritime industry and servicing a diverse client base at multiple locations nationwide. With over 140 years of combined operational experience, Auriga Aviation is committed to the highest standards of safety, compliance, and reliability.

Auriga Aviation is seeking a suitably qualified HMPT Captain and Training and Checking Pilot to join our Flight Operations team in Port Hedland. The role is touring to Port Hedland, WA.

We are seeking a Training and Checking Captain who demonstrates an exceptional commitment to safety and operational excellence, coupled with a collaborative mindset and the ability to communicate clearly and lead effectively.

Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct training and checking duties to support crew development in the delivery of HMPT services
- Act as a Captain for H135 HMPT operations, ensuring the highest standards of safety and professionalism
- Conduct marine pilot transfers and various aerial work operations with a focus on safety and professionalism on an equal-time roster
- Collaborate with our passionate team to deliver outstanding customer experiences
- Engage in continuous training and development to enhance your skills and advance your career

Minimum Qualifications and Experience:
- Current CASA Class 1 Medical Certificate
- 2000 hrs Total Time Helicopter
- 1000 hrs PIC Helicopter
- 100 hrs EC135
- Instrument Rating MEH with 2D/3D endorsements
- NVIS Rating
- 50 hours NVIS flight time
- Flight Instructor Rating Grade 2 or Grade 1
- Training Endorsements for IF and NVIS

Desired Qualifications:
- Current HUET, DG Awareness
- Previous maritime/overwater experience
- Flight Examiner Rating
- Previous CASR Part 133/138 TCS Training and Checking Experience
