Line Pilot-NSW

NSW · Posted 2026-07-07 · 👁 … views

R44 Helicopter Pilot. Location: Narrabri NSW. Company: Northwest Helicopters. Position Type: Full-Time/Contract. Start Date: ASAP.

About Us: Northwest Helicopters is a growing operator providing aerial services including Feral animal control, charter and low-level survey work. We value precision, safety, and professionalism. As we expand, we are looking for a dedicated and safety-conscious R44 Helicopter Pilot to join our team.

The Role: This position involves a variety of operational flying, including: Low-level aerial survey; Various Charter; Feral Animal Control; Maintain aircraft and hangar cleanliness; General R44 operations in regional and remote areas. This is a full-time role with most flying conducted locally, so you'll be home most nights.

Minimum Requirements: Valid Commercial Pilot Licence – Helicopter (CPL-H); R44 Type Rating; 250+ Hours Total Time; Demonstrated low-level flight experience; Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate; Completed Dangerous Goods (DG) awareness course; Valid First Aid certificate; Valid ASIC Card; Current DAMP Test; Nil Accident history; Valid Manual Drivers licence; Strong communication skills and situational awareness; Ability to work independently and in remote field conditions.

Desirable Skills/Experience: Low level flying e.g. Aerial Culling, Mustering, Surveys; Firearms licence/Firearms Experience; Basic Mechanical knowledge/Helicopter Servicing e.g. 50hr Maintenance Authority; Positive attitude and a strong commitment to safety and teamwork; Willingness to assist with ground operations when required; Ability to work independently and always mindful of the company's best interests; Female applicants strongly encouraged.

We Offer: Industry-competitive salary with room for growth; A safety-first culture and supportive team; Exposure to varied operational flying and real-world experience; Based in Narrabri, great community with modern facilities.

How to Apply: To apply, please submit your resume, cover letter, and flight hours breakdown.
