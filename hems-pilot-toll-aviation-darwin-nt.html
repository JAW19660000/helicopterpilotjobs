HEMS Pilot-Darwin NT

Darwin NT · Posted 2026-10-19 · 👁 … views

Toll Aviation (Toll Group) is seeking an expression of interest for residual Helicopter Pilot positions (residential) supporting its Top End Medical Retrieval Service (TEMRS) contract in Darwin, NT, providing safe, reliable aeromedical services to Northern Territory Government agencies. Salary described as “attractive”. Applicants who partly meet the requirements are encouraged to apply.

Requirements
- Excellent crew resource management and communication skills
- CASA Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter) with the endorsements and ratings to act as pilot in command on all TEMRS operations authorised by the Operator’s certificate
- 2,500 hours flight experience
- 1,500 hours PIC helicopter
- 1,200 hours turbine rotary wing
- NVG endorsement
- Multi-engine command instrument rating with minimum 2 renewals
- CASA Class 1 Medical
- Comprehensive night, winch and IFR experience
- Entitled to work in Australia; pre-employment checks including criminal history and medical

Highly desirable
- AW139 type endorsement with minimum 100 hours PIC on type
- EMS/SAR experience on AW139
- HUET/EBS qualification
- ATPL(H)

How to apply
Apply online via Toll Careers: tollgroup.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com (www.careers.tollgroup.com)
