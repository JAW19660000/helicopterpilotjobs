HEMS Pilot-Darwin NT

Darwin NT · Posted 2026-10-19 · 👁 … views

Toll Aviation (Toll Group) is recruiting residual Aircrew Officer positions supporting its Top End Medical Retrieval Service (TEMRS) contract in Darwin, NT, providing aeromedical services to Northern Territory Government agencies. Advertised by Toll as “HEMS Pilot | Darwin”. Salary described as “attractive”. Applicants who partly meet the requirements are encouraged to apply.

Requirements
- Excellent crew resource management and communication skills
- Minimum 500 hours rotary-wing experience as a winch operator / aircrew officer
- NVG endorsement with at least 50 hours ACO NVIS
- AVI40119 Certificate IV in Aviation (Aircrew Officer) or AVI40225 Certificate IV in Airborne Operations
- CASA Class 2 Aircrew Medical
- Comprehensive winching, night and IFR ACO FLHS experience
- Dangerous Goods Awareness Certificate
- Aviation Radio Operator Licence, including Level 6 English language proficiency
- Ability to pass the Toll Aviation ACO fitness test
- Entitled to work in Australia; pre-employment checks including criminal history and medical

Highly desirable
- AW139 type endorsement with relevant experience
- EMS/SAR experience on AW139
- HUET/EBS qualification

How to apply
Apply online via Toll Careers: tollgroup.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com (www.careers.tollgroup.com)
