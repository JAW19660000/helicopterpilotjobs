Helicopter Pilot - HEMS-Jandakot, Perth WA

Jandakot, Perth WA · Posted 2026-10-20 · 👁 … views

Heliwest is a Western Australian helicopter company conducting SAR/HEMS, air transport, aerial work, training and mining support (fleet: BK117, EC135, AS350, B206). It is seeking expressions of interest for HEMS Captains and Copilots to fly the BK117 on its HEMS operation in the Pilbara region, WA, based at Newman or mining camps on a fly-in fly-out 15/13 roster.

Duties
- Conducting HEMS, aeromedical retrieval and emergency response missions
- Operating into remote Western Australian mining country
- Working closely with medical crews and emergency services
- Aircraft preparation, mission planning and day-to-day operational requirements

Captains – mandatory
- CPL(H) and NVFR(H)
- 2,500 hours helicopter, 1,500 hours PIC helicopter
- 500 hours PIC multi-engine helicopter
- 100 hours PIC BK117/EC145
- Multi-engine command instrument rating
- NVIS rating

Copilots – mandatory
- CPL(H) and NVFR(H) (minimum 20 hours)
- 1,000 hours helicopter, 500 hours turbine helicopter
- 50 hours multi-engine helicopter
- Multi-engine command instrument rating

Desirable
- ATPL(H)
- EC145 type rating
- Training and check experience (Perth based)
- HUET/EBS and MCC

Candidates approaching the requisite qualifications may be assisted in completing ratings or endorsements if deemed suitable.
