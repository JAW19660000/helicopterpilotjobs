Helicopter Flight Instructor - Base Manager - Sydney

Sydney · Posted 2026-01-15 · 👁 … views

Aeropower is a leader in airborne electrical infrastructure, Maintenance and construction throughout Australia and internationally. We also operate two busy flight schools in Redcliffe, QLD and Bankstown, NSW. We have an opening for a Flight Instructor/Base Manager to join our busy flying school based at Bankstown Aerodrome, NSW. This position involves regular weekend work. Minimum Requirements: CASA Flight Instructor rating with Grade 1 training endorsement. Low Level Training Endorsement. Current Class 1 aviation medical. Current ASIC. Preferred: Cabri G2 experience; Instructor rating training endorsement; Experience running operations outside of direct supervision; Experience supervising junior instructors. The ideal candidate is motivated and enthusiastic about training and passing on their knowledge and skills to the next generation of pilots. A firm grasp of the rules, regulations and CPL(H) theory knowledge is essential.

Apply via SEEK or email: https://www.seek.com.au/job/89704584 or hoo@aeropower.com.au
