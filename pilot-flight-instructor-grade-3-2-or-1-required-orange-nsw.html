Pilot/Flight Instructor (Grade 3, 2, or 1)Required-Orange, NSW

Orange, NSW · Posted 2025-10-01 · 👁 … views

Role overview Orange Helicopters is seeking qualified flight instructors at all levels (Grade 3, Grade 2, or Grade 1) for a full time or part time position. The position offers a stable Monday–Friday schedule with occasional weekend work for scenic flights. Our fleet includes R22, R44, B206-L, B407, and AS350, providing diverse instructional opportunities and exposure to multiple rotor-craft platforms.

Key details - Position type: Permanent Full-time or Part time or Contract work available - Schedule: Monday–Friday; occasional weekends for scenic flights - Location: Orange Helicopters - Fleet: R22, R44, B206-L, B407, AS350 - Career path: Opportunity to advance into utility work

Compensation - Salary commensurate with experience

Required minimums - Commercial Pilot License (Helicopters) CPL(H) - Class 1 medical - Grade 3 Flight Instructor Rating (FIR) or higher - Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC)

Desirable qualifications - Low Level (H) endorsement - Aerial mustering helicopter (AMH) - Gas turbine endorsement (GTE)

What we offer - Clear pathways into utility roles and higher-level flight operations - Structured on-boarding and ongoing professional development - Competitive compensation aligned with experience and qualifications

How to apply Please submit your resume and a cover letter.

Apply via email to dean@orangehelicopters.com.au
