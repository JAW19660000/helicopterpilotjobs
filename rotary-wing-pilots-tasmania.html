Rotary Wing Pilots-Tasmania

Tasmania · Posted 2025-08-27 · 👁 … views

StarFlight Australia — a joint venture between LifeFlight Australia, and Linfox Group—delivers specialist helicopter operations for Medical Transport, Search and Rescue, Aerial Law enforcement, and Aerial firefighting across Australia. Our skilled crews and state-of-the-art aircraft operate to the highest safety and performance standards possible, ensuring vital missions are carried out with precision, professionalism, and care.

About the Role Our team is growing and we are seeking expressions of interest from RW pilots who wish to be considered for PIC at our Hobart/Cambridge base. Working a 4/4 roster, we are seeking highly skilled, and outcome focused pilots to deliver rapid aeromedical interventions, police response and search & rescue operations in Australia's most challenging environment. From day one, you'll serve as the vital link in Tasmania's health and Police network, saving lives and improving patient outcomes for isolated regional and remote communities.

As Rotary Wing PIC, your key accountabilities are to: - Act as the Pilot-In-Command (PIC) in accordance with the StarFlight Australia (SFA) AOC and Exposition, adhering to published administrative procedures and company policies. - Maintaining crew co-ordination and co-operation in a multi-crew environment. - Ensure compliance with safety protocols and statutory requirements for the safe, effective, and efficient operation of company aircraft. - Be prepared to fully support the Tasmanian Emergency Air Services (TEAS) contract, including adhering to community health guidelines where required. - Prioritise the well-being and safety of all staff, aircrew, passengers, and patients. - Identify, manage, and appropriately refer operational and safety risks in accordance with company policy and procedures. - Implement and actively participate in the safety management system, workplace health and safety system, and risk management system. - Monitor the currency of identified items nearing expiry and bring them to the attention of the appropriate manager. - Ensure the serviceability and maintenance of issued personal protective equipment and tools required for the conduct of flight or ground operations. - Attend, participate in, and successfully complete all company operational, safety, fitness, and training sessions, including government contractual requirements. - Undergo necessary training for current and new business tasks and assist in mentoring non-company staff where required. - Assist with media and public relations activities, fostering goodwill with the public at all times.

About You To be successful in this role, you will have the following qualifications, skills and experience: - Strong communication skills - A safety-first attitude - Enthusiasm and a drive to achieve and maintain the highest level of competency for Medical Transport and Emergency Service Operations - Be fully vaccinated against MMR (measles, mumps, rubella), Varicella, Pertussis, Hepatitis B - Hold or able to obtain a Registration to Work with Vulnerable People (RWVP) Tasmania - You must be domiciled within 2 hours drive of Hobart - ATPL(H) or CPL(H) with ATPL(H) subjects - CIR MEH with minimum 3 renewals (IPCs) - NVFR(H) - NVIS(H) - LL-SO - LL-WR - >2500 hours Helicopter - >2000 hours PIC Helicopter - >1500 hours PIC Turbine Helicopter - >500 hours PIC Multi-Engine Helicopter - >100 hours under the IFR - >100 hours PIC Night Unaided - >100 hours PIC on B412 or BK117/H145 - >30 hours NVIS - Class 1 medical - Dangerous Goods Certificate - HUET/EBS (preferred) - Previous SAR/ALE experience (preferred)

What's in it for you? Work/Life Balance: Enjoy genuine work-life balance with a predictable 4-on/4-off roster that lets you plan life around your schedule. Based in Hobart, you'll enjoy the best of Tasmania—spectacular hikes, pristine beaches, a thriving food and wine scene, plus great schools, affordable housing, and a safe, welcoming community—so you and your family can make the most of your downtime in one of Australia's most liveable cities.

Operational Excellence: StarFlight Australia is a partnership between LifeFlight Australia and the Linfox group of companies, combining aviation, medical, logistics, and transport expertise with a shared mission: saving lives and serving communities.

Meaningful Impact: You will have an opportunity to make a meaningful impact on people's lives. The organisation is dedicated to saving lives, delivering critical care services, and responding to emergencies.

Collaboration & Support: We are committed to fostering a culture of camaraderie and teamwork.

Salary Packaging Options: As an employee, you have the opportunity to benefit from salary packaging, allowing you to allocate up to $15,900 per Fringe Benefit Tax (FBT) year towards general living expenses. This includes covering various everyday costs like groceries, petrol, mortgage, rent, and school fees.

Successful applicants will be required to undergo national police check as a condition of employment. Preference for the successful applicant to be fully vaccinated against MMR (measles, mumps, rubella), Varicella, Pertussis, Hepatitis B and vaccinated and hold or be eligible to hold a Registration to Work with Vulnerable People (WRVP) Tasmania as a condition of employment.

Candidates please note: All roles are based in Tasmania, and we're looking for people who are ready to call this beautiful state home. No FIFO. Applications will be reviewed as submitted and we will be conducting interviews throughout the process.
