Touring EC225 SIC-Southeast Asia

Southeast Asia · Posted 2026-10-21 · 👁 … views

Air Center Helicopters (ACHI) is seeking expressions of interest for EC225 SIC Rotor Wing Pilots supporting search and rescue / casevac operations in Southeast Asia (overseas, Department of Defense). All positions are contingent upon contract award. Submit your resume now for consideration if the contract is awarded.

Roster and benefits
- 45 days on, 45 days off
- Scheduled training as required
- 401(k) match up to 6%
- Dental, health, vision and life insurance, plus long-term disability

Requirements
- 1,500 helicopter flight hours
- 100 rotor wing hours in the past 12 months
- Current FAA Class II medical certificate
- Ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Secret clearance
- Hoist or external load experience
- Night flying experience with NVGs
- Search and rescue experience in austere environments
- EC225 type certificate and two-pilot (PIC/SIC) multi-engine experience
- US citizenship

Terms
Start dates, specific terms and benefits eligibility will be confirmed after contract award.
