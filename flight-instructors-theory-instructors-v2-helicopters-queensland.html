Flight Instructors & Theory Instructors – V2 Helicopters - Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-03-23 · 👁 … views

Brisbane, QLD. Immediate Start Available. V2 Helicopters is a growing and forward-thinking rotary aviation business based in Brisbane. Due to continued expansion, we are seeking passionate and professional Flight Instructors and Theory Instructors to join our team. This is an exciting opportunity to be part of a dynamic training environment with modern aircraft, strong industry connections, and a commitment to high-quality aviation training.

About the Role: You will be responsible for delivering high-quality flight and/or theory instruction in accordance with CASA regulations, while supporting student progression and maintaining the highest standards of safety and professionalism. Both full-time and part-time positions may be considered.

Key Responsibilities: Deliver flight and/or theory instruction to students; Ensure training is conducted in accordance with CASA and company procedures; Monitor student progress and provide structured feedback; Maintain accurate training records and documentation; Uphold a strong safety culture and professional training environment.

Essential Requirements - For Flight Instructors: Commercial Pilot Licence – Helicopter (CPL(H)); Flight Instructor Rating (FIR). For Theory Instructors: Strong aviation knowledge (CPL(H) or higher preferred); Ability to deliver structured, engaging theory lessons. All applicants: Strong communication and interpersonal skills; Professional attitude and commitment to safety.

Highly Desirable: Gas Turbine Endorsement; Experience on Bell 206 and/or Airbus AS350 (H125); Previous experience in a Part 141 or Part 142 training environment.

What We Offer: Competitive remuneration (based on experience); Supportive and professional team environment; Opportunities for career progression and upskilling; Exposure to a diverse range of training and operational activities.

The Ideal Candidate: You are passionate about developing the next generation of pilots, take pride in delivering high-quality instruction, and thrive in a team-oriented environment.
