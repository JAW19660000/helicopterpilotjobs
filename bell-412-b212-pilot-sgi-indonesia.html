B412/B212 Pilot - VRLL Operations-Indonesia

Indonesia · Posted 2026-09-28 · 👁 … views

Sayap Garuda Indah (SGI), one of Indonesia's leading aviation companies, is looking for skilled Rotary Wing Pilots for precision long-line operations. If you're passionate about flying in challenging terrains and bringing vital services to remote areas, this is your chance to fly with the best.

Base: Indonesia
Join date: Immediate opening

Minimum Requirements:
- Valid CPL or ATPL licence
- Current Class 1 Medical Certificate
- Minimum 2,500 total flight hours
- Including 1,500 PIC hours
- 500 Multi-Engine hours
- 150 hours on type (B412/212)
- Minimum 200 hours External Load
- 100 hours VRLL Operations
- High Altitude & Seismic Operation Experience preferred
- No violations/accidents in the past 5 years
- Must pass SGI's Medical Examination

Submit your CV: talent.hub@sgi-aero.com
