Head of ACE (Aeromedical Crewing Excellence Training Centre)
Location: Bankstown, NSW

About the Role: At Toll Aviation, we are committed to delivering world-class aviation training that enables safe, effective and exceptional operational outcomes. We are seeking an experienced and inspirational Head of ACE (Aeromedical Crewing Excellence Training Centre) to lead our industry-leading training facility in Bankstown. Reporting to the Vice President Rotary Wing, this strategic leadership role is responsible for overseeing all training operations, facilities, compliance, commercial performance and people leadership across a diverse multidisciplinary team. This is a unique opportunity to shape the future of aviation training excellence while driving innovation, safety, customer outcomes and business growth.

A Day in the Life of a Head of ACE at Toll Group:
- Lead all aspects of the ACE Training Centre, including facilities, training operations, administration and resources
- Drive operational excellence across pilot, aircrew, medical, maintenance and simulation training programs
- Develop and inspire high-performing teams while fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration and safety
- Ensure compliance with aviation, RTO and regulatory requirements, including CASA and ASQA standards
- Identify commercial opportunities and support business growth through client engagement and third-party training services
- Build strong relationships with clients, regulators and internal stakeholders to deliver exceptional training outcomes

Qualifications:
- Tertiary qualification in Aviation or a related discipline
- Post-graduate qualifications in Aviation, Training Management or a related field highly regarded
- Significant leadership experience within aviation and training environments
- Strong experience managing people, operational assets and training functions
- Cert IV Training and Assessment, or the ability to obtain, is desirable
- Current Australian Driver Licence and ability to meet aviation security requirements

Skills and Experience:
- Extensive experience leading complex, multidisciplinary operational teams
- Strong commercial acumen with experience in business development, sales or revenue growth initiatives
- Proven capability in stakeholder management across executive, client and regulatory environments
- Demonstrated experience managing compliance, quality assurance and continuous improvement programs
- Exceptional communication, coaching, negotiation and change leadership skills
- Strong commitment to safety leadership, risk management and people development

You must be entitled to work in Australia and be prepared to undertake pre-employment checks including a criminal history check and medical.
