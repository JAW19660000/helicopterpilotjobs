Instructor/Commercial Helicopter Pilot - Christchurch

Christchurch · Posted 2025-09-10 · 👁 … views

Join Our Team - Experienced Commercial Pilot / Instructor. Garden City Helicopters Ltd is the commercial helicopter entity of the GCH Aviation Group, operating from bases in Wellington, Nelson, Kaikoura, Christchurch, and Greymouth, to support a wide range of commercial helicopter operations across New Zealand. We are looking for an experienced commercial pilot to join our team in our state of the art aeromedical and commercial Base at Grays Road, Christchurch Airport. Ideally we are looking for a pilot with: CAANZ Commercial Pilots licence; a valid and unrestricted NZ CAA Class 1 medical certificate; an Instructors Rating; ratings in Cabri, R44, EC130, AS350 desirable; a strong commercial and/or instructing background; experience with passengers and students. It would also be advantageous to have: Dangerous Good License; Long line experience; HUET; CRM flying in the wire environment. You need to be fit and healthy to meet the demands of the job and able to work as part of a small tight-knit team, have a great sense of humour and be reasonably flexible. At GCH Aviation all of our team muck in and help where required, safety in the air and on the ground is everything! Our commercial heli team work rostered shiftwork including weekends and public holidays in accordance with the Company Flight & Duty and Fatigue Management Schemes and all GCH Aviation personnel must pass NZCAA Aviation security vetting. Health insurance, free parking, discounted products and a uniform come with employment with GCH Aviation - full terms, conditions and remuneration package will be discussed and agreed dependent upon the skills and experience of the successful applicant. If this sounds like something you are interested in, please upload your cover letter and CV highlighting your current ratings, hours, skills and experience. No direct email applications will be accepted so that we can ensure we have all information about our applicants in one place.

Apply via SEEK: https://www.seek.com.au/job/87089331
