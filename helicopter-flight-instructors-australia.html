Helicopter Flight Instructors-Australia

Australia · Posted 2025-10-25 · 👁 … views

EXPRESSION OF INTEREST - HELICOPTER FLIGHT INSTRUCTORS
Auriga Aviation is more than just a service provider; we are the industry leader in the provision of specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services in Australia, operating a modern fleet of Airbus H135, H145 and H125 (AS350) helicopters. Backed by over 140 years of operational experience, we operate in multiple locations across Australia, adhering to our unwavering focus on safety and compliance.

Auriga Aviation is currently inviting expressions of interest from experienced and suitably qualified Helicopter Flight Instructors to become part of our flight operations team. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the development and delivery of high-quality flying operations within a company that champions safety, innovation, and excellence in a rapidly growing aviation landscape.

ROLE DESCRIPTION
As a Flight Instructor, you'll apply your expertise in real-world operational contexts through both simulator and line training activities. You will deliver advanced ground and flight training across a range of specialist areas, including multi-engine operations, Night VFR, Instrument Flight Rules (IFR), Night Vision, Instructor Ratings, and other endorsements.

QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
- CASA CPL/ATPL (H)
- Class 1 medical
- Instrument Rating
- NVFR
- Instrument training endorsement
- MEH training endorsement (ideally on at least one aircraft listed in EX49/22)
- CASR 61 Flight Instructor Rating (FIR)
- 2500 hours helicopter
- 1500 hours command (PIC) helicopter
- 500 hours PIC multi-engine turbine helicopter

Desirable Operational Ratings and Experience
- Grade 2 Instructor Rating
- Low Level Helicopter (LL-H)
- NVIS Rating
- NVFR training endorsement
- NVIS training endorsement
- Sling training endorsement
- Winch training endorsement
- Sling Rating

Importantly, you'll also play a key mentoring role—guiding and supporting pilots as they develop their skills, confidence, and professionalism throughout their training journey. Pilots are required to hold an ASIC and MSIC for the duration of their employment. Applicants must be able to live and work in Australia.

What Auriga Aviation Offers You
- Work in a trusted, safety-first environment with a focus on quality.
- A supportive and engaging workplace where motivated individuals can grow their aviation careers through professional training and development.
- Above-award remuneration and employment conditions designed to support a healthy balance between work and home
- Opportunities to innovate, take ownership and shape aviation training outcomes.

Ready to take off? If you're keen to progress your career and you meet the requirements above, we want to hear from you. At Auriga, we are committed to providing equal opportunities to all our employees. We believe that diversity is a strength, and we encourage all individuals to apply, regardless of their gender, race, religion, or background.
