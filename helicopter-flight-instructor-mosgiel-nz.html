Helicopter Flight Instructor-Mosgiel, Otago, NZ

Mosgiel, Otago, New Zealand · Posted 2026-09-04 · 👁 … views

About us
Helicopters Otago Ltd has been providing aviation services to the lower half of the South Island for over 30 years. Operating a modern fleet of 20 plus single and twin engine helicopters including Guimbal G2, Bell 206 and 206L, Airbus H125, H145, EC145 and BK117. Our company embraces innovative practices and technologies in all areas of our operation including agricultural work, flight training, commercial ops and EMS operations.

We are seeking a qualified flight instructor to join the organisation to take a lead role in ab-initio flight training.

Minimum Qualifications & Experience
- New Zealand CPL
- 'B'-Cat OR 'C'-Cat instructor rating
- Mountain flying experience
- MUST be able to work in New Zealand. (No sponsorship available)
- Night & night x-country rating (desirable)
- Turbine helicopter experience (desirable)

Tasks & responsibilities
- Scheduling and carrying out ab-initio flight training (PPL, CPL, type ratings, night ratings etc)
- Carry out commercial or passenger transport work if required
- Must be able to work well in a team environment.

Potential for the right candidate to progress to other roles within the company.
