HEMS Pilot (AW139)-Adelaide SA

Adelaide Airport, Adelaide SA · Posted 2026-10-23 · 👁 … views

Toll Aviation (Toll Group) has an AW139 line pilot position available on its upcoming HEMS contract in Adelaide, SA, providing aeromedical and police services under the State Police, Ambulance and Rescue Aviation Services (SPARAS) contract. Reporting to the Senior Contract Pilot, the role works on an equal time roster. Commencement is expected in March 2027, with employment under the Toll Aviation Pilot’s Enterprise Agreement. Full time.

Requirements
- Excellent crew resource management and communication skills
- CASA Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter) or ATPL(H) with the endorsements and ratings to act as pilot in command on all HEMS operations authorised by the Operator’s certificate
- 2,000 hours total flying experience
- 1,500 hours pilot in command
- 500 hours PIC turbine rotary wing
- NVFR rating with minimum 200 hours at night
- NVIS Rating Grade 1 with minimum 40 hours NVIS as PIC
- Multi-engine command instrument rating with minimum 3 renewals
- CASA Class 1 Medical
- Comprehensive night, winch and IFR experience in a multi-crew environment

Highly desirable
- AW139 type endorsement with minimum 100 hours PIC on type
- EMS/SAR experience on AW139

Also required
- Medical and police checks
- Hold or be able to obtain an Australian Aviation Security Identification Card (ASIC)
- Ability to apply for and obtain an NV1 security clearance if required
- Variations from the minimum requirements remain at the State’s discretion, case by case
