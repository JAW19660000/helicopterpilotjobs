VRLL / Utility / Fire Pilots-Newcastle, NSW

Newcastle, Newcastle, Maitland & Hunter NSW · Posted 2026-08-28 · 👁 … views

Aero Logistics is Australia's leading provider in scenic, passenger charter, and specialised aerial work aircraft. BARS Gold Standard and ISO Accredited, we pride ourselves on our core values – Safety, Value and Integrity, upholding them to all aspects of our operations, ensuring our customers receive the highest standards of service.

With a diverse fleet, including the AS350B3, Bell 407, & Bell 206/L, Aero Logistics offers exciting career opportunities for Pilots at all stages of their career. Providing specialist aircraft services, including firefighting and aerial work operations, we are the forefront of specialised aviation aerial services in Australia.

We are currently seeking applications from highly experienced and qualified VRLL / Utility / Fire Pilots with specific expertise in Firefighting and Vertical Reference Longline Airwork operations, to become a part of our Rotary Wing team based in Newcastle, NSW. Seeking a dynamic and progressive team player who thrives on challenges and excels in a fast-paced environment.

What Will I Be Doing?
Reporting directly to the Rotary Wing Head of Flying Operations & Head of Operations, you will be supporting them by:
- Conducting Firefighting and Airwork operations, including long lining
- Performing other Airwork duties as assigned
- Maintaining precise operational and flight records
- Participating in hangar maintenance and operations
- Supporting continuous improvement in rotary wing pilot operations.

Let's Hear About You
We are particularly interested to hear from you if you have?

Minimum Requirements:
- CASA CPL(H)
- CASA Class 1 Medical
- 2000+ hours PIC Helicopters
- Current Flight Review - SEH / LLR
- Aerial Application Rating - Fire H
- Sling Endorsement
- 100hrs on AS350
- 100hrs Fire experience
- 200hrs VRLL
- HUET / EBS
- Full, unrestricted working rights for Australia.

Desirable Qualifications and Experience:
- B206/B407 Rating
- Grade 2 Instructor Rating
- Ship / deck landing experience
- Manual Driver's Licence
