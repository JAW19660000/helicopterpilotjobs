Helicopter Pilot Required-New Zealand

New Zealand · Posted 2025-08-19 · 👁 … views

We're seeking a confident and personable helicopter pilot to deliver unforgettable scenic flights across South Westland's most iconic destinations - including Franz Josef Glacier, Fox Glacier, and Haast. You'll provide premium alpine experiences, blending tourism and aerial support in a dynamic, fast-paced environment. Essential Requirements: Legal Work Eligibility - Must hold New Zealand citizenship or the legal right to work in New Zealand; New Zealand CPL(H) with a current Class 1 Medical Certificate; Minimum 1500 hours rotary; Hughes 500 rating and experience; Mountain flying experience; Flexibility to work weekends and public holidays; Reliable and safety conscious with a strong work ethic. Preferred Requirements: AS350 rating; Proficiency in external load operations; Experience flying on the West Coast; Dangerous Goods Certificate - CAA and NZTA. Key Responsibilities: Conduct scenic passenger operations, including alpine landings; Conduct aerial utility tasks - long-line operations, film/charter flights, and hunters; Safely operate in diverse mountain environments and variable weather. What We Offer: Stunning landscapes - Glacier valleys and pristine wilderness; Supportive work environment - Close-knit, experienced and safety-driven team culture; Competitive salary - Based on your experience, qualifications, and flight hours. If you're ready to fly in an iconic landscape and contribute to delivering exceptional experiences, we invite you to apply.

Apply via email: malcolm.eunson@heliservices.nz
