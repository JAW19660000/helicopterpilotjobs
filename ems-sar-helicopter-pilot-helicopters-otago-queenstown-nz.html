EMS/SAR Helicopter Pilot-Helicopters Otago Queenstown NZ

Queenstown, Otago, NZ · Posted 2026-11-20 · 👁 … views

Helicopters Otago is a highly skilled and dedicated team delivering lifesaving air ambulance, search and rescue and emergency response services across the South Island, 24 hours a day, seven days a week, alongside emergency services, hospitals and search and rescue partners in some of New Zealand’s most challenging environments.

The role
An experienced EMS / SAR Helicopter Pilot to join the Queenstown-based team, flying a varied mix of emergency medical services, search and rescue, alpine rescue and specialist helicopter operations in one of New Zealand’s most spectacular and technically demanding environments. For contract requirements you must reside in close proximity to Queenstown airport. The role also requires some duty periods from the Taieri Base when required.

What’s on offer
- Competitive remuneration
- A 4-on / 4-off roster
- The opportunity to fly modern, high-performance twin-engine helicopters
- Exposure to challenging alpine, technical rescue and specialist EMS operations
- Ongoing professional development and advanced operational training
- The opportunity to make a genuine difference to communities across the South Island
- A supportive team of experienced aviation and clinical professionals

Essential skills and experience
- NZ CPL or ATPL(H)
- Minimum 2,000 hours Pilot-in-Command helicopter flight time
- Unrestricted night VFR rating
- Full NZ Class 1 medical certificate
- Twin-engine turbine helicopter experience
- Strong communication, decision-making and CRM skills

Preference given to applicants with
- H145, EC145 or BK117 type rating and experience
- Hoist operational experience
- EMS or helicopter search and rescue experience
- NVIS rating and experience
- SPIFR rating and experience
- Mountain and technical alpine flying experience
- HUET qualification

Applicants must be eligible to work in New Zealand and will be required to complete a Police Check.

How to apply
Forward your CV and cover letter to Andy Craig via the SEEK listing.
