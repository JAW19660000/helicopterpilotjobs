Helicopter Pilot & Training and Checking Pilot-Western Australia

Western Australia · Posted 2026-04-08 · 👁 … views

The Auriga Group is a safe, focused, customer centric, and dedicated team of Marine and Aviation professionals located across Australia. Auriga Group comprises of three operational divisions, which provide services across 20 locations nationally. These operating divisions are across five business entities, with a plan to grow. Auriga Aviation: As one of the key operating divisions, Auriga Aviation has earned a strong reputation as an industry leader in the delivery of specialist Helicopter Marine Pilot Transfer (HMPT) services throughout Australia. We operate a modern and well‑maintained fleet of Airbus helicopters, supporting the maritime industry with safe, efficient, and highly specialised aviation solutions. Our services support a diverse customer base and are delivered across multiple coastal and offshore locations nationwide. Safety, operational integrity, and customer satisfaction underpin every aspect of our aviation operations. Current Opportunities: Auriga Aviation is currently seeking two experienced and highly capable Helicopter Pilots to join our growing team: HMPT Captain – based in Karratha, Western Australia; Training and Checking Pilot – based on the West Coast of Australia. If you're an enthusiastic, self‑driven aviation professional with a strong focus on safety, operational excellence, and customer service, this could be the role for you. You'll thrive in a collaborative team environment while confidently working autonomously when needed - if this sounds like you, please apply. HMPT Captain – Mandatory Qualifications and experience: Australian Commercial Pilot Licence (Helicopter) - CPL(H); Minimum 2500 hours helicopter; Minimum 1500 hours PIC helicopter; Minimum 500 hours PIC Multi-Engine Helicopter; MEH Instrument Rating; Current Class 1 Australian Aviation Medical Certificate. HMPT Captain – Desirable Qualifications and experience: 100 hours PIC EC135; NVIS Rating; Previous maritime experience; Previous multi-crew experience. Training and Checking Pilot – Mandatory Qualifications and experience: Minimum flight experience as per HMPT Captain; CASA Part 61 Grade 2 Flight Instructor Rating. Training and Checking Pilot – Desirable Qualifications and experience: 100 hours PIC EC135; Previous maritime experience; Training Endorsements for NVIS, IF and Low Level.

Apply through SEEK: https://www.seek.com.au/job/91379662
