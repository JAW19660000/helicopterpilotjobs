Flight Examiner-Queensland

Queensland · Posted 2026-02-20 · 👁 … views

About LifeFlight: LifeFlight Australia is a leading aeromedical organisation dedicated to saving lives through rapid response medical services. Operating a fleet of helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, LifeFlight delivers critical care and emergency retrievals across Queensland and beyond. In addition to its lifesaving missions, LifeFlight runs a world-class training academy. About the Role: As a Flight Examiner, you will play a critical role in maintaining and advancing the safety, capability and professionalism of LifeFlight's flight operations. Working in accordance with CASA CASR Parts 61, 119, 133, 138 and supporting Part 142 training delivery, you will conduct pilot checking, flight testing and training across both aircraft and simulator environments. This role combines instructional excellence with operational credibility. You will deliver high-quality training and assessment, support EMS line flying, and ensure pilots meet all regulatory, operational and organisational standards. Based primarily at our Training Academy in Brisbane, with travel to LifeFlight bases as required. Key responsibilities include: Conducting flight tests, proficiency checks and flight reviews in line with CASA requirements and company standards; Delivering simulator-based and in-aircraft training and checking to approved training systems; Undertaking EMS line flying to support operational readiness and maintain operational proficiency; Mentoring and coaching pilots through constructive feedback and performance development; Contributing to the ongoing development and continuous improvement of training systems, procedures and materials. This is a full-time role, operating on an equal time roster reconciled monthly, with a company vehicle provided to support travel. About You: You will bring: ATPL (H); Flight Instructor Rating (FIR) with NVIS and Instrument Rating training endorsements; Flight Examiner Rating (FER) with NVIS and Instrument Rating examiner endorsements; Current CASA Class 1 Medical Certificate; Extensive EMS, SAR or multi-engine operational experience; Demonstrated experience delivering flight training, checking and assessment in accordance with CASA regulations; High levels of situational awareness, decision-making and professional judgement; Current ASIC. Desirable: Additional examiner approvals, including multiple aircraft type examiner ratings including multi-crew cooperation instructor experience and qualifications; Experience supporting simulator fidelity evaluation or training device improvement initiatives. Note: the successful applicant will be required to undergo background checks including a national police check. This position is only open to Australian Citizens, Permanent Residents, or those with unrestricted work rights in Australia. Eligible candidates will need to be fully vaccinated against MMR, Varicella, Pertussis, Hepatitis B and will be required to hold or obtain a Working with Children Blue Card (Queensland). Applications close on 5 March 2026.

Apply via LifeFlight careers page: https://www.lifeflight.org.au/careers/aviation-careers/?ja-job=829889
