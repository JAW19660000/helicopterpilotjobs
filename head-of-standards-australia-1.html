Head of Standards - Australia
West Perth | Flight Operations | Permanent Full Time

The company
PHI is a global leader in helicopter services. In the Asia Pacific (APAC) region, PHI takes on some of the most challenging assignments in aviation to deliver high quality helicopter support for energy customers and search and rescue authorities. We are one of the safest helicopter operators anywhere in the world, as every flight we take is guided by our core values of Safe, Efficient, Quality, Service.

About the role
Are you an aviation professional with a passion for safety, operational excellence, and leadership? PHI is seeking a dynamic and safety-focused individual to join our team as Head of Standards – Australia. This is a unique leadership role that combines strategic oversight with operational flying. You'll help shape flight standards, drive compliance, and influence best practices across our Australian operations, all while maintaining flying currency and staying connected to flying.

The role offers a balanced lifestyle with time at home and opportunities for travel, making it ideal for professionals looking for a rewarding secondment to suit their current life stage, starting their aviation leadership journey or looking to transition from full-time flying into a strategic leadership role.

Responsibilities of the role include:
- Lead standardisation and continuous improvement initiatives across flight operations.
- Maintain flying currency on an aircraft type operated by the company, performing operational/training flying duties as required.
- Ensure regulatory compliance and foster strong relationships with CASA.
- Oversee safety and quality programs, including FDM, LOSA, and internal audits.
- Manage operational documentation and ensure alignment with industry standards.
- Support strategic development of best practices in flight operations.
- Attend CASA seminars and monitor regulatory changes (CASR).
- Represent PHI in industry forums and contribute to global aviation standards.
- Administer Web Manuals and ensure documentation accuracy.

To be considered for this role, you will need:
- CASA Part 61 CPL(H) or ATPL(H) with Instrument Rating (co-pilots may apply).
- Experience in Offshore or Marine Pilot Transfer operations.
- Type rating on one or more of the following preferred: H175, AW189, S92.
- Strong knowledge of the CASA regulatory environment.
- Excellent writing skills.

About you:
- Collaborative and solutions-focused, with a continuous improvement mindset.
- Embraces change and adapts to new technologies and processes.
- Communicates clearly and respectfully across all levels.
- Sets measurable performance standards and leads by example.
- Balances strategic priorities with operational demands.
- Strong administrative and organisational skills.

Culture & Benefits
- Competitive base salary aligned with your piloting experience.
- Ongoing professional development, mentoring, and career growth opportunities.
- A values-led culture where safety is always the top priority.
- Salary sacrifice options including novated leasing and superannuation.
- A supportive and professional work environment with a diverse team.

Ready to take the next step in your aviation leadership career? Apply now via the PHI Career Centre and complete your application.
