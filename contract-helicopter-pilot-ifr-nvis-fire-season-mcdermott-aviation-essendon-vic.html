Contract Helicopter Pilot (IFR & NVIS, Fire Season)-McDermott Aviation Essendon VIC

Essendon, Melbourne VIC · Posted 2026-11-23 · 👁 … views

McDermott Aviation, Australia’s largest privately owned helicopter company, is seeking experienced CASA IFR and NVIS-qualified helicopter pilots for touring contract positions during the upcoming Australian fire season. With more than 40 years of experience in aerial firefighting, heavy lifting and emergency operations, McDermott Aviation operates a diverse fleet of more than 50 aircraft. This role is specifically for aerial firefighting operations under NVIS.

The role
Working on a touring basis, you will be responsible for:
- Conducting aerial firefighting operations using NVIS
- Operating medium Bell 214 helicopters in demanding fire environments
- Monitoring weather, visibility, terrain and changing fire conditions
- Completing required pre-flight and post-flight inspections and checks
- Maintaining compliance with CASA and company operational requirements
- Working closely with pilots, engineers, ground crews and operational teams
- Maintaining a strong focus on safety, situational awareness and operational readiness

Mandatory requirements
- CASA CPL(H)
- Current CASA Instrument Rating (Helicopter) / IFR
- Current NVIS qualification with recent operational experience
- Minimum 1,500 hours total helicopter flight time
- Minimum 500 hours turbine experience
- Minimum 100 hours on medium Bell helicopters, such as Bell 205, 212, 214 or 412
- Current aviation medical certificate
- Valid passport
- Ability to successfully complete pre-employment DAMP requirements

Only applicants who hold current CASA IFR and NVIS qualifications and meet the required medium Bell experience will be considered.

Highly regarded experience
- Aerial firefighting
- Search and Rescue (SAR)
- Emergency Medical Services (EMS)
- Previous Australian fire-season operations
- Utility or other complex, high-tempo helicopter operations

Why join McDermott Aviation?
- Work with one of Australia’s leading helicopter operators
- Operate in challenging and specialised aviation missions
- Join a highly experienced team of pilots, engineers and operational specialists
- Work across demanding fire and emergency-response operations
- Utilise your IFR and NVIS capability in operational environments
- Be part of a business with more than 40 years of helicopter operations experience

Apply via the SEEK listing. Due to the high volume of applications, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
