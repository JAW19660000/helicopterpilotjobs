Helicopter Pilots (Captains/PIC)-Papua New Guinea

Papua New Guinea · Posted 2026-10-29 · 👁 … views

Heli Niugini Limited is seeking experienced helicopter pilots (Captains / PIC) to join its professional team delivering safe, reliable and essential aviation services across Papua New Guinea. Opportunities are available across multiple aircraft types: Airbus H145 D2 / BK117 D-2, BK117, Bell 407 and Bell 206. PNG licence holders are strongly preferred.

Licence requirements
- CPL(H) or ATPL(H)
- Current Class 1 medical (or equivalent)
- Aircraft type rating / endorsement
- Sling / external-load qualification
- Vertical Reference Long Line (VRLL) experience
- Current CRM / Human Factors and recurrent training
- Licence acceptable for validation or conversion under CASA PNG
- Preference will be given to PNG licence holders

Operational experience
- PNG, mountainous and remote-area operations
- Utility, external-load and VRLL operations
- High-density-altitude environments
- Confined-area, pinnacle and ridgeline operations
- Resource-sector and mining aviation experience
- Previous Papua New Guinea operating experience highly desirable

Minimum experience (BARS pilot requirements)
- H145 D2 / BK117: 2,500 hours total helicopter time, 1,500 hours PIC, 500 hours PIC multi-engine, 100 hours PIC on type
- Bell 407 / Bell 206: 2,000 hours total helicopter time, 1,500 hours PIC, 100 hours PIC on type

External load / VRLL
- Minimum 200 hours external-load / sling operations
- Minimum 100 hours VRLL / long-line experience
- Demonstrated VRLL / long-line experience
- Competency in confined, mountainous and remote operations
- Ability to complete HNL sling, VRLL and aircraft competency checks

How to apply
Email hrd.manager@heliniugini.com with the subject “Helicopter Pilot Application (state aircraft type)”. Include your CV, licence, medical, type ratings, hours summary, relevant training and availability.

Apply: hrd.manager@heliniugini.com (subject: Helicopter Pilot Application, state aircraft type)
