AS332 Touring Captains-PNG

PNG · Posted 2026-04-30 · 👁 … views

AS332L2 Captain – Contract & Full-Time Touring Roles. Hevilift is seeking experienced AS332L2 Captains to support ongoing operations across Papua New Guinea and offshore environments. Opportunities are available on both a short-term contract and full-time touring basis. Minimum Requirements (All Applicants): Minimum 3000 hours Total Time; Minimum 500 hours Multi-Engine; Minimum 100 hours on type (AS332 or variant); Multi-crew experience; Below 65 years of age; Strong written and verbal English communication skills; Demonstrated strong safety record, with no accidents in the past three years and a commitment to operating within a high safety culture. Contract Roles (Offshore OGP) Essential: AS332L2 (or similar) command experience; ATPL (H); IFR rated and current; Minimum 500 hours offshore IFR experience; Minimum 1200 hours multi-engine command. Full-Time Touring Roles (PNG Operations) Requirements: AS332L2 (or similar) command experience; Proven operational background in VRLL (medium/heavy category desirable), High-density altitude environments, Remote and hostile operations. Highly Regarded: ATPL (H); Training Captain experience (TRI/TRE or equivalent); Aerial firefighting experience; IFR capability (not essential for full-time roles). Additional Information: Candidates endorsed on SA330 or AS332L1 are encouraged to apply; AS332L2 type conversion training provided for suitable candidates; Visa sponsorship available for successful applicants outside Papua New Guinea. Only short-listed candidates will be contacted for interview.

Apply through SEEK: https://www.seek.com.au/job/91824763 or via https://external-jobboard.myrecruitmentplus.com/job-details/query/9735250/
