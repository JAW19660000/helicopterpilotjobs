B407 - VRLL Captains-Western Highlands Province, PNG

Western Highlands Province, Papua New Guinea · Posted 2026-09-27 · 👁 … views

HEVILIFT Aviation is a leading aviation charter operator in Papua New Guinea and is seeking expressions of interest from experienced B407 VRLL pilots to join our operations.

Minimum requirements:
- 3,000 hours total helicopter flight time
- 100 hours on B407
- 200 hours VRLL experience
- ICAO English Level 6
- Accident-free record for the past 3 years
- Under 65 years of age

Preference will be given to candidates who already hold a CASA PNG Flight Crew Licence.

Hevilift offers:
- Equal-time touring
- Visa sponsorship
- Flights from home base to PNG and return
- Accommodation and meals while on tour

Interested applicants should submit an updated CV/resume and copies of relevant certificates by COB 10 October 2026. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview.

Closes 24th October 2026.

Apply via HEVILIFT careers site (closes 24 Oct 2026): https://hevilift.com/pngjobs/
