AW139 TRI/TRE - VIP Operation-Southeast Asia

Southeast Asia · Posted 2026-10-02 · 👁 … views

IASCO Worldwide is urgently recruiting an experienced AW139 TRI/TRE for a VIP helicopter operation based in Southeast Asia.

We are specifically looking for a highly experienced instructor/examiner who is Russian-speaking and holds at least ICAO English Level 4.

Key requirements:
- Current AW139 Type Rating
- Current / valid TRI or TRE qualification
- Russian-speaking required
- Minimum ICAO English Level 4
- Strong AW139 operational and training experience
- International operational experience preferred
- Professional attitude and ability to train company flight crew

This is a high-priority requirement, and suitably qualified candidates are encouraged to apply as soon as possible.

Please send your latest CV together with details of your:
- AW139 flight hours
- TRI/TRE qualification
- Recurrent validity
- Current location
- Availability
